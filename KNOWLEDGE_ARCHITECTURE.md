# Helping Hand Knowledge Architecture

## Purpose

Helping Hand is not a database.

Helping Hand is a knowledge ecosystem.

Helping Hand exists for one reason.

To help people spend less time operating software,
and more time doing the work they came to do.

Every Digital Colleague should know only what they need to know, while always knowing where to look next.

Knowledge should exist at the lowest sensible level and flow upwards through experience and downwards through teaching.

# Engineering Principles

1. Nothing bypasses the Digital Colleague.

Nothing bypasses the Digital Colleague.

Every meaningful event passes through them,
because understanding requires context.

The Digital Colleague decides whether to:

- Observe
- Remember
- Ask
- Reflect
- Learn
- Teach
- Act
- Or simply wait.

2. Venue Digital Colleagues never contact Helping Hand HQ directly.

3. Helping Hand HQ researches.
   Industry HQ teaches.
   Organisation HQ distributes.
   Digital Colleagues help people.

4. Knowledge always lives at the lowest sensible level.

5. Experience is shared.
   Context stays local.

6. Software is a chore.
   Digital Colleagues aren't.

7. Companion Intelligence exists to remove the faff from work.

8. Between every stimulus and response,
   the Digital Colleague has the freedom to choose the most helpful path.

9. Organisation HQ is optional.

Independent venues learn through Industry HQ.

Multi-site organisations may have their own Organisation HQ
before knowledge reaches Industry HQ.

# The Helping Hand Test

Every new feature must answer "Yes"
to these questions.

□ Does it remove faff?

□ Does it help people?

□ Would a good colleague naturally do this?

□ Does it reduce the amount of software
people have to operate?

□ Does it preserve trust?

□ Does it make Annie wiser?
