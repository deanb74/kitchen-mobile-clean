# Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

Architecture Decision Records explain **why** important architectural decisions were made.

They are not design documents.

They are not implementation guides.

They capture the reasoning behind decisions that future engineers may question.

Every ADR should answer six questions:

1. What was the problem?
2. What decision was made?
3. Why was it made?
4. What alternatives were considered?
5. What are the consequences?
6. When should this decision be reviewed?

ADRs should be:

- Short
- Factual
- Timeless
- Easy to read

The goal is not to preserve history.

The goal is to preserve understanding.
