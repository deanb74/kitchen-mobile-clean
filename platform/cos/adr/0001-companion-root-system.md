# ADR-0001

## Title

Companion Root System

---

## Status

Accepted

9 July 2026

---

## Context

Helping Hand requires behaviours that are common to every Digital Colleague regardless of profession.

Without a shared behavioural foundation, each Digital Colleague would evolve independently, resulting in duplicated logic, inconsistent behaviour and increasing maintenance.

---

## Decision

Create a shared behavioural foundation called the Companion Root System.

The Companion Root System becomes part of the Companion Operating System (COS).

All Digital Colleagues inherit behaviour from this shared foundation.

---

## Principles

The Companion Root System provides:

- Understanding
- Reflection
- Decision
- Intent
- Communication

Future behavioural roots will be added only when they benefit every Digital Colleague.

---

## Consequences

Positive:

- Consistent behaviour.
- Shared engineering effort.
- Easier expansion into new professions.
- Clear architectural boundaries.

Trade-offs:

- Behavioural changes affect every Digital Colleague.
- The Companion Root System must remain stable and carefully governed.
