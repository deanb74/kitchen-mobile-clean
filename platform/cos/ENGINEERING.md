# COS Engineering Principles

## Principle 1

Behaviour before knowledge.

---

## Principle 2

Seek first to understand.

Always.

---

## Principle 3

The architecture leads.

Implementation follows.

---

## Principle 4

Every capability belongs in the highest reusable layer.

---

## Principle 5

Simple systems grow into great systems.

Complicated systems rarely do.

---

## Principle 6

Every commit should leave COS healthier than it was found.

---

## Principle 7

Technology serves people.

People never serve technology.
