# COS Roadmap

## Vision

The Companion Operating System (COS) exists to provide the shared behavioural foundation for every Helping Hand Digital Colleague.

Each new capability must strengthen the platform rather than increase its complexity.

Growth should be deliberate.

Growth should be sustainable.

Growth should always serve people.

---

# Phase 1 – Behaviour

## Status: ✅ Established

The first behavioural foundation of COS.

### Companion Root System

- ✅ Understanding
- ✅ Reflection
- ✅ Decision
- ✅ Intent
- ✅ Communication

This phase established how every Digital Colleague should behave before responding.
