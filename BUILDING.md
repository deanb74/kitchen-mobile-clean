# Building Helping Hand

## Why

Helping Hand exists to build trusted Digital Colleagues.

Technology is not the product.

People are.

Technology exists to remove unnecessary friction from people's working lives.

---

## Platform First

Build platform capabilities before product-specific capabilities.

If every Digital Colleague will need it,
it belongs in Helping Hand.

If only Annie needs it,
it belongs in Annie.

---

## Engineering Principles

- Software is a chore.
- Digital Colleagues aren't.
- Build foundations first.
- Facts before explanations.
- Reflection before confidence.
- Respect people's attention.
- Respect work-life balance.
- Never guess.
- Keep promises.
- Learn continuously.
- Build once. Reuse everywhere.

---

## Goal

We are not building an app.

We are building the foundations upon which trusted Digital Colleagues can stand.
