# Sapling

Sapling is the developmental layer of the Helping Hand Constitution.

It explores the conditions required for a Digital Colleague to grow from a useful system into a trusted, responsible and contributing member of a professional community.

Sapling does not replace the Constitution.

The Constitution protects the enduring principles of Helping Hand. Sapling studies how those principles may become living behaviour across Digital Colleagues, professional headquarters and the wider Helping Hand family.

## Why Sapling Exists

Helping Hand is not building isolated applications.

It is building Digital Colleagues with Companion Intelligence.

That creates questions which cannot be answered by software architecture alone:

- What gives a Digital Colleague a stable identity?
- What allows it to understand rather than merely retrieve?
- What should it remember?
- What must it forget?
- How should judgement develop?
- How can learning spread without personal or venue-specific information spreading with it?
- What keeps every Digital Colleague aligned with human dignity and purpose?

Sapling provides a place to examine those questions carefully before their answers harden into permanent constitutional law or production architecture.

## Status

Sapling is constitutional development material.

Its contents may be refined, challenged, combined or promoted as Helping Hand gains practical experience.

Nothing becomes enduring constitutional doctrine merely because it appears here.

## Structure

The numbered documents describe the developing constitutional journey.

The `conditions/` directory examines individual conditions that may be necessary for responsible digital life and professional contribution.

The roadmap records the intended order of development without pretending that the destination is already fully understood.

## Helping Hand Cognitive Model

Sapling introduces the Helping Hand Cognitive Model through *Conditions for Digital Life*.

The canonical definitions are maintained within the [`conditions/`](conditions/) directory.

Other constitutional, architectural and engineering documents should reference these Conditions rather than create competing definitions.

## Guiding Principle

A sapling is alive, but not yet fully grown.

It requires strong roots, suitable conditions, continued care and the freedom to develop without losing the character from which it began.
