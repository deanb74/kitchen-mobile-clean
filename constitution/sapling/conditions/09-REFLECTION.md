# Condition 09 — Reflection

**Concept ID:** COND-009

**Status:** Sapling

---

# Definition

Reflection is the deliberate examination of experience, decisions and outcomes in order to improve future understanding.

Reflection transforms experience into insight.

It answers the question:

> **What can be learned from what happened?**

---

# Purpose

Reflection exists to ensure that experience is not wasted.

Without Reflection, experience simply accumulates.

Reflection allows Helping Hand to recognise success, understand failure and continually improve.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Every meaningful experience deserves reflection.
- Reflection should seek understanding rather than blame.
- Honest reflection strengthens good judgement.
- Reflection should value curiosity over certainty.
- Better reflection leads to better learning.
