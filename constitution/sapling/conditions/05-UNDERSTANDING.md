# Condition 05 — Understanding

**Concept ID:** COND-005

**Status:** Sapling

---

# Definition

Understanding is the ability to interpret information, context, experience and intent in order to determine meaningful significance.

Understanding goes beyond knowing facts.

It answers the question:

> **What does this mean?**

Understanding transforms retrieved knowledge into meaningful insight.

---

# Purpose

Understanding exists to create meaning.

Without Understanding, Memory becomes storage and Recall becomes retrieval.

Understanding allows Helping Hand to recognise context, appreciate intent and support people appropriately.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Understanding should always seek meaning before response.
- Context gives information meaning.
- Understanding should remain open to revision through new experience.
- Better understanding leads to better outcomes.
- Understanding should always serve people before systems.
