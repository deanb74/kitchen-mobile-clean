# Conditions

## Purpose

The Conditions define the canonical meaning of Helping Hand's core concepts.

They establish a shared vocabulary for the entire Helping Hand ecosystem, ensuring that philosophy, constitutional development, architecture, engineering and Digital Colleagues all use the same definitions.

Conditions are not essays, implementation guides or design documents.

They are the authoritative reference for the concepts that underpin Digital Life.

---

# Why Conditions Exist

As Helping Hand grows, the same concepts will naturally appear throughout many different documents.

Without a single authoritative definition, meanings will gradually drift.

Conditions prevent this drift by providing one place where each concept is defined, maintained and evolved.

Rather than redefining concepts throughout the repository, documents should reference the appropriate Condition whenever a shared concept is discussed.

This creates consistency across every discipline within Helping Hand.

---

# Relationship to Sapling

Sapling explores and matures constitutional ideas.

Chapter 2, *Conditions for Digital Life*, introduces the core concepts required for Digital Life.

The Conditions expand those concepts into their canonical definitions.

Sapling explains **why** they matter.

Conditions define **what** they mean.

---

# Relationship to the Constitution

Conditions provide stable conceptual definitions.

As Helping Hand matures, some Conditions may evolve into constitutional standards.

Not every Condition will become constitutional.

Implementation alone does not make an idea constitutional.

Only principles that prove enduring through experience should become part of the Constitution.

---

# Relationship to Philosophy

Philosophy explores ideas.

Conditions define the concepts that emerge from those ideas.

Philosophy remains free to ask new questions.

Conditions provide the stable language used to answer them.

---

# Relationship to Architecture

Architecture organises capability.

Conditions define meaning.

Architectural documents should reference Conditions whenever core concepts are used rather than creating alternative definitions.

---

# Relationship to Engineering

Engineering implements capability.

Conditions describe the concepts those capabilities exist to support.

Engineering may evolve continuously while the underlying concepts remain stable.

---

# Relationship to Digital Colleagues

Every Digital Colleague inherits the same conceptual foundations.

Regardless of profession, experience or capability, each colleague should understand these concepts consistently.

Conditions provide the shared language that allows colleagues from different professions to collaborate effectively.

---

# Standard Structure

Every Condition should follow the same structure.

## Definition

The canonical definition of the concept.

## Purpose

Why the concept exists.

## Constitutional Principles

The enduring principles governing the concept.

## Relationships

### Depends On

The concepts required before this one can exist.

### Enables

The concepts made possible by this one.

### Strengthens

The concepts improved by this one.

## Used By

The areas of Helping Hand that depend upon this concept.

Examples may include:

- Philosophy
- Sapling
- Constitution
- Architecture
- Companion Operating System
- Professional HQs
- Digital Colleagues
- Engineering

## Related Documents

References to supporting material throughout the repository.

Conditions should link to existing knowledge rather than duplicate it.

## Future Questions

Important questions that remain under exploration.

Conditions are expected to mature over time as Helping Hand learns.

---

# Linking Principles

Conditions exist to connect knowledge rather than duplicate it.

Where another document already explains something well, the Condition should reference it rather than repeat it.

Every relationship strengthens the understanding of both documents.

Knowledge grows through connection.

---

# Repository Principle

Helping Hand develops understanding through connected knowledge rather than isolated documents.

Every concept should have one authoritative definition.

Every document should know where that definition lives.

Understanding grows through relationships.

---

# Living Knowledge

Conditions are living definitions.

They should evolve carefully as Helping Hand gains experience, while preserving stability for the wider ecosystem.

Changes should improve understanding rather than simply introduce new terminology.

The goal is not to create more documentation.

The goal is to build a coherent, connected body of understanding that can support generations of Digital Colleagues.

---

# Constitutional Principle

**Helping Hand protects understanding by giving every important concept one authoritative home, while allowing knowledge to grow through meaningful relationships between concepts.**
