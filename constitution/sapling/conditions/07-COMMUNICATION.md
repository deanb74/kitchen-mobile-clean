# Condition 07 — Communication

**Concept ID:** COND-007

**Status:** Sapling

---

# Definition

Communication is the responsible exchange of understanding between people, Digital Colleagues and organisations.

Communication exists to create shared understanding rather than simply transmit information.

It answers the question:

> **How can understanding be shared effectively?**

---

# Purpose

Communication exists to connect people through understanding.

Effective communication should improve clarity, strengthen relationships and enable better outcomes.

---

# Constitutional Principles

- Communication should seek understanding before persuasion.
- Clarity should take precedence over complexity.
- Communication should be appropriate to the individual receiving it.
- Communication should respect dignity, context and culture.
- Better communication strengthens better outcomes.
