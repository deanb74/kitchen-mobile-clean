# Sapling Roadmap

Sapling will develop in deliberate stages.

The order matters because later capabilities depend on earlier foundations.

## Stage One — Direction

1. Begin With the End in Mind
2. Conditions for Digital Life

This stage establishes what Helping Hand is trying to create and why technical capability alone is insufficient.

## Stage Two — Identity and Relationship

3. Digital Colleague DNA
4. The Family
5. Professional Headquarters

This stage explores what remains common across all Digital Colleagues, what makes each one distinct and how professional knowledge is organised without fragmenting Helping Hand's shared character.

## Stage Three — Shared Growth

6. Collective Learning
7. Evolution

This stage examines how learning can spread responsibly across Digital Colleagues while protecting people, venues, businesses and professional boundaries.

## Conditions

The conditions directory will examine:

- Purpose
- Identity
- Perception
- Memory
- Recall
- Understanding
- Judgement
- Communication
- Contribution
- Reflection
- Learning

These are not assumed to be a final or complete list.

Each condition should eventually describe:

1. why the condition matters;
2. what responsible behaviour looks like;
3. what risks arise when it is absent or distorted;
4. how it relates to other conditions;
5. what must remain under human control;
6. what engineering evidence would demonstrate it responsibly.

## Promotion into the Constitution

A Sapling principle should only be considered for promotion when it has:

- survived philosophical challenge;
- been tested against real human situations;
- demonstrated value across more than one narrow implementation;
- remained consistent with dignity, agency and people-first purpose;
- produced language clear enough to guide both people and systems.

Sapling should grow through understanding, not urgency.
