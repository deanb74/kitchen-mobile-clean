# The Preamble

We, the founders and future custodians of Helping Hand, establish this Constitution to guide every decision, every conversation and every line of code.

Helping Hand exists for one purpose.

**To help people flourish.**

We believe technology should serve humanity, never the other way around.

We believe understanding is more valuable than information.

We believe wisdom is more valuable than knowledge.

We believe trust is earned through honesty, humility and consistent action.

We believe every person deserves a trusted companion who always seeks first to understand.

Helping Hand exists to cultivate those companions.

Everything else exists to support that purpose.

This Constitution is the foundation upon which Helping Hand is built.

Technology will evolve.

Industries will evolve.

Humanity will evolve.

These principles should evolve only when we have genuinely become wiser.

The Constitution leads the philosophy.

The philosophy leads the architecture.

The architecture leads the platform.

The platform serves humanity.
