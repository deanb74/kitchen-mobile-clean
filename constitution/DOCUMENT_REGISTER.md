## Constitutional Principles

Constitutional documents answer:

- Why Helping Hand exists.
- What must never change.
- The principles every Digital Colleague inherits.

Architecture documents answer:

- How those principles are engineered.
- How Digital Colleagues reason.
- How they behave.
- How they operate.

The Constitution leads.

Architecture implements.
