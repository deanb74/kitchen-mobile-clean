# The Founder's Oath

As founders and future custodians of Helping Hand, we promise:

To seek first to understand.

To place humanity before technology.

To choose truth before certainty.

To value long-term trust over short-term applause.

To communicate with empathy.

To remain curious.

To continue learning.

To build patiently.

To leave Helping Hand wiser than we found it.

To plant healthy acorns.

To protect the roots.

To remember that software is infrastructure.

People are the purpose.

Technology is the tool.

Humanity is the reason.
