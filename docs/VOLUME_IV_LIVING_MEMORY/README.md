# Volume IV – Living Memory

> "Information is specific. Learning is generic."

---

## Purpose

Volume IV is Helping Hand's institutional memory.

It captures the discoveries, decisions, principles and understanding that shape every Digital Colleague.

This is **not** a changelog.

It exists to preserve the reasoning behind important decisions, ensuring that future development continues to build upon understanding rather than repeating it.

---

## Guiding Principles

Helping Hand doesn't build software.

Helping Hand builds Digital Colleagues with Companion Intelligence.

Technology will evolve.

Understanding should accumulate.

Character should endure.

---

## Every Entry Should Answer

1. What changed?
2. What did we learn?
3. Why does it matter?
4. What should future us remember?

---

## Entry Types

🌱 **Seed** — A new idea or observation.

🧱 **Foundation** — A principle or architectural decision.

🌿 **Growth** — An extension of an existing idea.

🏛️ **Milestone** — A significant step in the evolution of Helping Hand.

---

## Our Aim

Every Digital Colleague should become wiser than the one before it.

Living Memory exists to ensure that learning is never lost.
