# 🌱 002 – Recall: Turning Memory into Understanding

**Date:** 21 July 2026

**Status:** Foundation

---

## Summary

Today Helping Hand formally distinguished **Memory** from **Recall**.

Memory preserves understanding.

Recall applies understanding.

This distinction establishes Recall as a universal Companion Intelligence capability rather than a search function.

---

## What Changed

A new Recall capability was introduced within:

```
lib/os/recall/
```

The initial implementation deliberately remains simple.

Its purpose is to establish clear architectural responsibilities before implementing complex behaviour.

The current contracts define:

- RecallRequest
- RecallResult
- RecallSource
- recall()

---

## Major Discovery

Memory answers:

> What do I know?

Recall answers:

> What knowledge matters here?

A Digital Colleague should never retrieve information simply because it exists.

Recall begins with understanding the current situation before deciding what previous understanding may help.

## Four Sources of Recall

### Venue Intelligence

Understanding the workplace.

Examples include:

- equipment
- layout
- workflow
- environmental conditions

---

### Business Intelligence

Understanding how the organisation operates.

Examples include:

- procedures
- compliance
- suppliers
- operational practice

---

### People Intelligence

Understanding developed through trusted working relationships.

Examples include:

- experience
- communication preferences
- responsibilities
- strengths

People Intelligence must always respect dignity, privacy and purpose.

---

### Institutional Intelligence

Understanding inherited from Helping Hand itself.

Examples include:

- philosophy
- governance
- architecture
- professional learning
- validated organisational experience

Institutional Intelligence enables every new Digital Colleague to begin wiser than the previous one.

---

## Relationship to Living Memory

Living Memory preserves important understanding.

Recall brings that understanding into the present when it can improve an outcome.

Without Living Memory, Helping Hand forgets.

Without Recall, Helping Hand cannot benefit from what it remembers.

---

## Engineering Principle

The Recall implementation should remain intentionally simple while its responsibilities mature.

The architecture should evolve before the algorithms.

Understanding should evolve before optimisation.

---

## What Future Us Should Remember

Do not build Recall as search.

Do not build Recall as memory retrieval.

Build Recall as contextual understanding.

Search may support Recall.

Memory enables Recall.

Understanding gives Recall purpose.

---

## Closing Thought

> Memory preserves yesterday.

> Recall helps yesterday improve today.

> Companion Intelligence decides what should happen next.
