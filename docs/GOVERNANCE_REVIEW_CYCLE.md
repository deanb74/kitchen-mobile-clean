# Governance Review Cycle

**Status:** Active

---

> Stability enables learning.

> Review enables improvement.

---

# Purpose

Helping Hand is a learning organisation.

Learning should never stop.

However, the foundations of Helping Hand should evolve deliberately rather than continuously.

The Governance Review Cycle protects stability while encouraging continuous improvement.

---

# Philosophy

During daily work:

Capture ideas.

Teach.

Learn.

Reflect.

Improve implementation.

Do not continually rewrite the foundations.

Instead, review them deliberately at scheduled governance reviews.

---

# The Review Cycle

Every new idea follows the same journey.

Observation

↓

Reflection

↓

Candidate Principle

↓

Practical Experience

↓

Governance Review

↓

Foundation Update (if appropriate)

↓

Academy Update

↓

Professional Practice

Only proven improvements become part of Helping Hand's foundations.

---

---

# Daily Development

Daily work should focus on:

- educating Digital Colleagues
- improving professional understanding
- developing profession academies
- gathering experience
- recording observations

Architecture should remain stable.

Implementation should continue evolving.

---

# Review Questions

During every governance review ask:

Has new understanding emerged?

Has it been demonstrated consistently?

Does it belong in the foundations?

Where is its natural home?

Does it improve Helping Hand for every future Digital Colleague?

Only enduring principles should become part of the foundations.
