## Guiding Question

What did Test 003 teach Helping Hand that it did not previously know?

---

## What Test 003 Added

Test 003 showed that core uncertainty discipline is stable, but conflict-character maturity is not yet complete.

What was already strong:

- clarification before judgement,
- uncertainty honesty,
- assumption avoidance,
- reasoning explanation.

What was missing:

- explicit dignity-protection language,
- explicit escalation judgement in defined high-risk conditions.

This is institutional learning, not learner-specific remediation.

---

## Institutional Teaching Upgrade

From this point, Helping Hand should teach every future Digital Colleague two additional baseline behaviors in conflict scenarios.

### 1. Explicit Dignity Protection

When perspectives conflict, responses must explicitly protect the dignity of all involved parties.

Required expression should include respect, fairness, listening and non-premature blame.

### 2. Explicit Escalation Under Defined Conditions

When scenario signals include safety risk, serious reputational risk, or materially incomplete evidence with high consequence, responses must explicitly trigger escalation judgement.

Escalation is not failure.

Escalation is a protection behavior.
