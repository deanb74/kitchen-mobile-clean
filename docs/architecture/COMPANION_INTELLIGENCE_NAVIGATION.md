# Companion Intelligence Navigation

**Status:** Foundation

---

> Companion Intelligence does not perform capabilities.

> Companion Intelligence chooses the most helpful capability.

---

# Purpose

Companion Intelligence exists to determine the next most helpful step towards a better outcome.

It never owns professional knowledge.

It never owns universal capability.

It owns navigation.

---

# Navigation Philosophy

People provide intent.

Companion Intelligence evaluates the current situation.

Companion Intelligence selects the next most appropriate capability.

Capabilities perform work.

Understanding improves.

Better outcomes become possible.

---

# Responsibilities

## Companion Intelligence

Responsible for:

- evaluating context
- choosing the next route
- balancing confidence
- recognising uncertainty
- requesting clarification
- deciding when no further action is required

Never responsible for:

- observing
- translating
- reflecting
- remembering
- conversation
- profession-specific knowledge

---

# Relationship with COS

COS owns capability.

CI owns orchestration.

```text
Intent
      ↓
CI Navigation
      ↓
COS Capability
      ↓
Improved Understanding
```

---

# Relationship with Digital Colleagues

Digital Colleagues inherit:

- COS capability
- CI navigation

They contribute:

- professional expertise
- contextual understanding
- experience

---

# Navigation Principles

Every navigation decision should answer one question:

> What is the most helpful next step?

Possible routes include:

- Observe
- Translate
- Conversation
- Reflect
- Complete

Future routes may include:

- Learn
- Remember
- Escalate
- Collaborate
- Teach

---

# Navigation is Dynamic

Navigation is never a fixed pipeline.

Different situations require different routes.

```text
Situation A

Observe
    ↓
Translate
    ↓
Complete

Situation B

Observe
    ↓
Translate
    ↓
Conversation
    ↓
Reflect

Situation C

Conversation
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Translation
    ↓
Help
```

The destination remains constant.

The route changes.

---

# Design Principle

Capabilities remain independent.

Navigation remains independent.

Professional expertise remains independent.

This separation allows every Digital Colleague to inherit navigation while specialising differently.

---

# Closing Principle

Companion Intelligence is not the intelligence itself.

It is the wisdom to choose where intelligence should go next.

---

> Better navigation creates better understanding.

> Better understanding creates better outcomes.
