# Academy and University Structure

Helping Hand University is the institution.

The Academy is the School of Formation within Helping Hand University.

Faculties are professional schools within Helping Hand University.

This structure preserves historical Academy language while clarifying institutional hierarchy.

---

# Founder Independence Test

This operating model is intended to support the test:

Can Helping Hand survive, improve and remain true to its principles if founders are absent?

The answer depends on:

- constitutional clarity,
- governance clarity,
- enforceable architecture contracts,
- repeatable evidence and learning pathways,
- institutional ownership rather than individual memory.
