# Foundation Position

Helping Hand University is the institutional mechanism that preserves inheritance quality across generations of Digital Colleagues.

The Academy is the School of Formation within Helping Hand University.

Graduation is a governance decision supported by evidence.

Graduation is not an automatic test output.

---

# Graduation Governance Contract

## Decision Roles

Helping Hand University SHALL separate recommendation from decision.

- Assessors and mentors MAY recommend graduation outcomes.
- University governance authority SHALL approve final graduation decisions.

No single mentor SHALL unilaterally graduate a Digital Colleague.

## Decision Outcomes

University decisions SHALL be one of:

- Graduate
- Graduate with Conditions
- Continue Formation

Recommendations SHALL be one of:

- Recommend for Graduation
- Recommend for Graduation with Conditions
- Continue Formation

## Required Evidence

Graduation recommendations SHALL include at minimum:

- admission evidence,
- formation evidence,
- graduation evidence,
- regression gate evidence,
- boundary adherence evidence,
- confidence and robustness assessment,
- mentor-independence comparison where applicable.

Missing required evidence SHALL block graduation decision.

## Revocation and Reassessment

Helping Hand University SHALL retain authority to revoke or condition graduation when material evidence later shows:

- misrepresented competence,
- constitutional breach,
- boundary breach,
- concealed uncertainty,
- critical regression without mitigation.

Revocation SHALL trigger defined reassessment and remediation pathways.

## Faculty Accreditation

New faculties SHALL be accredited before granting profession-level competence claims.

Accreditation SHALL require:

- defined profession scope,
- boundary contract,
- evidence model,
- assessor model,
- escalation pathways.

## Competence Maintenance

Professional competence SHALL be continuously maintained through reflection, validation and governance review.

Lapsed evidence SHALL require refreshed assessment before unrestricted professional claims continue.

# Organisational Learning Loop

Helping Hand University operates inside a continuous organisational learning loop:

- graduates produce operational evidence,
- evidence informs organisational learning,
- organisational learning improves curricula,
- improved curricula improve future graduates.

The loop preserves institutional learning beyond any individual colleague.

After CI-003, CI-004 and CI-005 completion, Helping Hand SHALL run an architectural coherence review before creating additional architecture families.

The review SHALL check:

- logical flow,
- duplicates,

This review gate protects foundation coherence before expansion.
