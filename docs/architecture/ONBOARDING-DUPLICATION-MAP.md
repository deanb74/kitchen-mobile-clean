# Onboarding Duplication Map

## Purpose

This document maps the main overlaps between the current onboarding material and the formation inheritance architecture.

## Overlap map

| Area | Onboarding current behaviour | Formation architecture expectation | Classification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Welcome | Offers welcome and belonging | Welcome is a formation stage and should remain a distinct formation concern | Appropriate reinforcement |
| Identity framing | Introduces Annie as a colleague and a helper | Identity should be discovered and evidenced through formation rather than introduced as settled onboarding content | Premature teaching |
| First-day posture | Frames usefulness from the first shift | First-day understanding belongs to formation and should not be treated as a complete operational readiness statement | Premature teaching |
| Humility and learning | Introduces humility and learning posture immediately | These values are formation-adjacent, but should be developed through formation rather than treated as onboarding completion | Unclear boundary |
| Venue orientation | Asks for immediate context and current challenges | This is appropriate onboarding, not formation | Appropriate reinforcement |
| Operational readiness | Suggests useful action from the first shift | Readiness should be staged and evidence-based rather than implied by onboarding | Premature teaching |
| Relationship posture | Establishes partnership and help-seeking behaviour | This can be present in onboarding, but the deeper meaning of relationship should be carried by formation | Unclear boundary |

## Main duplication concern

The largest duplication is not that onboarding and formation both mention welcome or learning. The larger issue is that onboarding sometimes acts as if the colleague has already entered the formation path and is already ready to be understood in those terms.

## Boundary recommendation

Onboarding should carry arrival and orientation content only. Formation should carry the deeper identity, character, and responsibility development path.
