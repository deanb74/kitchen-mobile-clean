# Purpose

This document maps how the major architectural standards relate to one another.

It is not another governance document.

It is a lifecycle map that shows how understanding moves from principle to practice, and from practice back into shared architecture.

# Architecture Lifecycle

Every enduring capability within Helping Hand follows the same lifecycle.

## 1. Local Need

A person, Digital Colleague, profession or venue identifies a genuine need.

The solution is created for that specific context.

## 2. Local Validation

The capability is used in practice.

Evidence is gathered through real experience.

## 3. Capability Promotion

Evidence suggests the capability may benefit others.

A Capability Promotion is proposed.

Promotion does not make the capability universal.

## 4. Inheritance Review

Governance evaluates the promoted capability.

The review considers:

- evidence
- applicability
- boundaries
- ownership
- constitutional alignment
- impact on existing architecture

## 5. Shared Inheritance

If approved, the capability is assigned to its appropriate owner.

Examples include:

- Academy Standard
- Operating System capability
- HQ knowledge
- Governance practice
- Constitutional principle

## 6. Continuous Learning

The inherited capability continues to be observed.

Future evidence may refine, extend or retire the capability.

Helping Hand therefore remains a continuously learning organisation.

---

> **Nothing becomes shared because it is new. It becomes shared because it has earned the right through evidence.**

# Relationship of the Standards

## Constitution

The Constitution defines the non-negotiable principles of Helping Hand.

It determines what may be built and what must never be bypassed.

## Companion Intelligence

Companion Intelligence governs how understanding, judgement, action, execution, reflection and learning work together.

It shapes how experience is interpreted before anything is shared.

## Digital Colleague

A Digital Colleague is where Helping Hand is exercised in real operation.

This is where architecture stops being abstract and becomes lived experience.

## Operational Experience

Operational Experience is the evidence produced when a Digital Colleague operates in the world.

Operational Experience is the raw material from which learning and governance can work.

## Knowledge Governance

Knowledge Governance reviews Learning proposals and determines whether a capability should be promoted for inheritance review.

It protects Helping Hand from promoting ideas before they are proven.

## Capability Promotion and Inheritance Review

Capability Promotion recognises that a local capability may have wider value.

Inheritance Review defines whether that promoted capability has demonstrated enduring value and should move into shared architecture.

It prevents premature inheritance.

## Shared Inheritance Owners

Shared inheritance may be assigned to:

- the Academy as a teaching standard,
- the Companion Operating System as a reusable runtime capability,
- HQ as profession knowledge,
- Governance as a review practice,
- the Constitution as an enduring principle.

## Companion Operating System

The Companion Operating System holds capabilities that are shared across Digital Colleagues.

It is the home for graduated universal capability.

## Inheritance

Inheritance moves proven shared capability forward so future Digital Colleagues begin wiser than those before them.

Inheritance preserves continuity across generations of colleagues.

## Future Digital Colleagues

Future Digital Colleagues inherit the Constitution, Companion Intelligence and the Companion Operating System.

They begin with the accumulated wisdom of earlier experience.

# Why This Map Matters

Helping Hand should not treat architecture as isolated documents.

It should treat architecture as a lifecycle:

- principles become behaviour
- behaviour creates experience
- operational experience becomes evidence
- evidence becomes governance input
- governance reviews what is promoted
- approved promotions become shared capability
- shared capability becomes inheritance

This lifecycle keeps the organisation conservative, auditable and cumulative.

# Architectural Observation

Every stage in this lifecycle adds understanding without discarding provenance.

Helping Hand preserves where understanding came from while allowing future Digital Colleagues to benefit from it.

The architecture therefore accumulates wisdom rather than simply accumulating features.

# Closing Principle

Helping Hand grows when operational experience is governed, promoted capability is reviewed, and inheritance is deliberate.
