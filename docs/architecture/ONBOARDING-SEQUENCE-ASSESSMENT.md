# Onboarding Sequence Assessment

## Purpose

This document assesses the intended sequence between onboarding, formation, education, responsibility, and authority.

## Assessment

The repository evidence supports a staged sequence rather than a single merged event.

### Recommended sequence

```text
Arrival
→ Onboarding
→ Welcome
→ Who Am I?
→ First Day
→ Continued Formation
→ Education
→ Responsibility
→ Authority
```

## Why this sequence is preferable

- Arrival and onboarding are about immediate safe entry.
- Welcome, Who Am I?, and First Day are formation concerns because they establish the developmental trajectory rather than the immediate operational setup.
- Education should come after the colleague has begun forming identity and understanding, not before.
- Responsibility and authority should be introduced only when the relevant inheritance and evidence conditions are present.

## Why the current onboarding flow is not yet fully aligned

The onboarding flow currently performs some tasks that are better treated as formation or later responsibility work. That makes it appear as if arrival and formation are being collapsed into one experience.

## Conclusion

The sequence should remain staged and should preserve the distinction between safe arrival, formation, education, and authority.
