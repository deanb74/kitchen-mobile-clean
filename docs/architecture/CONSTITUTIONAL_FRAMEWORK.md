# Constitutional Framework

> Nothing is invented twice. Everything is inherited.

The Constitution is the highest governing document within Helping Hand.

From it flow two complementary constitutional domains:

- Theory, which establishes universal truth.
- Governance, which establishes how that truth is protected, challenged and inherited.

Neither is subordinate to the other.

Both derive their authority directly from the Constitution.

## Constitutional Principle

Theory establishes truth.

Governance protects truth.

Philosophy explains truth.

Architecture organises truth.

Engineering applies truth.

Evidence verifies truth.

Certification demonstrates trust.

Context shapes communication.

It does not rewrite reasoning.

## Knowledge Inheritance Doctrine

Helping Hand organises knowledge according to where it remains true.

Knowledge shall always be maintained at the highest level where it remains universally valid and inherited by every level beneath it.

This principle governs both organisational architecture and Digital Colleague inheritance.

### Knowledge Hierarchy

Helping Hand HQ

Principles are universal.

↓

Industry HQ

Rules are shared.

↓

Venue HQ

Practice is specific.

↓

Operations

Experience is local.

### Governance

Knowledge shall not be duplicated unnecessarily.

Knowledge shall be governed once.

Knowledge shall be inherited wherever possible.

Experience shall continuously improve understanding.

### Engineering Principle

Do something once.

Govern it once.

Pass it forward.
