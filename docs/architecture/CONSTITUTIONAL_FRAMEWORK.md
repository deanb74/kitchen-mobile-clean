# Constitutional Framework

> Nothing is invented twice. Everything is inherited.

The Constitution is the highest governing document within Helping Hand.

From it flow two complementary constitutional domains:

- Theory, which establishes universal truth.
- Governance, which establishes how that truth is protected, challenged and inherited.

Neither is subordinate to the other.

Both derive their authority directly from the Constitution.

## Responsibilities

### Constitution

Defines the identity, purpose and governing principles of Helping Hand.

---

### Theory

Defines universal concepts.

Answers:

> What is true?

---

### Governance

Defines how truth is maintained.

Answers:

> How is truth protected?

---

### Philosophy

Explains why theory matters.

---

### Architecture

Organises theory into coherent systems.

Includes the separation of stable reasoning from adaptive explanation.

---

### Engineering

Builds those systems.

Implements contextual adaptation without altering constitutional reasoning.

---

### Implementation

Applies engineering in practice.

---

### Evidence

Measures whether implementation behaves as intended.

---

### Certification

Demonstrates justified trust through validated evidence.

---

## Constitutional Principle

Theory establishes truth.

Governance protects truth.

Philosophy explains truth.

Architecture organises truth.

Engineering applies truth.

Evidence verifies truth.

Certification demonstrates trust.

Context shapes communication.

It does not rewrite reasoning.
