## Purpose

This document records the first completed Capability Promotion under the Helping Hand governance model.

It exists as precedent.

Future promotions should follow the process demonstrated here rather than inventing a new process each time.

---

## Precedent Statement

When a capability moves from created-for-one to available-for-all, follow the process demonstrated by the First Capability Promotion.

---

## Capability Promoted

**Name:** Reflection Record

**Original context:** Local reflection form for Andy

**Promoted context:** Shared Academy standard for guided learner reflection and mentor review

---

## Why Promotion Was Triggered

The capability stopped being local to one colleague.

It became a reusable discipline that can improve learning quality for future colleagues.

The transition met the local-to-shared threshold:

- created for one;
- valuable for many;
- boundary and owner became clear;
- governance rationale was explicit.

---

## Inheritance Review Summary

### Evidence

Operational use showed the record supports structured reflection, mentor feedback, and actionable improvement signals.

### Applicability

Applicable beyond Andy to future learners and mentors in Academy formation journeys.

### Boundaries

The record is educational practice.

It is not runtime Operating System capability.

### Ownership

Owner assigned to Academy.

### Constitutional Alignment

Aligned with principles of understanding, honesty, learning, and deliberate inheritance.

### Architectural Impact

Improves consistency of reflection practice while preserving local learner voice.

## Reuse Rule for Future Promotions

Future Capability Promotions should include the same minimum elements:

1. Local origin and context.
2. Evidence of wider value.
3. Boundary clarity.
4. Owner assignment.
5. Governance review outcome.
6. Canonical shared location.

## Closing Principle

This document is not important because Reflection Record is special.

It is important because it establishes precedent.

Helping Hand should evolve by governed inheritance, not ad hoc promotion.
