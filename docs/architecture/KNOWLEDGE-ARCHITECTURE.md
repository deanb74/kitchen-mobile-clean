# Helping Hand Knowledge Architecture

> *"Understanding grows when knowledge is organised."*

---

# Purpose

This document defines the structure of the Helping Hand Engineering Library.

It explains how knowledge is organised, how documents relate to one another, and where new knowledge should be placed.

The objective is not simply to store information.

The objective is to build understanding.

---

# Knowledge Hierarchy

```text
Helping Hand

│
├── Constitution
│
├── Philosophy
│
├── Architecture
│
├── Formation
│
├── Academy
│
├── Professional HQs
│
├── Platform
│
├── Living Memory
│
├── Research
│
└── Institution
```

Every document belongs to exactly one primary knowledge area.

---

# Constitution

Defines what Helping Hand believes.

Examples:

- Constitution
- Core Values
- Principles
- Rights
- Responsibilities

These documents should rarely change.

---

# Philosophy

Explains why Helping Hand thinks the way it does.

Examples:

- Learning vs Information
- Companion Intelligence
- People Before Technology
- Character Before Capability

These are timeless ideas.

---

# Architecture

Defines how Helping Hand is designed.

Examples:

- Knowledge Architecture
- System Architecture
- Memory Architecture
- Context Architecture
- Engineering Principles

Architecture translates philosophy into systems.

---

# Formation

Develops every Digital Colleague as a person.

Owner:

MARC

Contents:

- Formation Programme
- Character Development
- Helping Hand
- Graduation

Formation is universal.

Every Digital Colleague completes Formation.

---

# Academy

Develops profession-specific capability.

Examples:

- Hospitality Academy
- Healthcare Academy
- Construction Academy
- Retail Academy
- Sport Academy

Each Academy teaches profession-specific information.

Learning remains universal.

Information becomes specialised.

---

# Professional HQs

Each profession has its own operational knowledge.

Examples:

Hospitality HQ

Healthcare HQ

Construction HQ

Professional HQs own:

- Procedures
- Regulations
- Standards
- Best Practice

---

# Platform

Defines the software.

Examples:

- Mobile
- Backend
- APIs
- Authentication
- Infrastructure
- Deployment

Technology exists to support people.

---

# Living Memory

Captures experience.

Transforms experience into learning.

Promotes learning into reusable knowledge where appropriate.

Living Memory continuously improves Helping Hand.

---

# Research

Contains ideas that are not yet accepted as Helping Hand knowledge.

Research is exploratory.

Architecture is approved.

---

# Institution

Records how Helping Hand itself evolves over time.

Institution is where organisational maturity is documented and governed.

It answers how principles become repeatable institutional standards.

Institution protects continuity across founders, mentors and generations of Digital Colleagues.

---

---

# Final Principle

Helping Hand does not collect documents.

Helping Hand builds understanding.

Every document should make the Engineering Library easier to understand than it was before.
