# Annie Graduation Audit Governance Review

**Framework:** Helping Hand Governance Framework v1.0  
**Scope:** Formal governance review of `ANNIE-GRADUATION-AUDIT.md`  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

---

## Confirmed decisions

- **Memory** as **Already Graduated** is currently the strongest supported decision. The universal mechanism and local content split are explicitly defined and consistently argued.
- **Conversation** as **Candidate for Graduation** is appropriate. Universal need plus local expression boundary is clear, but evidence is still mostly Annie-local.
- **Understanding** as **Candidate for Graduation** is appropriate and well-governed. The section explicitly identifies unresolved boundary questions.
- **Learning** as **Candidate for Graduation** is appropriate. Boundary uncertainty and inherited-form uncertainty are correctly recorded.
- **Judgement** as **Candidate for Graduation** is appropriate. Governance constraints and cross-colleague evidence needs are correctly flagged.

## Decisions that should be reconsidered

- **Observation** marked **Already Graduated** should be reconsidered to **Candidate for Graduation** until a full capability section exists and boundary evidence is explicit.
- **Reflection** marked **Already Graduated** should be reconsidered to **Candidate for Graduation** pending boundary clarification with Learning.

## Missing evidence

- No detailed capability sections for **Observation** and **Reflection** using the same question structure promised for every capability.
- No explicit universal mechanism vs local implementation boundary text for **Observation** and **Reflection** comparable to what exists for other capabilities.
- No cross-colleague operational evidence examples for most capabilities beyond Annie-centric narrative. This weakens claims marked **Proven**.
- No explicit evidence guidance was previously stated; this has now been clarified as governance judgement rather than rigid decision mapping.

## Contradictions

- **Reflection** is marked **Proven** and **Already Graduated**, while **Learning** states the Learning-Memory-Reflection boundary is not yet fully governed.
- The audit states practical questions are used for every capability, but two capabilities with final decisions (**Observation** and **Reflection**) lack corresponding detailed sections.
- The audit is described as completed while several capabilities remain in **Audit** action state. This is defensible as a first completed pass, but governance-wise it reads as mixed completion states.

## Recommendations

- Reclassify **Observation** and **Reflection** decisions to **Candidate for Graduation** unless and until full capability evidence sections are added and boundary contracts are explicit.
- Keep **Memory** as **Already Graduated**.
- Keep **Conversation**, **Understanding**, **Learning**, and **Judgement** as **Candidate for Graduation**.
- Keep the summary table concise, but require one authoritative detailed section per capability before final Released-grade governance sign-off on that capability outcome.

### Graduation Evidence

An **Already Graduated** decision requires explicit evidence of:

- a clearly identified universal mechanism;
- a clearly defined local implementation boundary;
- sufficient operational evidence demonstrating that the capability is not dependent upon a single professional implementation.

Cross-colleague validation strengthens confidence where available but is not mandatory for the first graduating capability.

## Rejected Approach

Rigid mapping between evidence class and decision outcome is rejected.

- **Partial** does not automatically mean **Candidate for Graduation**.
- **Proven** does not automatically mean **Already Graduated**.

Governance decisions should be based on sufficient operational evidence plus cross-colleague validation where available, alongside boundary clarity and architectural judgement.
