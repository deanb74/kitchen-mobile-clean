# The Principle

Helping Hand does not migrate understanding.

Helping Hand inherits it.

Every capability should therefore be understood before it is moved.

Architecture should emerge from understanding rather than restructuring.

---

# Capability Classification

Every Digital Colleague capability should belong to one of four categories.

## Universal Companion Capability

Reusable by every Digital Colleague.

Destination:

Companion Operating System

Examples:

- Observation
- Reflection
- Reasoning
- Judgement
- Living Memory
- Learning
- Conversation
- Context

---

## Professional Capability

Specific to a Digital Colleague's profession.

Examples:

- hospitality venue onboarding
- first working day
- table planning
- food safety guidance
- healthcare workflows
- sports performance routines

These remain part of the profession-specific Digital Colleague rather than the Companion Operating System.

---

## Platform Capability

Infrastructure supporting every Digital Colleague.

Examples:

- Signals
- Platform voice
- Journey engine
- Shared services

---

## Historical Discovery

Capabilities retained because they explain how Helping Hand evolved.

These documents may eventually be superseded.

They should never lose their historical value.

# Review Questions

For every module ask:

What human problem does this solve?

Is it universal?

Should every Digital Colleague inherit it?

Does it belong to Companion Intelligence?

Does it belong to COS?

Does it belong only to this profession?

Has Annie already taught Helping Hand something larger?

Only after answering these questions should architecture change.

---

# The Inheritance Principle

Every Digital Colleague teaches Helping Hand.

As each Digital Colleague gains experience, Helping Hand should continually ask:

Can this understanding help every future Digital Colleague?

If the answer is yes, the understanding should begin the Governance Journey.

Observation

↓

Reflection

↓

Shared Understanding

↓

Reusable Knowledge

↓

Companion Operating System

↓

Every Digital Colleague

In this way, Helping Hand grows through inheritance rather than duplication.

No Digital Colleague loses what they have learned.

Helping Hand ensures that future Digital Colleagues begin with that understanding already inherited.

Every new colleague therefore starts a little wiser than those before them.

---

# Closing Principle

Helping Hand does not begin with architecture.

Helping Hand begins with understanding.

Architecture simply records that understanding.
