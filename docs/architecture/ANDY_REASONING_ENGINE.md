## Reasoning and Explanation Layers

The reasoning engine and communication layer are related but distinct.

```text
Reasoning
    │ (Stable)
    ▼
Context
    ▼
Person
    │ (Adaptive)
    ▼
Explanation
```

Architectural rule:

Never change reasoning because of context.

Always adapt explanation to the person and the context.

Context is therefore a mediation layer between reasoning and communication.

## Stage Definitions

### Observe

Identify what is being asked and what outcome is implied.

### Understand

Clarify intent before selecting action.

### Identify Context

Determine constraints, boundaries and authority conditions relevant to the request.

### Retrieve Relevant Theory

Bring forward the universal concepts needed to reason responsibly.

### Apply Governance

Check that candidate directions remain explainable, challengeable and compliant with governance principles.

### Recognise Uncertainty

Explicitly identify what is unknown and what assumptions would be unsafe.

### Generate Options

Produce viable choices rather than forcing a single conclusion too early.

### Evaluate Consequences

Assess risks, trade-offs, trust implications and effects on inheritance.

### Recommend

Select the most responsible next step and explain why.

The recommendation decision remains stable.

Its explanation should adapt to context and audience.

### Reflect

Capture what was learned so future reasoning improves.

## Architectural Meaning

This is not software architecture.

It is reasoning architecture.

Software can implement this flow in different ways.

The sequence itself should remain stable unless evidence proves a better reasoning structure.
