# Helping Hand Architecture Review Board

**Document ID:** HH-GOV-004
**Status:** Foundation
**Owner:** Helping Hand Governance
**Category:** Governance
**Depends On:** HH-GOV-001, HH-GOV-002, HH-GOV-003
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Helping Hand Architecture Review Board safeguards the long-term integrity of Helping Hand.

Its role is to ensure that all institutional standards remain consistent with the Constitution, organisational architecture and long-term vision.

---

# Responsibilities

The Board shall:

- review proposed standards;
- assess architectural coherence;
- identify duplication;
- identify contradictions;
- identify governance gaps;
- recommend improvements;
- protect institutional consistency;
- support long-term sustainability.

---

# Independence

The Architecture Review Board shall operate independently of the authors of the standards under review.

Constructive challenge is encouraged.

Personal ownership shall never outweigh institutional integrity.

---

# Review Principles

Every review shall consider:

- constitutional alignment;
- organisational impact;
- architectural coherence;
- governance consistency;
- clarity;
- maintainability;
- founder independence.

---

# Founder Independence Test

Every major review shall ask:

> **If the founders disappeared tomorrow, could Helping Hand continue from these standards alone?**

Where the answer is No, the missing knowledge shall be identified and documented.

---

# Review Outcomes

The Board may recommend:

- approval;
- approval with recommendations;
- revision;
- deferment;
- rejection.

Final ratification follows the governance process defined in HH-GOV-003.

---

# Long-Term Stewardship

The Architecture Review Board exists to protect Helping Hand beyond any individual contributor.

Its responsibility is to preserve the organisation's purpose while enabling continual improvement.

---

# Summary

Helping Hand is intended to outlive its founders.

The Architecture Review Board exists to ensure that every generation inherits an organisation that remains true to its Constitution while continuing to learn, improve and serve people.
