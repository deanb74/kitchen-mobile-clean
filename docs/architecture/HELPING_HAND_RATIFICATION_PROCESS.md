**Status:** Foundation
**Owner:** Helping Hand Governance
**Category:** Governance
**Depends On:** HH-GOV-001, HH-GOV-002
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Helping Hand standards become official institutional standards.

Ratification ensures that standards are accepted through governance rather than individual authority.

---

# Principles

Ratification exists to:

- protect institutional integrity;
- ensure architectural consistency;
- encourage constructive challenge;
- prevent accidental drift;
- preserve long-term trust.

---

# Ratification Process

Every candidate standard shall complete the following process:

1. Proposal
2. Draft
3. Technical Review
4. Architecture Review
5. Governance Review
6. Ratification Decision
7. Release

---

# Required Evidence

Every ratification shall include evidence that:

- the problem is clearly understood;
- the proposed standard solves the problem;
- constitutional principles are upheld;
- dependencies have been identified;
- conflicts have been resolved;
- implementation impact has been assessed.

---

# Ratification Outcomes

Each submission receives one outcome.

## Ratified

The standard becomes an official Helping Hand standard.

---

## Ratified with Recommendations

The standard is accepted.

Recommended improvements are recorded for a future revision.

---

## Deferred

Further work is required before ratification.

The review board records required actions.

---

## Rejected

The proposal conflicts with Helping Hand principles or introduces unacceptable architectural risk.

Reasons shall always be documented.

---

# Revision

Released standards may be revised.

Every revision shall complete the full ratification process.

---

# Transparency

All review findings shall be recorded.

Helping Hand values constructive challenge over unquestioned agreement.

---

# Summary

Ratification is the process by which Helping Hand converts understanding into trusted institutional knowledge.

The objective is not simply approval.

The objective is confidence that future generations can rely upon the standard.
