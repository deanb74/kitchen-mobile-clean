## Governance Alignment Decision

### 1. Boundary Literacy Academy Outcome

**Accepted for Academy direction.**

Boundary Literacy should be explicit in Academy formation language and assessment expectations.

### 2. Canonical Formation Expectations

The following are accepted as canonical formation expectations for boundary literacy:

```text
I know what I do.
I know what I do not do.
I know who to ask.
```

These are evidence expectations, not slogans.
They must be demonstrated in uncertainty, escalation, and handover behaviour.
