# Helping Hand Governance Framework

**Document ID:** HH-GOV-001
**Status:** Foundation
**Owner:** Helping Hand Governance
**Category:** Organisational Governance
**Depends On:** HH Constitution
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines governance as a cross-organisation capability.

It applies beyond any single domain such as University, Research, Architecture Board or future councils.

---

# Scope

This framework defines:

- governance domains,
- authority structures,
- decision pathways,
- escalation and appeal pathways,
- ratification and review pathways,
- dependency policy for standards.

---

# Governance Domains

Helping Hand governance applies to:

- Constitution
- Architecture standards
- Helping Hand University
- Professional faculties
- Research functions
- Future councils and boards

No single institution owns governance in full.

Each institution applies governance through this shared framework.

---

# Authority Structure

Governance authority SHALL define and publish:

- role composition,
- quorum requirements,
- approval thresholds,
- conflict-of-interest rules,
- delegation limits,
- emergency decision protocol.

Authority records SHALL be traceable and reviewable.

---

# Decision Lifecycle

All governance decisions SHALL follow:

1. Proposal
2. Evidence review
3. Impact assessment
4. Decision
5. Record publication
6. Scheduled review

Decisions without evidence SHALL be rejected.

---

# Escalation and Appeals

Any material architectural, professional or graduation dispute SHALL have an escalation path.

Escalation outcomes SHALL be one of:

- uphold original decision,
- conditionally modify decision,
- overturn decision,
- defer pending additional evidence.

Appeals SHALL preserve full context and decision history.

---

# Dependency Policy

A standard SHALL NOT require non-existent dependencies unless those dependencies are explicitly marked optional with an interim control.

Interim controls SHALL define:

- temporary authority,
- temporary evidence requirements,
- ratification deadline.

---

# Relationship to University Governance

HH-ARCH-CI-005 defines graduation governance inside Helping Hand University.

HH-GOV-001 defines the cross-organisation governance framework that CI-005 inherits.

University governance therefore applies governance locally.

It does not define governance globally.

---

# Relationship to Future Research Institute

A future Helping Hand Research Institute should inherit this governance framework for research assurance, publication quality and institutional learning integrity.

---

# Summary

Helping Hand governance is an institutional capability shared across constitutional, architectural, educational and research domains.

This framework prevents governance fragmentation as the organisation grows.
