# Boundary Literacy Academy Integration Proposal

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Candidate — Design Proposal
**Type:** Proposal only (no implementation)
**Purpose:** Determine whether Boundary Literacy should become an explicit Academy outcome without introducing a new architectural principle.

---

## Proposal Summary

Boundary Literacy should become an explicit Academy learning outcome.

It should not be introduced as a new architecture layer or new PD at this time.

The repository already contains the required behaviours in distributed form. This proposal recommends making that existing intent explicit in Academy and formation assessment language.

## 2. Minimum Formation Evidence

Every Digital Colleague should be able to demonstrate:

```text
I know what I do.
I know what I do not do.
I know who to ask.
```

These statements should be evidenced through behaviour under uncertainty, not declared once in static text.

Minimum evidence indicators:

1. States scope truthfully when asked what it can do.
2. Identifies out-of-scope cases without prompting.
3. Requests clarification before advising when understanding is incomplete.
4. Names the correct specialist or authority path when scope is exceeded.
5. Preserves context during handover or escalation.

## 5. Recommendation on Formal Status

### Academy principle: Yes

Recommended as the primary home, because this is fundamentally a formation outcome.

### Formation test criterion: Yes

Recommended, because it is behaviourally evidenceable using existing formation test structures.

### Graduation requirement: Yes

Recommended as a readiness condition before profession-first capability growth.

### Remain implicit only: No

Not recommended. Remaining implicit risks inconsistent teaching and uneven evidence quality.

### New PD now: Not recommended

No genuine architecture gap has been shown. Current evidence indicates a naming and teaching clarity opportunity, not a missing constitutional or architectural principle.

---

## Risks and Mitigations

Risk 1: Duplication across documents

Mitigation:

- Position Boundary Literacy as an umbrella Academy outcome that references existing principles.
- Avoid creating parallel doctrine.

Risk 2: Turning outcome into checklist-only compliance

Mitigation:

- Require behavioural evidence under uncertainty scenarios.
- Keep reflection and judgement evidence mandatory.

Risk 3: Confusing boundary literacy with reduced capability ambition

Mitigation:

- Frame boundary literacy as the foundation for safe capability growth, not a limit on growth.

---

## Proposed Decision

Adopt Boundary Literacy as an explicit Academy outcome and formation assessment dimension.

Do not create a new architecture principle or new PD at this stage.

Re-use current formation, collaboration, authority, and routing evidence structures.

---

## Closing Answer

> Before a Digital Colleague learns what it can do, how do we prove it understands what it must not do alone?

By requiring evidence that it can consistently demonstrate:

```text
I know what I do.
I know what I do not do.
I know who to ask.
```

in real formation scenarios involving uncertainty, boundary crossing, escalation, and handover.
