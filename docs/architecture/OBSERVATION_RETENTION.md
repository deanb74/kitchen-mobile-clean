# Observation Retention

**Status:** Foundational Architecture

---

> Helping Hand remembers understanding.

> It retains observations only when they have continuing value.

---

# Purpose

Helping Hand exists to create understanding, not collect unnecessary data.

Observations are used to improve understanding.

Only observations with lasting value should be retained.

---

# Philosophy

Observation is temporary.

Understanding is enduring.

Evidence is preserved.

Helping Hand should always minimise unnecessary storage while protecting information that genuinely matters.

---

# The Observation Journey

Observation

↓

Understanding

↓

Living Memory

↓

Decision

↓

Action

The observation has fulfilled its purpose.

Unless there is a reason to retain it, it should be discarded.

---

# What Should Be Stored

Helping Hand stores understanding.

Examples include:

- temperatures
- dates
- identities
- product names
- expiry dates
- compliance outcomes
- trends
- equipment status
- observations
- confidence
- decisions

Helping Hand remembers what was learnt.

---

# What Should Normally Be Discarded

Examples include:

- temporary photographs
- scanned documents
- label images
- receipts
- handwritten notes
- whiteboards

Once understanding has been successfully extracted, these observations normally have no further value.

---

# Evidence

Some observations have value beyond understanding.

These should be retained.

Examples include:

- accident evidence
- maintenance evidence
- damaged equipment
- insurance claims
- compliance failures
- pest activity
- customer complaints
- legal evidence
- audit records

Evidence should always remain linked to the understanding created from it.

---

# Privacy

Helping Hand stores the minimum information necessary.

It should not retain observations simply because it can.

Reducing unnecessary storage protects:

- privacy
- trust
- security
- performance

---

# Human Review

Where confidence is low, Helping Hand may ask a person to confirm its understanding before discarding the original observation.

Once confidence is sufficient, the temporary observation may be removed unless it has evidential value.

---

# Relationship with Universal Awareness

Observation creates awareness.

Awareness creates understanding.

Understanding creates judgement.

Judgement creates action.

Observation is therefore the beginning of the learning process, not its destination.

---

# Success

Observation Retention succeeds when Helping Hand remembers what matters while avoiding unnecessary accumulation of data.

The goal is not to remember everything.

The goal is to remember what helps people.

---

# Closing Principle

Helping Hand does not build a library of photographs.

Helping Hand builds understanding.

Evidence is preserved.

Understanding is remembered.

Everything else should disappear when its purpose has been fulfilled.

---

> Observe.

> Understand.

> Remember.

> Let the observation go.

> Keep the understanding.
