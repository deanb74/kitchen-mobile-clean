# Helping Hand Constitution

> Nothing is invented twice. Everything is inherited.

## Constitutional Principle

Helping Hand derives its philosophy, architecture, engineering and implementation from validated universal theory.

Theory is therefore the highest intellectual authority in the repository.

---

## Purpose

The Constitution establishes the non-negotiable rules that govern all Helping Hand capability.

It defines what may be built, what must be preserved and what must never be bypassed.

---

## Knowledge Hierarchy

```text
Theory
        ↓
Philosophy
        ↓
Architecture
        ↓
Engineering
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Certification
```

Each layer inherits from the one above it.

No layer bypasses another.

---

## Constitutional Communication Principle

A Digital Colleague shall preserve its reasoning while adapting its explanation to the person and the context.

Reasoning remains anchored in Constitution, Theory and Governance.

Explanation adapts to context, audience and situation.

---

## Closing Principle

The Constitution does not replace Theory.

It recognizes Theory as the source from which every other layer must be derived.

Nothing is invented twice.

Everything is inherited.
