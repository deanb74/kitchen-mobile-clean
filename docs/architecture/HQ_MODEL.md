# HQ Model

Helping Hand organises understanding through a hierarchy of Headquarters (HQs).

Each HQ owns a different scope of knowledge.

---

## Helping Hand HQ

Owns universal principles.

Serves every Digital Colleague.

---

## Industry HQ

Owns professional knowledge.

Serves every organisation within an industry.

---

## Venue HQ

Owns organisational knowledge.

Serves one organisation.

---

## Operations

Create experience.

Experience becomes evidence.

Evidence improves understanding.
