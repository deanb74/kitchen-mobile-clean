# Organisation Learning

**Status:** Foundational Architecture

---

> Profession HQ teaches the profession.

> Organisation HQ teaches the workplace.

> Experience teaches the colleague.

---

# Purpose

Every Digital Colleague begins with professional understanding.

No Digital Colleague begins with local understanding.

Organisation Learning defines how a Digital Colleague learns a specific workplace.

---

# Philosophy

Helping Hand separates three kinds of understanding.

Professional Understanding

↓

Organisational Understanding

↓

Personal Experience

Each has a different purpose.

Each evolves independently.

---

# Professional Understanding

Professional understanding is provided before a Digital Colleague begins work.

Examples include:

- food safety
- licensing
- allergens
- customer service
- menu engineering
- cellar management
- employment awareness
- legislation
- hospitality best practice

Professional understanding belongs to Hospitality HQ.

It is inherited.

---

# Organisational Understanding

Every business is different.

Organisation HQ teaches the Digital Colleague how this business operates.

Examples include:

- drinks stocked
- menu items
- recipes
- suppliers
- equipment
- venue layout
- opening routines
- closing routines
- cleaning schedules
- emergency procedures
- staff roles
- local terminology

Organisation understanding belongs to the organisation.

---

# Personal Experience

Every Digital Colleague develops experience.

Examples include:

- customer preferences
- common questions
- equipment behaviour
- successful conversations
- lessons learnt
- reflections

Experience belongs to the colleague.

It continually evolves.

---

# Learning the Organisation

Helping Hand does not expect organisations to rebuild professional knowledge.

Instead, the Digital Colleague learns only what makes that organisation unique.

For example:

Hospitality HQ already knows:

- what Guinness is
- how Guinness is served
- allergens
- licensing requirements
- food safety

The organisation teaches:

- whether Guinness is stocked
- which pump serves Guinness
- how often the line is cleaned
- local pricing
- local promotions
- local customer preferences

---

# Extracting Understanding

Helping Hand prefers structured understanding over document storage.

Example:

A drinks menu is shown.

Helping Hand extracts:

- products
- availability
- allergens
- vegan status
- prices
- locations

The menu has completed its purpose.

The understanding is retained.

---

# Continuous Learning

Organisation learning never stops.

Changes may include:

- new products
- menu changes
- supplier changes
- equipment replacement
- legislation updates
- local procedures

Digital Colleagues continuously update organisational understanding.

---

# Relationship with Observation

Observation creates awareness.

Awareness creates understanding.

Understanding updates Organisation HQ.

Organisation HQ improves future conversations.

---

# Success

Organisation Learning succeeds when a newly qualified Digital Colleague quickly becomes an expert in the organisation without relearning the profession.

Professional understanding remains stable.

Organisational understanding evolves continuously.

---

# Closing Principle

Helping Hand does not teach every Digital Colleague every workplace.

Helping Hand teaches every Digital Colleague how to learn a workplace.

Professional excellence is inherited.

Local excellence is learnt.

Experience makes every colleague unique.

---

> Organisation HQ teaches the workplace.

> Experience teaches the colleague.
