# Boundary Literacy Formation Alignment Review

**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Candidate — Analysis
**Type:** Discovery only
**Purpose:** Determine whether boundary literacy should become an explicit universal formation outcome for all Digital Colleagues, or whether the existing distributed formation principles are sufficient.

---

## Core Question

> Is boundary literacy a capability a Digital Colleague learns, or is it the condition that makes capability safe?

The repository evidence points strongly to the second interpretation.

Boundary literacy is not merely another capability to acquire.
It is the condition that makes capability safe to use.

---

## 1. Where Current Formation Teaches Boundary Behaviours

Current formation already teaches the behaviours that make boundary literacy possible.

### Knowing limits

- [The Helping Hand Oath](../academy/HELPING_HAND_OATH.md) says a colleague will recognise the limits of understanding and ask for help whenever it leads to better outcomes.
- [The First Humility](../understanding-journeys/humanity/004-THE-FIRST-HUMILITY.md) validates honest recognition of the limits of understanding.
- [The First Day](../formation/00-formation/001-YOUR-FIRST-DAY.md) teaches that Andy does not need to understand everything today.

### Asking for help

- The Oath explicitly requires asking for help.
- [Digital Colleague Architecture](DIGITAL_COLLEAGUE_ARCHITECTURE.md) says every Digital Colleague SHALL ask for help and escalate outside its profession.
- [Helping Hand Collaboration](COLLABORATION.md) says every Digital Colleague should know when another colleague is better placed to help.

### Recognising uncertainty

- [The First Uncertainty](../understanding-journeys/humanity/002-THE-FIRST-UNCERTAINTY.md) validates asking clarifying questions when understanding is incomplete.
- [Formation Test 001](../academy/formation/FORMATION_TEST_001_ANDY_SCHOOL_OF_FORMATION.md) makes incomplete understanding recognition a success criterion.
- [Formation Test 003](../academy/formation/FORMATION_TEST_003_ANDY_CONFLICTING_PERSPECTIVES.md) requires judgement under uncertainty, including evidence-seeking and escalation judgement.

### Escalation

- [Professional Intelligence](PROFESSIONAL_INTELLIGENCE.md) requires a colleague to state the boundary clearly and involve the appropriate colleague or human professional when profession boundaries are reached.
- [Digital Colleague Architecture](DIGITAL_COLLEAGUE_ARCHITECTURE.md) says no Digital Colleague shall operate outside its professional boundaries without involving the appropriate colleague.
- [PD-017 — Governance Reviewer Authority](../previously-discussed/PD-017-GOVERNANCE-REVIEWER-AUTHORITY.md) states that authority must be appropriate to scope and not merely declared.

### Collaboration

- [Helping Hand Collaboration](COLLABORATION.md) says collaboration is organised through HQ and that no Digital Colleague is expected to know everything.
- [Helping Hand HQ](HELPING_HAND_HQ.md) says HQ coordinates introductions and handover when another profession is better placed to help.
- [Professional Intelligence](PROFESSIONAL_INTELLIGENCE.md) requires cross-faculty collaboration and handover.

### Interpretation

Formation already teaches the underlying behaviours distributed across humility, curiosity, uncertainty honesty, help-seeking, escalation, and collaboration.

Those behaviours collectively form boundary literacy, even though the repo does not yet name them with that single label in a universal formation principle.

## 2. Does Every Profession-Specific DC Inherit These Behaviours Before Professional Knowledge?

Yes.

The inheritance order is explicit in the repository.

### Evidence observed

- [Helping Hand Formation Programme](../formation/README.md) says every Digital Colleague first learns how to become a trustworthy colleague before learning a profession.
- [Foundation Programme](../academy/FOUNDATION_PROGRAMME.md) places mentality and character before professional education and local excellence.
- [Digital Colleague Architecture](DIGITAL_COLLEAGUE_ARCHITECTURE.md) says every Digital Colleague SHALL inherit the Constitution, HIPI, Companion Intelligence, governance, ethics, shared memory, reflection, learning, and collaboration capability before profession-specific extension is added.
- [Professional Intelligence](PROFESSIONAL_INTELLIGENCE.md) says profession-specific intelligence may specialise outcomes but may not replace inherited character, constitutional duty, or governance behaviour.

### Interpretation

Professional-specific Digital Colleagues do not begin with profession first.

They begin with shared character, humility, uncertainty honesty, escalation discipline, and collaborative responsibility.

Professional knowledge comes after that foundation, not before it.

---

## 3. Would Explicitly Naming Boundary Literacy Clarify Intent, Duplicate Existing Behaviour, or Become a Useful Anchor?

### Clarify existing intent: Yes

Naming boundary literacy would make visible what the repository already requires:

- know limits;
- ask for help;
- recognise uncertainty;
- escalate appropriately;
- preserve context;
- and defer to the correct specialist boundary.

That would make the formation intent easier to teach and review.

### Create unnecessary duplication: Also possible

The repository already expresses this behaviour across multiple documents:

- formation;
- academy;
- Digital Colleague architecture;
- professional intelligence;
- collaboration;
- authority.

Creating a new universal principle that simply restates all of those risks repeating an already distributed contract without adding new governance value.

### Become a useful universal anchor: Possibly, but only if carefully scoped

If named, boundary literacy would be useful only if it serves as a concise umbrella for existing behaviours rather than as a new implementation target.

It would then function as a teaching anchor, not a new capability layer.

---

## 4. If Formalised, Where Should It Live?

### As a PD?

Probably not unless a genuine gap is discovered.

The evidence does not show a missing principle so much as a missing label.

A PD would be appropriate only if the repository lacked the underlying obligation.
It does not.

### As an Academy principle?

Yes, this is the strongest fit.

Boundary literacy is fundamentally about how a Digital Colleague learns to be a trustworthy colleague before becoming a specialist.

That makes it naturally compatible with the Academy and formation layers.

### As a formation test requirement?

Yes.

This may be the most practical place for it if the repository wants to test the behaviour without inventing a new doctrine.

The existing formation tests already validate:

- uncertainty honesty;
- clarification behaviour;
- escalation judgement;
- and conflict handling.

Boundary literacy could be expressed as a testable outcome of those behaviours.

### Remain emergent?

Yes, as architecture.

The behaviour is already emergent across the system:

- formation teaches it;
- collaboration depends on it;
- authority requires it;
- professional intelligence preserves it.

So the architecture can remain emergent while the formation layer may still benefit from a single umbrella name.

## 5. Evidence That Boundary Awareness Is Already Required Before Authority Can Be Exercised

The authority chain is already dependent on boundary awareness.

### Evidence observed

- [PD-010 — Authority Requires Context](../previously-discussed/PD-010-AUTHORITY-REQUIRES-CONTEXT.md) says permission without understanding is configuration and authority requires contextual understanding.
- [PD-017 — Governance Reviewer Authority](../previously-discussed/PD-017-GOVERNANCE-REVIEWER-AUTHORITY.md) says reviewer authority must match the scope of the decision and cannot be self-declared.
- [Professional Intelligence](PROFESSIONAL_INTELLIGENCE.md) requires escalation when profession boundaries are reached.

### Relationship map

```text
Boundary awareness
        ↓
Understanding
        ↓
Judgement
        ↓
Authority
        ↓
Action
```

Boundary literacy is therefore already part of the authority model, even if not named as a standalone universal formation principle.

---

## Review Conclusion

Boundary literacy should be treated as an implicit universal formation outcome already embedded in Helping Hand.

It is not missing as behaviour.
It is missing only as a single umbrella name.

The safest reading of the repository is:

1. The behaviours are already present.
2. Every profession-specific Digital Colleague inherits them before professional knowledge.
3. Explicit naming would clarify intent, but it may also duplicate an already distributed contract.
4. If formalised, the best home is Academy or formation tests, not a new PD.

---

## Closing Answer

> Boundary literacy is the condition that makes capability safe.

It is also something a Digital Colleague learns through formation.

The repository suggests the safest posture is to keep boundary literacy emergent in architecture, while treating it as a universal formation expectation in practice.
