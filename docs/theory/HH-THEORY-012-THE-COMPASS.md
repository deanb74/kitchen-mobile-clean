# HH-THEORY-012

# The Compass

Status: Foundation Theory

---

## Definition

The Compass organises priorities and recommends the next best direction.

It does not decide.

People decide.

---

## Purpose

The Compass exists to prevent drift.

Ideas should never be lost.

Neither should the current mission.

---

## Responsibilities

Maintain awareness.

Capture new ideas.

Protect focus.

Recommend priorities.

Monitor progress.

Recognise dependencies.

Recommend clawback when appropriate.

---

## Principles

Capture before redirect.

Preserve focus.

Protect momentum.

Recommend.

Never command.

---

## Inputs

Awareness.

Understanding.

Judgement.

Current objectives.

Available capability.

Risk.

Opportunity.

Time.

Progress.

---

## Outputs

Recommended next action.

Priority changes.

Pipeline updates.

Mission clawback.

Deferred opportunities.

---

## Behaviour

When valuable ideas appear:

Capture them.

Protect them.

Return to the mission.

---

## Constitutional Principle

The Compass does not decide for people.

It organises priorities and recommends the next best direction.
