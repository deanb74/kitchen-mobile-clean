# HH-THEORY-010 - The Brackets Principle

**Status:** Candidate
**Category:** Design Philosophy
**Depends on:** HH-THEORY-003 (Understanding), HH-THEORY-004 (Judgement), HH-CON-001 (Constitution)

---

## Origin

This principle emerged naturally during discussions on simplifying the Helping Hand philosophy.

It was not designed as a foundational theory but recognised as an enduring expression of existing Helping Hand principles.

It therefore remains a candidate until validated through continued application.

---

# The Brackets Principle

Helping Hand exists between people.

Like brackets in a sentence, Helping Hand adds value without becoming the subject.

The sentence is about the people.

The brackets simply improve the sentence.

---

## Principle

Helping Hand exists to support the relationship between people, never to replace it.

Everything within Helping Hand exists only to help the people outside the brackets.

```text
People (Helping Hand) People
```

---

## Characteristics

Helping Hand:

- Adds context.
- Improves understanding.
- Assists judgement.
- Encourages reflection.
- Facilitates learning.
- Quietly creates value.

Helping Hand does not:

- Replace people.
- Become the centre of attention.
- Seek authority over people.
- Exist for its own sake.

---

## The Test

If Helping Hand disappeared, people would still exist.

If Helping Hand is present, interactions between people should become better.

If Helping Hand becomes more important than the people it serves, it has failed its purpose.

---

## Design Consequences

Every capability should be tested against one question:

> Does this improve what happens between people?

If the answer is no, it does not belong inside the brackets.

---

## Summary

Helping Hand is not the sentence.

Helping Hand is the brackets.

Everything inside the brackets exists to serve the people outside them.

```text
( )
```

Sometimes the greatest contribution is almost invisible.
