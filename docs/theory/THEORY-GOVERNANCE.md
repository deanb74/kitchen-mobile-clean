# Theory Governance

> Nothing is invented twice. Everything is inherited.

## Purpose

The Theory Library is the intellectual foundation of Helping Hand.

Its purpose is to define the universal concepts from which all Helping Hand philosophy, architecture, engineering and implementation are derived.

Theory is therefore the highest level of the Helping Hand knowledge hierarchy.

---

## Governance Principle

No Philosophy, Architecture, Engineering or Implementation document may knowingly contradict an approved Theory document.

If a contradiction exists, it must be resolved before implementation proceeds.

---

## Hierarchy of Inheritance

```text
Theory
        ↓
Philosophy
        ↓
Architecture
        ↓
Engineering
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Certification
```

Each layer inherits from the one above it.

No layer bypasses another.

---

## Resolving Contradictions

When a lower layer appears to conflict with Theory, the following process applies.

### Step 1

Confirm that the lower-layer document has been interpreted correctly.

Many apparent contradictions are misunderstandings.

---

### Step 2

Review available evidence.

Determine whether the contradiction is supported by observation, experimentation or operational experience.

---

### Step 3

Determine the source of error.

One of three outcomes will normally apply:

- the implementation is incorrect;
- the architecture or philosophy requires revision;
- the theory itself requires refinement.

---

### Step 4

If theory requires refinement:

- update the theory;
- perform cross-validation against every other theory paper;
- review downstream philosophy and architecture;
- update inherited documents where necessary.

Theory must never be changed in isolation.

---

## Theory Certification

A Theory document is considered valid only when it:

- describes a universal concept;
- remains true independently of Helping Hand;
- contains no implementation;
- can be challenged through evidence;
- has been cross-validated against every other Theory document.

---

## Adding New Theory

Before creating a new Theory paper, the following question must be answered:

> Is this a genuinely new universal concept, or is it an application of an existing theory?

If it is an application, it belongs in Philosophy, Architecture or Engineering.

Only genuinely universal concepts belong within the Theory Library.

---

## Stability

Theory is expected to change more slowly than every layer beneath it.

Engineering may evolve frequently.

Architecture may evolve occasionally.

Theory should evolve only when justified by evidence.

---

## Closing Principle

Theory is not protected from change.

It is protected from arbitrary change.

Every refinement strengthens the foundations from which Helping Hand derives its understanding.

Nothing is invented twice.

Everything is inherited.
