# 005 - Theory of Learning

Information is specific.
Learning is generic.

Learning is not remembering.

Learning is the process through which experience improves future judgement.

## It Explains

- Improvement
- Adaptation
- Reflection
- Generalisation
- Capability growth
- Feedback

## Core Idea

Learning emerges when the outcomes of judgement are reflected upon, compared and generalised.

Specific experiences remain specific.

Learning extracts the principles that can improve future judgement across many different situations.

Learning transforms experience into reusable capability.

## Universal Properties

- Learning depends upon experience.
- Learning depends upon reflection.
- Learning depends upon judgement.
- Learning is cumulative.
- Learning generalises beyond individual events.
- Learning improves future capability.
- Learning is continuous.

## Derived Theorems

### First Learning Theorem

Experience alone does not produce learning.

Reflection transforms experience into learning.

### Second Learning Theorem

Repeated experience without reflection produces repetition rather than improvement.

### Third Learning Theorem

Learning improves future judgement beyond the situation from which it was originally derived.

### Fourth Learning Theorem

The value of learning increases as it becomes applicable across a wider range of situations.

## Implications

Learning enables continuous improvement rather than repeated behaviour.

It transforms individual experience into reusable capability.

Learning provides the foundation from which wisdom can emerge.

## Canonical Definition

Learning is the reflective process through which experience improves future judgement by producing reusable capability.
