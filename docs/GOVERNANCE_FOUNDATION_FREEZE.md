# Foundation Freeze v1.0

**Status:** Ratified

**Freeze Declaration:** HH-ARCH-000 v1.0 Foundation Freeze

---

## Purpose

Helping Hand's foundational governance has been established.

The purpose of this freeze is to protect foundational stability while the rest of Helping Hand evolves.

The purpose of the Foundation Freeze is not to prevent change. It is to ensure that foundational change occurs only through evidence, reflection and disciplined governance.

This is not a technical freeze.

It is a governance practice.

HH-ARCH-000 v1.0 marks the point where Helping Hand moves from architecture invention to architecture governance.

Foundation documents should not be continually rewritten during normal development.

Instead, they should be reviewed at planned governance reviews.

Only significant evidence or demonstrable improvement should justify a change to the foundation.

---

## Included Documents

This freeze applies to:

- Constitution
- Constitutional Framework
- Operating Model
- Theory Library
- Theory Governance
- HH-ARCH-000 Helping Hand Organisational Operating Model
- HH-GOV-001 Helping Hand Governance Framework
- HH-GOV-002 Helping Hand Standards Lifecycle
- HH-GOV-003 Helping Hand Ratification Process
- HH-GOV-004 Helping Hand Architecture Review Board
- HH-ARCH-CI-001 Companion Intelligence Architecture
- HH-ARCH-CI-002 Human and People Intelligence (HIPI)
- HH-ARCH-CI-003 Digital Colleague Architecture
- HH-ARCH-CI-004 Professional Intelligence
- HH-ARCH-CI-005 Helping Hand University Architecture

These documents define the foundation of Helping Hand.

They should evolve deliberately, not continuously.

New ideas should first be expressed through Philosophy, Architecture or Engineering.

Only concepts proven to be universal should result in changes to the constitutional or theoretical foundations.

New architectural families should not be created during this freeze unless operational experience identifies a genuine requirement through HH-GOV-001 to HH-GOV-004.

### Working Cadence

Helping Hand now operates through two loops:

Foundation loop, which is slow and evidence-based:

- Constitution
- Constitutional Framework
- Operating Model
- Theory
- Governance

Capability loop, which is fast and experimental:

- Venue Brain
- Professional Brains
- Digital Colleagues
- Runtime
- Hospitality
- Healthcare
- Sport
- Construction

When the fast loop exposes a genuine universal principle, it may graduate into the foundation loop.

---

## Working Practice

New understanding should not immediately change the foundations.

Instead:

Capture the idea.

Continue building.

Review during the next Foundation Review.

Only enduring improvements should be incorporated.

If a proposed change affects the foundation, validate it against evidence, cross-check it against the rest of the theory layer, and confirm that it is genuinely universal before updating the foundation.

Any change to frozen standards SHALL follow the lifecycle in HH-GOV-002 and ratification in HH-GOV-003, with independent review under HH-GOV-004.

---

## Current Priority

The priority is now:

build derived capability from the established foundation,

prove the foundation through the smallest possible useful expression,

and allow future work to evolve through governed learning.

Learning through implementation will naturally inform future architectural reviews.

---

## Closing Principle

Strong foundations allow confident construction.

Helping Hand now builds upon its foundations rather than continually rebuilding them.

The organisation now governs architecture rather than continuously inventing it.

Nothing is invented twice.

Everything is inherited.
