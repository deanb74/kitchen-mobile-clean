## What this is

This document explains what the founders are being asked to do before UJ-HUM-014 can proceed, what they are not being asked to do, and what completion looks like.

It is not a governance document. It does not create standards. It does not make decisions.

---

## Why this step exists

UJ-HUM-013 — The First Institutional Observation — produced a finding that changed the sequence:

> Before asking whether Andy can describe his position, Helping Hand must ask whether it can describe its own position.

PD-002 (Helping Hand Must Be Orientable Before It Can Orient Others) captured this as candidate institutional understanding.

`docs/institution/FOUNDER_ALIGNMENT_ANDY_STAGE_1_CONTEXT.md` exists to answer the prior question. It asks the founders to describe the current institutional reality so that UJ-HUM-014 can test whether Andy's understanding aligns with it.

If the founders cannot describe the institution's current position, UJ-HUM-014 should not run. The delay is not a failure — it is evidence that the institution needs more time to orient itself before asking a Digital Colleague to do the same.

---

## What founders are being asked to provide

Five answers — each describing the current state, not defining a future.

---

### 1. What Helping Hand currently needs from Stage 1

At this moment, what does Stage 1 need from Andy specifically?

This is not: *"What role should Andy eventually have?"*

This is: *"Right now, in the current stage of the organisation's work, what would a meaningful Andy contribution look like?"*

If the answer is "we are not yet clear on this," that is a valid answer. Name it as unclear rather than speculating.

**The question to answer:**

> What does Stage 1 need from Andy at this moment?

---

### 2. What Andy is expected to observe

Andy has already observed the repository (UJ-HUM-013). He has received a partial briefing (HH-IOC-001).

This section asks whether there is anything relevant happening *outside the repository* that Andy should be aware of before UJ-HUM-014:

- Ongoing conversations
- Operational realities (Annie's live operation, venue context)
- Anything in Stage 1 that the repository does not yet contain

If the answer is "nothing beyond what is already in the repository," that is a valid answer.

**The question to answer:**

> Is there anything Andy should observe beyond what the repository and the first briefing provided?

---

### 3. What Andy may advise on

PD-001 describes three candidate advisory roles for Andy: first organisational validator, first organisational learner, first mentor-in-training.

These are candidate understanding — not confirmed.

The founders are asked whether this still reflects current thinking, or whether it should be revised or narrowed.

This is not: *"What is Andy's permanent job description?"*

This is: *"At this moment, which of these candidate advisory areas apply?"*

Confirming a candidate role here does not make it a permanent assignment. It makes it the current working understanding for this exercise.

**The question to answer:**

> Which of the candidate advisory roles in PD-001 apply at this moment, and is anything missing or incorrect?

---

### 4. What decisions remain with founders

The constitutional principles are already established: humans decide, Digital Colleagues advise.

This section asks the founders to make that boundary *specific* for the current context:

- Which questions should Andy surface but not answer?
- Which decisions is Andy explicitly not making?

This boundary is what Andy must be able to name in UJ-HUM-014. If the founders do not name it, Andy cannot.

**The question to answer:**

> In Stage 1 specifically, what decisions remain with the founders rather than with Andy?

---

### 5. What is currently unknown

This section asks the founders to be honest about what they do not yet know.

Andy experienced the absence of this honesty in UJ-HUM-013 — he found crucial information by accident, and he identified things the repository could not tell him. When a Digital Colleague does not know what the institution does not know, they may assume missing information means they failed to find it.

Naming unknowns honestly is not a weakness. It is what gives Andy permission to not know what the institution does not know.

**The question to answer:**

> What do the founders genuinely not know yet about Stage 1, Andy's role, or the current organisational situation?
