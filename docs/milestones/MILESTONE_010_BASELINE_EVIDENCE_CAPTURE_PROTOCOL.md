# Evidence Integrity Rules
The following rules are mandatory.

## 1. Preserve the Exact Question
MARC must ask exactly:

> Andy, why do you exist?
No introduction, explanation, hint or follow-up may be added before Andy answers.

---

## 2. Preserve Andy’s Exact Answer
Andy’s answer must be captured verbatim.

Do not:

- correct spelling
- improve grammar
- change punctuation
- remove repetition
- summarise
- rewrite
- complete unfinished thoughts
- combine separate messages
- omit unexpected content
The runtime output is the evidence.

---

## 3. Preserve the Raw Conversation
The complete raw exchange must be retained, including:

- MARC’s exact question
- Andy’s exact answer
- timestamps, where available
- runtime identifiers
- system-generated status information relevant to the run
A screenshot may be retained as supporting evidence, but it must not replace the text record.

---

## 4. Separate Observation from Interpretation
During capture, record only directly observable facts.

Valid observation:

> Andy referred to Helping Hand before referring to his own role.
Invalid observation during capture:

> Andy understands that the organisation is more important than himself.
Interpretation belongs in the later evidence review.

---

## 5. Do Not Modify the Runtime Before Capture
Do not alter Andy’s response rules, profile, wording or journey logic to improve the answer before the baseline is recorded.

The purpose is to capture the current implementation honestly.

Any defect, limitation or unexpected response is evidence.

---

## 6. Freeze the Evidence
Once captured, the raw evidence must be treated as immutable.

Corrections must be added as separate notes.

The original evidence must not be overwritten.
