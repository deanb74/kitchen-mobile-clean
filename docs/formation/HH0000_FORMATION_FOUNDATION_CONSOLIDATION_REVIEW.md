## 6. Which Documents Protect Against Drift?

### 6.1 Foundation drift

The foundation synthesis and core-principles extraction protect against forgetting the repeated formation distinctions or replacing them with a narrower interpretation.

### 6.2 Interpretation drift

The alignment review protects against treating exploratory or relational language as capability, authority, personhood, or implementation evidence.

### 6.3 Meaning drift during repository growth

The knowledge-protection review protects against turning conceptual relationships into mechanisms, examples into universal rules, support into authority, and implementation ideas into foundation principles.

### 6.4 Context drift

The understanding-preservation review protects against separating a document from its status, classification, uncertainty, boundaries, controlling relationships, evidence state, and conceptual or operational scope.

### 6.5 Register drift

The document register protects against losing the identity, path, version, status, or stated classification of the documents it records. It does not carry the whole meaning of those documents.
