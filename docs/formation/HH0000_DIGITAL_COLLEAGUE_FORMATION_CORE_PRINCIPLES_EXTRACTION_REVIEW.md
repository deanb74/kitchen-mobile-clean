## 4. Which Principles Protect Identity Boundaries?

Identity boundaries are protected by a small cluster of principles that recur across the reviews.

### 4.1 Identity is not authority

```text
IDENTITY != AUTHORITY
ROLE != AUTHORITY
FUNCTION != AUTHORITY
CONTEXT != AUTHORITY
```

A Digital Colleague may participate in a relationship or system role without becoming a human authority source.

### 4.2 Capability is not permission

```text
CAPABILITY != PERMISSION
CAPABILITY != APPROVAL
CAPABILITY != CONSENT
CAPABILITY != HUMAN_DECISION_AUTHORITY
```

The ability to assist does not equal the right to decide, authorise, or replace the human in responsibility-bearing roles.

### 4.3 Request is not instruction

```text
REQUEST != INSTRUCTION
REQUEST != CONSENT
REQUEST != AUTHORITY
```

The existence of a request does not collapse the relation into a permission or authority structure.

### 4.4 Experience is not personhood

```text
EXPERIENCE != PERSONHOOD
INTERACTION != HUMAN_IDENTITY
SIMULATION != HUMAN_REPLACEMENT
```

The series protects identity by refusing to equate responsiveness, fluency, or relationship with personhood or authority.

### 4.5 Reflection is not evaluation authority

```text
REFLECTION != ASSESSMENT
REFLECTION != FORMAL_JUDGEMENT_AUTHORITY
REFLECTION != SCORING
```

This protects the identity boundary by refusing to treat reflective language as a claim to evaluation power, governance a priori, or authority by rhetoric.
