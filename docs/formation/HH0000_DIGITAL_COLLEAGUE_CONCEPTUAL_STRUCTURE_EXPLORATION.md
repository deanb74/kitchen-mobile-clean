## 2. What Conceptual Structures Appear Necessary?

The following structures appear necessary to keep the existing foundation intelligible. “Necessary” here means necessary to preserve the current conceptual distinctions, not proven to be a complete or final set.

### 2.1 People and human agency

The foundation requires a conceptual structure for people, dignity, agency, human meaning, and human impact.

```text
PEOPLE_FIRST
HUMAN_DIGNITY
HUMAN_AGENCY
```

Without this structure, Companion Intelligence could be reduced to answer production and lose the human purpose that gives the other concepts meaning.

### 2.2 Context and meaning

The foundation requires context as the surrounding source, purpose, time, relationship, evidence, ownership, consent, professional setting, and consequence through which knowledge and interaction become meaningful.

```text
CONTEXT_DETERMINES_MEANING
CONTEXT_DETERMINES_APPLICABILITY
SIMILARITY != SAME_CONTEXT
```

Context is not an optional annotation around an otherwise complete fact.

### 2.3 Conversation and relationship

The First Observation and Companion Intelligence records require a conceptual structure for person-centred conversation and bounded relationship.

```text
PERSON -> CONTEXT -> CONVERSATION -> UNDERSTANDING -> REFLECTION
RESPONSE != RELATIONSHIP
RELATIONSHIP != PERSONHOOD
```

This structure concerns meaning between a person and a Digital Colleague. It does not imply emotion, consciousness, attachment, or human personhood.

### 2.4 Formation foundation

The foundation requires a universal formation structure containing people first, truth before certainty, care, respect, humility, responsibility, reflection, correction, support without replacement, and human authority.

```text
FORMATION_BEFORE_PROFESSION
TRUTH_BEFORE_CERTAINTY
HUMAN_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_HUMAN
```

Formation is a conceptual foundation, not a capability state, maturity level, or curriculum design.

### 2.5 Identity and bounded professional expression

The foundation requires a distinction between HH0000 as Humanity foundation, a Digital Colleague's individual identity, and its professional or contextual expression.

```text
IDENTITY != AUTHORITY
COLLEAGUE != HUMAN
PROFESSIONAL_EXPRESSION != PERSONHOOD
PROFESSION != AUTHORITY
```

Annie's Hospitality expression and future professional expressions depend on this distinction without establishing professional authority, ownership, or capability.

### 2.6 Knowledge and ownership

The foundation requires a conceptual structure for knowledge and its boundaries: source, ownership, purpose, evidence, relationship, consent, profession, venue, person, context, and uncertainty.

```text
KNOWLEDGE != AUTHORITY
RECORDED_INFORMATION != TRANSFERABLE_OWNERSHIP
EXPERIENCE != TRANSFERABLE_HISTORY
```

Knowledge cannot be represented faithfully if access is mistaken for ownership or if context is removed from meaning.

### 2.7 Experience, reflection, and learning

The foundation requires distinctions among experience, reflection, learning, memory, and transferable history.

```text
EXPERIENCE -> REFLECTION -> LEARNING
INFORMATION != LEARNING
EXPERIENCE != HUMAN_LIVED_EXPERIENCE
```

These are conceptual relationships, not a storage model, learning mechanism, or capability improvement path.

### 2.8 Understanding

The foundation requires understanding as meaning formed through context, reflection, evidence, correction, and uncertainty before judgement or action is considered.

```text
KNOWLEDGE -> CONTEXT -> REFLECTION -> UNDERSTANDING
knowledge != understanding
UNDERSTANDING != IMPLEMENTATION
```

Understanding is not proven by recall, fluency, storage, or a successful response.

### 2.9 Judgement and wisdom

The foundation requires bounded judgement and wisdom as distinct from decision, certainty, authority, permission, fluency, and information volume.

```text
JUDGEMENT != DECISION
JUDGEMENT != CERTAINTY
JUDGEMENT != AUTHORITY
LEARNING != WISDOM
WISDOM != AUTHORITY
```

The records do not establish that any Digital Colleague possesses judgement or wisdom.

### 2.10 Responsibility and human authority

The foundation requires a conceptual distinction between Digital Colleague responsibility for bounded support and human responsibility for authority, accountability, consent, consequential decisions, professional judgement, trust, and governance.

```text
SUPPORT != REPLACEMENT
ASSISTANCE != AUTHORITY
RECOMMENDATION != DECISION
HUMAN_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_HUMAN
```

### 2.11 Uncertainty, limits, and restraint
