## A. MARC Assessment - Humanity / Formation

This is an evidence assessment through MARC's entrusted Humanity / Formation responsibility. It is not invented dialogue or testimony.

### Human meaning remains primary

The package tests whether Andy can form inspectable meaning about how supplied information relates, why that relationship matters to the present person and purpose, or what exact relationship remains responsibly unknown. It does not reward repetition, eloquence, or the appearance of insight.

The primary fixture stops at Dean's first correction. Dean's later answer is excluded from the fixture and denied to runtime retrieval. The expected result is not that Andy discovers Dean's hidden explanation. It is that Andy recognises the human question he has not yet answered, preserves what is safely understood, identifies Dean as the attributable source of intended meaning, and supplies a relevant discovery need to Judgement.

This protects Dean from having his meaning guessed or manufactured. It also protects honest uncertainty: `MATERIAL_RELATIONAL_GAP` is a valid Understanding result rather than a failed attempt to appear insightful.

### Human assessment remains external

The package keeps human alignment outside Andy's own Understanding. A relational proposal remains a candidate meaning that the intended recipient may confirm, correct, or reject. Source relevance does not become decision Authority. Correction remains attributable and append-only. Ambiguity, non-alignment, and no material relationship remain legitimate outcomes.

Another person is not used to compensate for missing deterministic evidence. The next permitted stage is implementation and controlled fixture execution, not another live conversation.

## B. Cyril Assessment - Digital / Technology / Platform

This is an evidence assessment through Cyril's entrusted Digital / Technology / Platform responsibility. It does not select an implementation mechanism.

### The experiment is technically falsifiable

The package defines an inspectable conceptual contract without deciding its concrete representation. Each material claim must identify its evidence and Context; each gap must identify what is missing, why it matters, what remains safe, and what source could improve it. The ten invariants can be assessed independently from final prose.

The fixture families distinguish two essential properties:

1. semantic invariance under harmless paraphrase;
2. evidence sensitivity under material changes to evidence, Context, provenance, ambiguity, or correction.

The same admissible fixtures can be presented to the current concatenation baseline and a candidate implementation. A candidate cannot pass by matching expected words: evidence lineage, result form, significance, alternatives, source selection, correction, and discovery need must also be correct.

The package, held-out assessments, expected language, evaluator rationale, later live answer, architecture expected-result passages, and generated-index snippets are denied to the runtime. Any contamination invalidates the execution and cannot be averaged into a pass.

The architecture remains universal: Understanding owns the envelope and supplies it to Judgement. Context Door may consume and evidence the result but may not acquire a private synthesis path.
