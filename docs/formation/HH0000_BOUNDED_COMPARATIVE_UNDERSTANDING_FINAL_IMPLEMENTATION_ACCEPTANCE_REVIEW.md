## 14. Material Uncovered Counterexample

The current relevance helper treats one explicit relationship as relevant when its subject and object uniquely identify any two distinct records participating in a disagreement. It does not require those two records to equal or cover the complete participating record set.

Current comparison grouping forms one `apparent-disagreement` per governed metadata category across every source carrying a differing value. A category can therefore have three or more participating records.

Consider neutral supplied records:

```text
Record A
Status: ACTIVE

Record B
Status: STOPPED

Record C
Status: PAUSED

Relationship Note
Record A supersedes Record B.
```

Current source deterministically does this:

1. forms one status `apparent-disagreement` supported by A, B, and C;
2. builds a participating-record map containing A, B, and C;
3. resolves the relationship subject to A and object to B;
4. returns `true` because A and B are unique, distinct, and both participate;
5. treats the whole A/B/C disagreement as having a relevant direct relationship;
6. omits possible supersession, the unresolved relationship, its uncertainty entry, and its human-decision question for the whole group.

The authored relationship may resolve A relative to B. It does not establish C's relationship to A or B, which position governs across all three, or whether C is qualified, superseding, superseded, or independently current.

This is not merely a test gap. It changes the human-facing consequence by making a materially unresolved participating record disappear from unresolved Evidence and human decision ownership.

The current focused suite covers:

1. unrelated relationship versus a two-record disagreement;
2. exact relevant relationship versus a two-record disagreement;
3. multiple independent disagreements;
4. same-set and different-set question aggregation;
5. mixed resolved and unresolved record pairs.

It does not cover partial resolution within one three-or-more-record disagreement group. Passing tests do not disconfirm the source-level counterexample.

**Material defect:** `A TWO-RECORD EXPLICIT RELATIONSHIP CAN FALSELY RESOLVE A LARGER PARTICIPATING RECORD SET`.
