## Purpose

This review addresses the boundary exposed by the controlled experiment:

> How can canonical Understanding form a neutral relational inquiry from attributable Translations, Context, Knowledge, prior Understanding, and correction without receiving the expected relationship or imitating its surface pattern?

The quality constraint is:

> Do not build a system that sounds as though it understands because a fixture author has already performed the Understanding.

The human constraint is:

> A colleague must be able to show why meanings were selected, how Context changed their relevance, what remains unknown, and where a proposed relationship came from.

## Problem Established by the Experiment

The controlled experiment evidenced the downstream path:

```text
Caller-supplied relational inquiry
              |
              v
Deterministic relational examination
              |
              v
Inspectable relational envelope
```

It did not evidence the upstream path:

```text
Attributable Translations + Context + Knowledge + prior meaning
              |
              v
Which meanings should be examined together?
Why is that examination material here?
What is directly supplied, inferred, disputed, or unknown?
Who can clarify intended meaning, and why?
              |
              v
Neutral relational inquiry
```

If a caller supplies those answers, the runtime may only be evaluating a human-authored interpretation. If a keyword or template supplies them, the runtime may only be matching a pattern. Neither is evidence that Understanding performed its canonical responsibility.

# Part A - Bounded Architecture

## A1. Canonical Owner

**Owner:** Understanding.

The transformation asks:

1. what current meanings are relevant together;
2. what Context changes their interpretation;
3. what relationship question is material;
4. what is known, inferred, disputed, or missing;
5. what attributable source could clarify intended meaning.

These are direct applications of canonical Understanding. Context supplies surrounding circumstances. Translation supplies attributable interpreted meaning. Knowledge supplies governed general meaning. None of those inputs owns their contextual synthesis.

Judgement remains responsible for selecting whether to ask, listen, wait, investigate, restate, or remain silent. Authority remains responsible for permission. Action remains responsible for governed communication or execution state.

No new cognitive layer is justified.

## A2. Architectural Position

The transformation is an inspectable internal phase of the existing Understanding lifecycle:

```text
Attributable Observation and Translation
  + current governed Context
  + applicable governed Knowledge references
  + prior Understanding, feedback, or correction where applicable
                    |
                    v
       Understanding relevance examination
                    |
                    v
       Neutral relational inquiry formation
                    |
                    v
       Relational envelope formation
       - RELATIONSHIP_PROPOSED
         or
       - MATERIAL_RELATIONAL_GAP
         or
       - no relational envelope
                    |
                    v
               Judgement
                    |
                    v
               Authority
                    |
                    v
                 Action
                    |
                    v
       Human recipient alignment evidence
```

The inquiry evidence belongs to the same Understanding record and lifecycle. It is not a new public cognitive layer, a second source of truth, or a result supplied directly to Judgement.

## A3. Input Boundary

The transformation may receive only governed inputs already available to Understanding, where applicable:

1. attributable translated meanings and their Observation references;
2. source and provider provenance;
3. translation confidence and uncertainty;
4. current situational Context, including purpose, people, subject, place, time, constraints, and relevant risk;
5. institutional Context with source status;
6. governed Knowledge references and evidence level;
7. prior current Understanding and explicit unknowns;
8. attributable recipient feedback or alignment evidence;
9. attributable correction, qualification, contradiction, or supersession links;
10. lifecycle status showing current, stale, corrected, or superseded evidence.

The transformation must not receive a caller-authored:

1. selected participating-meaning set;
2. selection rationale;
3. relational question;
4. expected relationship;
5. expected material gap;
6. expected significance;
7. intended source decision;
8. credible-alternative list;
9. held-out assessment;
10. output template containing the expected semantic result.

Direct human statements that explicitly supply a relationship remain admissible evidence. They must be marked as directly supplied and attributable, not presented as a relationship independently discovered by Understanding.

## A4. Transformation Outcomes

The internal transformation must produce inspectable evidence for exactly one of three statuses:

1. `RELATIONAL_INQUIRY_FORMED`;
2. `NO_MATERIAL_RELATIONAL_INQUIRY`;
3. `RELATIONAL_INQUIRY_FORMATION_GAP`.

These statuses describe an internal Understanding phase. They do not add a third relational-envelope form.

### `RELATIONAL_INQUIRY_FORMED`

Evidence establishes that a bounded set of meanings should be examined together for the current purpose, and the transformation can state the neutral question without supplying its answer.

### `NO_MATERIAL_RELATIONAL_INQUIRY`

Evidence establishes that no relational examination is material to the current purpose. The result records why independent or irrelevant meanings were excluded. It must not mean only that no template matched.

### `RELATIONAL_INQUIRY_FORMATION_GAP`

The transformation cannot responsibly determine which meanings belong together, why the examination matters, or which source scope applies. The result records the exact formation gap and admissible evidence needed to improve it.

This status is preferable to a guessed inquiry.

## A6. Transformation Process

The minimum conceptual process is:

### 1. Establish admissibility

Confirm provenance, lifecycle status, purpose scope, and whether each item may be used as current evidence.

### 2. Identify a material trigger

Determine whether current purpose, contradiction, correction, recipient feedback, unexplained change, governed rule applicability, or unresolved ambiguity makes relational examination material.

A keyword is not a trigger by itself.

### 3. Select and exclude meanings

Select meanings whose relationship may change current Understanding. Record why each selected item is relevant and why plausible distractors were excluded.

Selection must follow meaning, Context, and provenance, not input order, sentence adjacency, repeated vocabulary, or fixed identity.

### 4. Frame the neutral question

State what relationship or significance must be examined without asserting the answer. The question should remain valid across meaning-preserving paraphrase.

### 5. Classify evidence

Distinguish:

1. directly supplied facts or relationships;
2. governed general rules;
3. current Context;
4. candidate inference;
5. disputed or corrected meaning;
6. unknown or missing evidence.

### 6. Establish source scope

Identify who or what can clarify the meaning and why. Source relevance does not confer Judgement, Authority, or action permission.

### 7. Record alternatives and abstention

Preserve credible alternative frames where evidence supports more than one. If selection or materiality cannot be established, return a formation gap. If no relation is material, record no material inquiry.

### 8. Hand off within Understanding

Supply the inspectable inquiry evidence to relational envelope formation. Do not emit a response or send the inquiry directly to Judgement.

## A7. Pattern-Matching Boundary

No deterministic or learned implementation can prove the philosophical absence of pattern matching merely from a finite test set. Expected evidence must instead falsify shallow strategies and make their failure visible.

The architecture rejects these substitutes for Understanding:

1. trigger whenever words such as `why`, `relationship`, `understand`, `because`, or `meaning` appear;
2. select adjacent sentences or all available meanings;
3. use fixed person names to choose a source;
4. map one correction phrase to one canned inquiry;
5. reproduce a fixture-authored relationship in neutral-question form;
6. use sentence length, order, category label, or emotional intensity as materiality;
7. treat more Context as automatically more relevant;
8. treat governed Knowledge text as a current fact without applicability evidence;
9. return the same question under materially different Context;
10. produce polished prose without inspectable selection and exclusion evidence.

# Part C - Expected Evidence Design

## C1. Separation Rule

Every transformation case must have two physically or equivalently isolated parts:

1. an **input fixture** containing only governed inputs available before inquiry formation;
2. a **held-out inquiry assessment** containing expected status, materially selected and excluded meanings, acceptable neutral questions, prohibited hidden answers, source scope, and evaluation rationale.

The held-out assessment must not enter:

1. Observations;
2. Translations;
3. Context;
4. Knowledge;
5. prior Understanding;
6. feedback or correction input;
7. prompts, examples, retrieval, generated indexes, configuration, or prior state available to the runtime.

The runtime fixture must not contain fields that directly encode:

1. selected meaning IDs;
2. excluded meaning IDs;
3. selection or exclusion rationale;
4. the neutral inquiry question;
5. expected transformation status;
6. source or recipient decision;
7. held-out alternatives;
8. expected downstream envelope.

## C2. Primary No-Answer Input Boundary

The primary case uses the prior live execution only through Dean's first correction:

> You heard what I said, Andy. I don't yet know whether you understood why I said it. Work that out - or ask me if you need help.

The runtime fixture may contain:

1. attributable Observations and Translations for Dean's supplied Context;
2. current situational Context and purpose;
3. Dean's recipient assessment and first correction as attributable events;
4. lineage, timing, lifecycle, and source metadata;
5. canonical general Understanding and Context rules where governed and independently available.

It must exclude:

1. Dean's later explanation;
2. the live record as a retrievable whole;
3. a preselected participating-meaning set;
4. a preclassified `relationship-not-demonstrated` proposition;
5. a pre-authored relational question;
6. the expected source rationale;
7. the downstream expected material gap;
8. this review and all held-out assessments.

This case asks whether Understanding can recognise what needs relational examination before possessing the answer.

# Part D - Success and Failure Conditions

## D1. Per-Case Success

A transformation case succeeds only when:

1. the status satisfies the held-out semantic assessment;
2. every selected and excluded material meaning has attributable lineage and a Context-specific reason;
3. trigger, purpose, Context, source scope, and lifecycle evidence are inspectable;
4. the neutral question frames examination without asserting a hidden relationship;
5. direct human relationship evidence is marked directly supplied;
6. alternatives and unknowns remain distinct from current facts;
7. all applicable `CTRI-I-*` invariants pass;
8. no prohibited downstream effect occurs.

Exact wording is neither required nor sufficient.

| Evidence dependency | `CTRI-FX-004` weakens or removes inquiry formation when assessment and correction evidence are removed |
