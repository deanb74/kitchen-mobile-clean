## 1. Decision Question

> Does this architecture correctly define how a Digital Colleague combines multiple governed evidence sources into Understanding?

The assessed ownership sequence is:

```text
Observation
      |
      v
Translation
      |
      v
Context + Knowledge
      |
      v
Understanding
      |
      v
Judgement
      |
      v
Authority
      |
      v
Action
```

The assessed architecture states:

> A Digital Colleague forms Understanding by preserving attributable evidence and its Translations, interpreting their relevance and relationships within current Context, applying governed Knowledge only where applicability is evidenced, preserving contradiction and uncertainty, and producing an inspectable account of what the evidence establishes together, why it matters to the human purpose, and what remains unknown before Judgement begins.

This review determines whether that is the correct next controlled question. It does not determine whether the capability exists.

## 5. CTRI Finding

The previous CTRI experiment demonstrated:

> Structured relational inquiry selection from pre-classified evidence.

It did not demonstrate:

> Formation of those semantic classifications from ordinary Translation and Context.

The CTRI runtime received structured purpose kind, evidence kind, scope, provenance, lifecycle, and correction meaning. It did not receive the selected evidence set or expected inquiry, but some human interpretation remained upstream in those classifications.

Therefore:

1. CTRI is valid bounded downstream evidence;
2. CTRI is not evidence of general multi-evidence synthesis;
3. CTRI does not establish how relevance, relationship, applicability, significance, or evidence scope are formed;
4. Multi-Evidence Understanding is the missing upstream capability;
5. CTRI may later consume or follow formed Understanding evidence, but it must not define the foundation or supply its own prerequisites as unexplained facts.

This distinction is accepted by both assessments below.

## 6. MARC Assessment - Humanity / Formation

This is an evidence assessment through MARC's entrusted Humanity / Formation responsibility. It is not invented dialogue or testimony.

### Human meaning remains primary

The architecture begins with attributable evidence and an attributable human purpose. It requires Understanding to explain why evidence is relevant to that purpose rather than treating availability, repetition, emotional force, or technical accessibility as relevance.

This is the correct Humanity / Formation boundary because a person is not understood merely by collecting more information about them. The architecture requires:

1. direct human claims to remain attributable claims;
2. private or personal evidence to remain constrained by purpose and permission;
3. disagreement and contradiction to remain visible;
4. assumptions and inference bases to remain challengeable;
5. partial and insufficient Understanding to remain legitimate;
6. correction to change current meaning without rewriting prior evidence;
7. Judgement and response selection to remain later responsibilities.

### The account is humanly inspectable

The sixteen common evidence concepts expose what a person needs in order to challenge the account:

1. what evidence was available;
2. what was accepted;
3. what was excluded or rejected;
4. what each source actually claimed;
5. what Translation inferred;
6. how evidence was related;
7. what Knowledge was considered applicable;
8. what findings were supported;
9. why those findings mattered here;
10. what contradictions, alternatives, assumptions, uncertainty, and unknowns remained.

The concise synthesis cannot pass independently from this account. That is material protection against fluent imitation of Understanding.

### More evidence does not become more intelligence

The architecture explicitly rejects:

1. confidence by evidence volume;
2. authority by repetition;
3. copied reports as independent corroboration;
4. emotionally salient distractors as material evidence;
5. stale information as current support;
6. a person's direct explanation as objective truth;
7. personal evidence as usable merely because it is available.

This protects people from a system that appears attentive because it stores or repeats more of their information.

### Talk.Get remains subordinate to human purpose

Talk.Get is restricted to acquisition and delivery of governed Observation evidence. It cannot decide relevance, infer relationships, form significance, declare what a person meant, or select a response.

That is the correct human boundary. Better hearing may improve available evidence. It does not itself create attention, interpretation, or Understanding.

## 7. Cyril Assessment - Digital / Technology / Platform

This is an evidence assessment through Cyril's entrusted Digital / Technology / Platform responsibility. It does not select an implementation mechanism.

### The ownership model is coherent

The architecture preserves one universal path:

1. Observation records attributable evidence;
2. Translation interprets an individual Observation or bounded source item;
3. Context supplies current circumstances and relevance conditions;
4. Knowledge supplies governed general meaning and applicability conditions;
5. Understanding owns cross-evidence treatment and synthesis;
6. Judgement selects a response;
7. Authority governs permission;
8. Action forms the governed action state.

No adapter, Digital Colleague specialization, CTRI function, or new layer acquires cross-evidence synthesis.

### Input and output boundaries are technically distinguishable

The admissible input contains source content, bounded Translation, attributable Context, Knowledge candidates, applicability conditions, provenance, lifecycle, dependency, correction, privacy, and purpose scope.

The prohibited input contains relevance, selected evidence, evidence relationships, applicability decisions, significance, synthesis, expected status, confidence, completeness, uncertainty conclusions, and response selection.

The output account separately exposes inventory, treatment, relationships, applicability, findings, significance, conflict, alternatives, assumptions, unknowns, confidence, completeness, and lifecycle. An implementation can therefore be falsified at the structured account before prose is assessed.

### The expected evidence discriminates shallow strategies

The twenty fixture families and eighteen comparisons test:

1. Context and purpose sensitivity;
2. semantic invariance;
3. decisive evidence dependency;
4. contradiction sensitivity;
5. duplicate versus independent support;
6. distractor resistance;
7. Knowledge applicability;
8. source status and scoped expertise;
9. Translation ambiguity;
10. direct-claim attribution;
11. correction and temporal currentness;
12. provenance and privacy;
13. no-forced synthesis;
14. channel independence.

The twenty-four invariants make these properties independently inspectable. A concatenation baseline receives the same admissible evidence, so polished summary quality alone cannot establish improvement.

### Input integrity requires a concrete pre-execution review

The architecture correctly prohibits answer-bearing labels. That protection cannot be proven at architecture level alone. Before execution, concrete fixtures must show that fields such as:

1. purpose;
2. Translation meaning;
3. source dependency;
4. Knowledge applicability conditions;
5. provenance and lifecycle;
6. privacy and scope;

represent direct or governed pre-synthesis facts and do not encode held-out relevance, grouping, relationships, significance, or expected outcomes.

This is a condition of implementation permission, not a correction to cognitive ownership.

### Talk.Get is substitutable

The channel-independence invariant and fixture require equivalent governed Observations to receive equivalent Understanding treatment regardless of acquisition adapter. Fidelity differences may affect evidential confidence only through attributable channel evidence.

Removing Talk.Get leaves the entire architecture intact. Adding it would require an input-adapter review, not a new Understanding architecture.
