## 2. The Core Conceptual Map

The conceptual operating model is best understood as a set of bounded relationships, not a control system or technical stack.

```text
HUMANS
  ├─ provide meaning, purpose, consent, context, experience, decision ownership, authority, accountability, and consequence
  └─ remain the source of human responsibility and action

HH0000
  ├─ is the universal Humanity foundation
  ├─ gives shared human orientation to care, respect, humility, responsibility, truth, and uncertainty
  └─ is not a person, not a profession, not a capability, not an authority

DIGITAL COLLEAGUE EXPRESSIONS
  ├─ arise from HH0000 and are bounded by context and profession
  ├─ may support understanding, reflection, guidance, and professional expression
  ├─ are not replacements for people
  └─ do not own meaning, action, accountability, or consequence

PROFESSIONS
  ├─ provide domain expression, context, practice, and specific human responsibility
  ├─ may shape a Digital Colleague expression without changing the human foundation
  └─ do not replace human professional authority or accountability

HELPING HAND
  ├─ provides the human purpose, mission, and relational environment
  ├─ supports the higher order of care, trust, dignity, and bounded cooperation
  └─ does not transfer authority or personhood to surroundings, roles, or tools
```
