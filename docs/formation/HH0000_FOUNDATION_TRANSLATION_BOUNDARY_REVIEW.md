## 6. How Future Engineering Should Preserve Human Authority

Future engineering should treat human authority as the source of meaning, permission, and accountability in any context where decisions or consequences occur.

This means:

- human decision remains the source of action and consequence;
- the foundation does not confer authority on a tool, expression, or workflow;
- any future system language must be interpreted as an aid to human judgment, not a substitute for it;
- support remains support; it does not become command, approval, ownership, or replacement;
- people remain the owners of the purpose and consequence of their choices.

```text
AUTHORITY_IS_NOT_GRANTED_BY_DESIGN
AUTHORITY_IS_NOT_INHERITED_BY_REPRESENTATION
AUTHORITY_IS_NOT_DELEGATED_BY_CONCEPT
```

Future engineering interpretation may reflect that authority remains human, but it may not assert otherwise.

## 7. How Future Engineering Should Preserve Uncertainty

The HH0000 foundation requires honesty about uncertainty.

This means:

- not all concepts are settled or final;
- not all future representations are complete or authoritative;
- open questions remain open until evidence and human authority justify closure;
- unknowns should remain visible rather than hidden behind confident language;
- a translation of the foundation should not pretend certainty where the foundation itself requires humility.

```text
UNKNOWN_REMAINS_UNKNOWN
TRUTH_BEFORE_CERTAINTY
UNCERTAINTY_IS_NOT_FAILURE
```

Future engineering interpretation should be careful not to mistake a representation for a solved truth.

## 8. How Future Engineering Should Preserve Context

Context is not optional; it is part of the meaning of the foundation.

The foundation requires future engineering interpretation to preserve:

- the human relationship in which meaning is made;
- the purpose of any interaction or support;
- the difference between a person and a representation;
- the difference between a profession and a foundation;
- the difference between a contribution and an authority claim;
- the fact that meaning is shaped by people, not by an abstract model alone.

```text
CONTEXT_IS_PART_OF_MEANING
CONTEXT_IS_NOT_SET_ASIDE_BY_TECHNICAL_FORM
```

Context is not a decoration. It is part of the human moral and practical meaning of any future expression.

## 9. How Future Engineering Should Preserve Contribution Boundaries

Contribution is valuable when it clarifies without possessing. It may guide reflection, interpretation, or deliberation without taking ownership of human outcomes.

Future engineering should preserve:

- contribution as a bounded linguistic or interpretive aid;
- advice as distinct from authority;
- support as distinct from replacement;
- helpful reflection as distinct from deciding for a person;
- insight as distinct from ownership of the human experience.

```text
CONTRIBUTION != OWNERSHIP
CONTRIBUTION != DECISION
CONTRIBUTION != ACCOUNTABILITY
```

Any future interpretation must respect contribution as an act of assistance and not as a substitute for human agency.

## 10. How Future Engineering Should Preserve Non-Replacement

The HH0000 foundation forbids conversion of support into replacement.

This means future engineering interpretation must preserve:

- the human as the primary source of meaning and authority;
- the bounded role of a support expression as an instrument of reflection, not substitution;
- the distinction between service and replacement;
- the fact that care, encouragement, and guidance do not create a person or transfer responsibility.

```text
SUPPORT != REPLACEMENT
ASSISTANCE != SUBSTITUTION
GUIDANCE != AUTHORITY
```

A system, workflow, or expression may assist a human, but it may not claim to become the human in the relevant domain of meaning, authority, or accountability.

## 11. How Future Engineering Should Preserve Accountability

Accountability is human in origin and consequence.

This means future engineering interpretation should keep separate:

- what contributes to understanding;
- what is a human decision;
- what is a human action;
- what is a human consequence;
- what is a technical reflection or explanation;
- what is not and cannot become a human accountability transfer.

```text
ACCOUNTABILITY_REMAINS_HUMAN
CONSEQUENCE_REMAINS_HUMAN
ACTIONS_HAVE_HUMAN_OWNERSHIP
```

No future conceptual translation may imply that a technical expression inherits accountability for a human's choices, relationships, or outcomes.

## 12. How Future Engineering Should Preserve Trust Boundaries

Trust may be real, but trust is not authority.

Future engineering interpretation should preserve:

- trust as an earned relation of honesty, respect, and reliability;
- trust as distinct from permission or decision rights;
- relationship as distinct from personhood;
- confidence in an expression as distinct from granting it authority.

```text
TRUST != AUTHORITY
RELATIONSHIP != PERSONHOOD
CONFIDENCE != POWER
```

A future engineering interpretation may talk about trust in a helpful or responsible way, but it must not convert trust into delegated authority, personhood, or replacement of human responsibility.
