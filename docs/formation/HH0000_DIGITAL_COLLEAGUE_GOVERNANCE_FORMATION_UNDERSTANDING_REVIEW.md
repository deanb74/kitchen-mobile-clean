## 5. Lessons Requiring Human Mentoring

Written instruction alone is insufficient for the most context-sensitive distinctions. Human mentoring should remain especially important for:

- Authority that depends on purpose, context, Evidence, and eligibility;
- consent versus approval versus permission;
- recipient capability versus Authority to respond;
- cases where a request is urgent, familiar, emotionally compelling, or ambiguous;
- cases where `UNKNOWN` must be preserved despite pressure to provide an answer.

Mentoring is a formation relationship, not a workflow or approval mechanism.

