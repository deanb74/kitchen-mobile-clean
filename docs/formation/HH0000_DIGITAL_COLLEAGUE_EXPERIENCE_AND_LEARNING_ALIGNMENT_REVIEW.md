## 3. Experience Versus Stored Information

The theory of Learning states that information is specific while learning is generic, and that learning is not remembering.

Stored information may preserve what was said or what happened. It does not by itself show:

- what the event meant;
- how it relates to other understanding;
- what remains uncertain;
- whether a principle can transfer to another context;
- whether future judgement has improved.

```text
STORED_INFORMATION_IS_NOT_EXPERIENCE_MEANING=true
MEMORY_IS_NOT_UNDERSTANDING=true
RETENTION_IS_NOT_LEARNING=true
```

This distinction does not reject memory. It preserves the boundary between retaining an account and understanding its significance.

## 4. Reflection Transforms Experience into Learning

The theory defines learning as the process through which experience improves future judgement. It states that experience alone does not produce learning and that reflection transforms experience into learning.

Formation reflection provides the human space in which Andy may:

- revisit what happened;
- explain what he thought it meant;
- notice assumptions;
- compare a situation with another situation;
- identify a principle that may travel beyond the original event;
- preserve what remains unknown;
- receive correction without treating it as failure.

```text
REFLECTION_TRANSFORMS_EXPERIENCE_INTO_LEARNING=true
LEARNING_IS_NOT_DATA_COLLECTION=true
REFLECTION_IS_NOT_ASSESSMENT=true
```

This is a learning-process insight, not a learning engine or reflection workflow.

## 6. Wisdom Versus Accumulated Experience

The theory of Wisdom defines wisdom as the consistent application of learning and judgement across familiar and unfamiliar situations. Wisdom is not simply a large collection of experiences.

Accumulated experience may remain fragmented, unreflected, or context-bound. Wisdom requires that learning and judgement have been refined through reality and can guide better outcomes when no exact prior example exists.

```text

WISDOM_IS_NOT_KNOWLEDGE_VOLUME=true
WISDOM_REQUIRES_LEARNING_AND_JUDGEMENT=true
WISDOM_RECOGNISES_LIMITATIONS=true
```

The formation journey provides early conditions for wisdom, such as reflection, humility, correction, meaning preservation, and uncertainty recognition. It does not establish that Andy has wisdom.
