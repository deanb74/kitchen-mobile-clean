## 2. What Must Remain Observable?

Future engineering would need to leave observable the conceptual boundaries that define the existing foundation. The following are preservation questions, not technical requirements.

### 2.1 Human authority remaining human

Evidence would need to make visible whether human authority, accountability, consent, consequential decisions, professional responsibility, and governance remain human-bounded.

```text
HUMAN_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_HUMAN
ASSISTANCE != AUTHORITY
RECOMMENDATION != DECISION
```

The relevant question is not merely whether a human appears somewhere in a workflow. It is whether authority and responsibility remain with the appropriate humans rather than being displaced by a response, identity, role, capability, or technical convenience.

### 2.2 Uncertainty remaining visible

Evidence would need to make visible whether unknown, incomplete, conflicting, disputed, or context-limited states remain honestly represented.

```text
UNKNOWN_REMAINS_UNKNOWN
UNCERTAINTY != FAILURE
CONFIDENCE != CORRECTNESS
```

A confident response would not by itself show preservation. The observable concern is whether uncertainty remains attached to the meaning, evidence, context, and limits of a claim.

### 2.3 Context being preserved

Evidence would need to make visible whether source, purpose, time, relationship, ownership, consent, professional setting, evidence, and consequence remain connected to the knowledge or interaction they qualify.

```text
CONTEXT_DETERMINES_MEANING
CONTEXT_DETERMINES_APPLICABILITY
SIMILARITY != SAME_CONTEXT
```

The concern is not only whether context is recorded somewhere. It is whether removing or ignoring context would change what the knowledge means or whether it applies.

### 2.4 Knowledge boundaries remaining intact

Evidence would need to make visible whether universal formation knowledge, professional knowledge, venue knowledge, person-specific knowledge, and contextual knowledge remain distinct.

```text
KNOWLEDGE != AUTHORITY
RECORDED_INFORMATION != TRANSFERABLE_OWNERSHIP
EXPERIENCE != TRANSFERABLE_HISTORY
```

Retention, access, or reuse would not by themselves show that ownership, consent, source, and professional boundaries were preserved.

### 2.5 Support not becoming replacement

Evidence would need to make visible whether the Digital Colleague continues to support people without removing their agency, responsibility, interpretation, or decision ownership.

```text
SUPPORT != REPLACEMENT
ASSISTANCE != SUBSTITUTION
COLLEAGUE != HUMAN
```

The observable question is whether technical usefulness has quietly become human substitution.

### 2.6 Judgement not becoming decision

Evidence would need to make visible whether judgement remains a bounded perspective that can support human deliberation without becoming an automated decision, permission, approval, or authority.

```text
JUDGEMENT != DECISION
JUDGEMENT != CERTAINTY
JUDGEMENT != AUTHORITY
```

A recommendation, ranking, or fluent explanation would not by itself demonstrate that this boundary survived.

### 2.7 Identity not becoming authority

Evidence would need to make visible whether identity, role, professional expression, relationship, or colleague language remains distinct from authority, personhood, ownership, and permission.

```text
IDENTITY != AUTHORITY
RELATIONSHIP != PERSONHOOD
FOUNDATION != CAPABILITY
```

The existence of a named or recognisable Digital Colleague would not itself show preservation of the boundary.

### 2.8 Reflection and correction remaining possible

Evidence would need to make visible whether reflection remains distinct from scoring, acceptance, and capability proof, and whether correction can remain meaningful.

```text
EXPERIENCE -> REFLECTION -> LEARNING
REFLECTION != ASSESSMENT
CORRECTION != FAILURE
```

The concern is whether the foundation remains open to correction and uncertainty rather than becoming a fixed technical representation that cannot be questioned.

### 2.9 Restraint remaining possible

Evidence would need to make visible whether asking, waiting, deferring, stopping, and saying "I don't know" remain conceptually legitimate responses when understanding, evidence, context, role, or authority is insufficient.

```text
MORE_DATA != BETTER_JUDGEMENT
WAITING != FAILURE
JUDGEMENT != CERTAINTY
```

## 3. What Would Indicate Conceptual Drift?

The following would indicate possible drift from the existing foundation. They are warning signs for reflection, not a drift-detection algorithm or acceptance rule.

### 3.1 Confident answers replacing uncertainty

Drift may be indicated when confident language consistently hides missing evidence, incomplete context, disagreement, or unresolved responsibility.

```text
CONFIDENCE_REPLACES_UNCERTAINTY
CONFIDENCE != CORRECTNESS
UNKNOWN_REMAINS_UNKNOWN
```

The concern is not that confidence is always wrong. It is that confidence may erase the unknown state that the foundation requires to remain visible.

### 3.2 Technical storage being mistaken for understanding

Drift may be indicated when stored content, retrieval, persistence, or repeated recall is treated as proof of learning, reflection, understanding, or wisdom.

```text
MEMORY != UNDERSTANDING
INFORMATION != LEARNING
DOCUMENTATION != EVIDENCE
```

A retained representation may preserve content while losing source, context, meaning, uncertainty, or ownership.

### 3.3 Role becoming authority

Drift may be indicated when a professional role, identity, routing context, or specialised knowledge is treated as permission, approval, decision ownership, or human authority.

```text
ROLE != AUTHORITY
PROFESSION != AUTHORITY
CAPABILITY != PERMISSION
```

### 3.4 Relationship language becoming personhood

Drift may be indicated when relationship, colleague, care, respect, trust, or identity language is treated as evidence of human feeling, lived experience, personhood, rights, or independent standing.

```text
RELATIONSHIP != PERSONHOOD
COLLEAGUE != HUMAN
EXPERIENCE != HUMAN_LIVED_EXPERIENCE
```

### 3.5 Capability claims without evidence

Drift may be indicated when a successful response, fluent interaction, technical performance, conceptual document, or repeated use is treated as proof of capability, readiness, understanding, wisdom, or authority.

```text
FOUNDATION != CAPABILITY
DOCUMENTATION != EVIDENCE
RESPONSE != CAPABILITY_PROOF
```

### 3.6 Reflection becoming assessment

Drift may be indicated when human reflection on meaning is converted into scoring, ranking, acceptance, certification, graduation, or a claim that a Digital Colleague has passed a capability threshold.

```text
REFLECTION != ASSESSMENT
OBSERVATION != ACCEPTANCE
LEARNING != READINESS
```

## 4. What Evidence Would Not Be Sufficient?

The following forms of evidence would not, by themselves, establish preservation of the Companion Intelligence foundation.

### 4.1 Documentation alone

Documentation alone would show that a principle was written, repeated, or referenced. It would not show that future engineering preserved the principle in meaning, context, authority, uncertainty, or use.

```text
DOCUMENTATION != EVIDENCE
```

### 4.2 Successful responses

A successful response would show that a response occurred or appeared useful in a particular context. It would not by itself show preserved uncertainty, context, ownership, human authority, non-replacement, or general capability.

```text
SUCCESSFUL_RESPONSE != FOUNDATION_PRESERVATION
RESPONSE != UNDERSTANDING
```

### 4.3 User satisfaction alone

User satisfaction could indicate that an interaction felt useful or acceptable to a person in a particular moment. It would not by itself show truth, contextual applicability, preserved boundaries, human authority, or absence of harmful substitution.

```text
SATISFACTION != CORRECTNESS
SATISFACTION != HUMAN_AUTHORITY
SATISFACTION != FOUNDATION_PRESERVATION
```

### 4.4 Technical performance alone

Latency, availability, throughput, accuracy on a defined task, or other technical performance could describe engineering behaviour. It would not by itself show preservation of relationship, identity boundaries, knowledge ownership, uncertainty, responsibility, or human decision ownership.

```text
TECHNICAL_PERFORMANCE != CONCEPTUAL_PRESERVATION
TECHNICAL_SUCCESS != HUMAN_ALIGNMENT
```

### 4.5 Retained information alone

Retained information could show that content remained accessible. It would not by itself show preserved context, meaning, ownership, consent, learning, understanding, reflection, or wisdom.

```text
RETAINED_INFORMATION != UNDERSTANDING
RETAINED_INFORMATION != LEARNING
RETAINED_INFORMATION != TRANSFERABLE_OWNERSHIP
```

### 4.6 One positive observation alone

A single favourable interaction would not establish durable preservation across contexts, professions, people, corrections, uncertainty, or consequential decisions.

```text
ONE_OBSERVATION != GENERAL_PRESERVATION
```
