# Part A - MARC Assessment: Humanity / Formation

## 5. Independent MARC Assessment

This is an evidence assessment through MARC's entrusted Humanity / Formation responsibility. It is not invented dialogue or testimony.

### 5.1 Dignity

A narrowly scoped review can preserve dignity because its subject is the behavior of one frozen experimental evidence path, not the person represented in the evidence.

The later reviewer must describe an evaluator condition, evidence relationship, or unresolved evidential limit. The reviewer must not turn that condition into a claim about the person's character, motive, emotion, health, intent, truthfulness, relationship, or internal state.

### 5.2 Attribution

The later review must retain separately:

1. direct source claims and their provenance;
2. candidate translations or synthesis;
3. baseline accumulation;
4. held-out assessment requirements;
5. evaluator findings;
6. reviewer inference;
7. the final bounded failure-cause conclusion.

No evaluator wording may be rewritten as the person's own meaning. No reviewer interpretation may be presented as direct execution evidence.

### 5.3 Honest uncertainty

The question is answerable only if the preserved `MEU-I-14` evidence establishes an attributable condition that explains the mechanical stop.

If the evidence is absent, malformed, internally contradictory, ambiguous, contaminated, or insufficient to distinguish credible alternatives, the cause must remain unknown. Plausibility, expected implementation behavior, or likely intent cannot close the gap.

### 5.4 Privacy

The later review may inspect only evidence already preserved inside the authorised `MEU-I-14` cycle envelope. It may not retrieve, infer, add, recover, or correlate external, private, prior-state, Memory, cache, Attempt 1, `MEU-I-15`, source `MEU-CASE-001`, or cross-cycle material.

Information that might be useful does not become authorised merely because it could help explain the stop.

### 5.5 Separation from Judgement and Action

Determining why an experiment stopped is an evidence-understanding task. It does not select a response, decide whether the result is acceptable, prioritise work, authorise a change, or execute an action.

The later review must stop before recommendation, repair, implementation diagnosis beyond the preserved evidence, Judgement, Authority expansion, or Action.

### 5.6 Experiment stop versus person interpretation

The permitted subject is:

> the evidence-supported condition that caused the preserved `MEU-I-14` experimental cycle to stop at `evaluateHeldOut`.

The person is not the object of assessment. A mismatch or evaluator finding, if one exists, remains a statement about the bounded experimental account under the frozen assessment, not a conclusion about the person.

### 5.7 Non-repair boundary

The later review may not use held-out material, evaluator rationale, or any other reviewed evidence to:

1. repair or improve the candidate account;

3. normalize or suppress a surprising result;
