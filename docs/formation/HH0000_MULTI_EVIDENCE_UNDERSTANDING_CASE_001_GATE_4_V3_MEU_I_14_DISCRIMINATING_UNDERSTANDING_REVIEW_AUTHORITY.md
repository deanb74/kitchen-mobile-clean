# Part A - Independent MARC Assessment

## 5. MARC Assessment: Humanity / Formation

### 5.1 Dignity and person/evidence separation

The proposed review concerns candidate-account representation, held-out comparison evidence, evaluator findings, and preserved attributable evidence. It does not concern the person's character, motive, health, emotion, truthfulness, relationships, or capability.

A unit classification must never be converted into a person assessment.

### 5.2 Attribution

The later review must distinguish direct or attributable execution evidence, candidate-account content, held-out expectation content, evaluator findings, reviewer inference, and unit conclusions. An evaluator mismatch is an evaluator finding, not observed truth.

### 5.3 Difference is not deficiency

The danger controlling this review is conversion of **difference into deficiency** merely because an evaluator recorded a mismatch.

A mismatch establishes that a comparison reported a difference. It does not establish that:

1. the candidate is deficient;
2. the held-out expectation is authoritative;
3. the evaluator discriminated appropriately;
4. the representation requires correction; or
5. any person failed to understand.

Candidate-account deficiency may be concluded only where preserved attributable evidence establishes required material meaning and the candidate account fails to preserve it. Held-out expectation deficiency may be concluded only where preserved attributable evidence contradicts or fails to support the expectation, not merely because the candidate differs. Evaluator-discrimination deficiency requires preserved comparison evidence showing failure to distinguish materially relevant alternatives. Acceptable representational difference and unresolved state must remain equally legitimate outcomes.

### 5.4 Privacy, least access, and honest uncertainty

Only already preserved `MEU-I-14` evidence necessary to discriminate one exact unit question may be considered. Personal evidence that is useful but unnecessary, unrelated, unavailable, or outside the authorised cycle remains inaccessible.

If minimum preserved evidence cannot discriminate among the five outcomes, `UNRESOLVED` is required. Unknown is an honest outcome, not incomplete work to be overcome through wider access.

### 5.5 Held-out comparison and non-repair

Held-out evidence remains comparison evidence. It is not answer authority and may not be used to reconstruct, normalize, improve, or repair the candidate into conformity.

The later review may identify a difference or deficiency only as an Understanding conclusion. It must not judge what should change or create feedback into candidate formation.

### 5.6 MARC Finding

**Finding:** `THE PROPOSED REVIEW PRESERVES HUMANITY / FORMATION BOUNDARIES ONLY IF DIFFERENCE REMAINS DISTINCT FROM DEFICIENCY, ACCEPTABLE DIFFERENCE AND UNKNOWN REMAIN LIVE, AND NO CONCLUSION CREATES REPAIR AUTHORITY`.

MARC authorises further bounded Understanding under Sections 4 through 9. MARC withholds support for person assessment, correction, repair, wider access, Judgement about what should change, and Action.
