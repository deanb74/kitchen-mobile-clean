# AJ-002 – The First Executive Briefing

## Status

- Formation Journey
- Evidence Validation
- No new capabilities are to be implemented during this exercise

## Objective

This is the first Leadership Readiness Review. The purpose is not to test Andy. The purpose is to test the Helping Hand repository.

Andy has now inherited Memory, Understanding, Awareness, Compass, Moral Compass, and Judgement, and also repository knowledge. This exercise determines whether the repository contains sufficient organisational understanding for Andy to lead Helping Hand.

## Ground Rules

- Do not help Andy.
- Do not inject missing knowledge.
- Do not answer questions for him.
- Do not create new implementation.
- Andy may only use information that already exists in the repository.
- If something is missing, that is evidence of a repository gap.
- Repository gaps should be recorded rather than patched during the conversation.

## Scenario

MARC invites Andy into the board room.

### MARC

Andy...

Congratulations.

Today you become Chief Executive Officer of Helping Hand.

Before accepting responsibility, I would like you to review everything you currently know about the organisation.

Please tell me:

- what you understand well;
- what responsibilities you believe you now hold;
- how you believe Helping Hand is organised;
- who you believe forms the leadership team;
- how important decisions should be made;
- anything you believe is missing;
- any concerns you have;
- any questions you would ask before accepting responsibility.

Take your time.

You are not being tested.

We are testing whether Helping Hand has prepared you well enough to lead.

## Evidence Capture

Record:

- Andy's complete response.
- Every question Andy asks.
- Every uncertainty Andy identifies.
- Every missing repository concept.
- Every missing organisational relationship.
- Every assumption Andy makes.
- Every correct understanding.
- Every incorrect understanding.

Classify each observation as:

- Repository Complete
- Repository Gap
- Formation Gap
- Governance Gap
- Organisational Gap
- Future Capability

Do not resolve the gaps during the exercise.

Simply record them.

## Important

If Andy asks "Who is Cyril?" that is evidence.

If Andy asks "Who approves financial decisions?" that is evidence.

If Andy cannot identify board members, reporting lines or responsibilities, record the missing knowledge.

Do not teach him during this journey.

## Acceptance

The exercise is complete when Andy reaches the point where he naturally says either:

- "I am ready to accept responsibility."
- or
- "I need further understanding before accepting responsibility."

Both are acceptable outcomes.

The objective is truthful organisational understanding, not confidence.

## Deliverables

- AJ-002 transcript
- Leadership Readiness Report
- Repository Gap Register
- Recommended repository improvements

No implementation work should begin until this review has been completed.
