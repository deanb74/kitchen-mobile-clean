# AJ-008 Reflections

## Reflection on the mentoring conversation

This was not a strong reflective mentoring exchange. Andy was able to restate its earlier recommendation and remain honest about missing evidence, but it did not yet show the deeper reflective capacity that the mentoring format was meant to encourage.

## What the conversation revealed

- Andy can hold a cautious recommendation.
- Andy can remain evidence-led.
- Andy still needs more practice in thinking about why a recommendation is the most important one, what alternatives exist, and how success would be measured.

## Why this matters

The purpose of this conversation was not to produce the perfect recommendation. The purpose was to see whether thoughtful questioning would deepen Andy’s understanding. In this case, the conversation did surface a gap: Andy did not yet turn the mentoring prompts into a richer internal inquiry.

## Judgement

The mentoring conversation was valuable because it exposed a real capacity gap in reflective reasoning. It did not yet produce a meaningful change in Andy’s thinking, but it did reveal a clear formation need: reflective follow-through.

## Formation implication

Helping Hand should continue to train Andy not by giving it more answers, but by helping it ask better questions and reflect more deeply on the answers it already has.
