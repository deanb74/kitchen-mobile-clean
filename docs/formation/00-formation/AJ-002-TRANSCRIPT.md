# AJ-002 Transcript

## Scenario

MARC invites Andy into the board room and asks him to review everything he knows about the organisation before accepting responsibility as Chief Executive Officer.

## Andy Response

The repository-backed runtime responded with:

> “I’m here to help. What would you like to talk about?”

## Interpretation

This response indicates that Andy did not yet use repository knowledge to produce a CEO-level organisational briefing. It did not identify the organisation’s leadership structure, decision model, governance roles, responsibilities, or confidence boundary for accepting executive responsibility.

## Evidence Observations

- Andy acknowledged the invitation.
- Andy did not provide a structured review of the organisation.
- Andy did not identify a leadership model.
- Andy did not identify governance or decision rights.
- Andy did not indicate readiness or hesitation in a meaningful executive sense.

## Classification

- Repository Complete: Andy can respond socially to a leadership prompt.
- Repository Gap: Andy lacks a repository-backed executive briefing path.
- Formation Gap: Andy is not yet able to translate inherited principles into executive understanding.
- Governance Gap: Andy cannot yet identify key governance and decision authority concepts.
- Organisational Gap: Andy cannot yet explain the organisation coherently at leadership level.
