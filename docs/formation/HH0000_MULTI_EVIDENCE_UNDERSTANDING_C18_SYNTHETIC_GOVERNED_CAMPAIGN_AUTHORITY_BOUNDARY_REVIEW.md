## 5. Meaning of `COMPLETED` in Each Context

### 5.1 Synthetic `COMPLETED`

For direct coordinator invocation:

> Synthetic `COMPLETED` means only that the coordinator completed the mechanical scenario supplied to it under its structural rules.

It may establish that the supplied scenario contained values sufficient for the coordinator's fixed continuation and package-completion checks. It does not establish where those values originated.

Synthetic `COMPLETED` does not mean:

1. actual governed execution occurred;
2. C18, C20, C21, candidate, baseline, held-out, or cross-cycle owners actually ran;
3. owner-origin provenance was established beyond the supplied scenario;
4. Gate 4 was invoked, available, revived, or consumed;
5. an attempt identity or authoritative destination existed;
6. the result was preserved or became reviewable governed Evidence;
7. evaluator truth or semantic truth was established;
8. Current Understanding was formed;
9. Judgement selected a response;
10. Action Authority, execution readiness, deployment, publication, certification, or capability exists.

A self-consistent surrogate reaching synthetic `COMPLETED` is therefore not, by itself, a production provenance defect. It becomes a governance violation only if presented, preserved, or relied upon as actual governed campaign Evidence without the required authorised path.

### 5.2 Governed `COMPLETED`

Within an independently authorised `runCase001Campaign` invocation, governed campaign `COMPLETED` means only that:

1. the accepted gate allowed campaign mechanics to begin;
2. the three required cycles were processed in fixed order;
3. each owner-produced cycle reported opaque pass and cycle mechanical `COMPLETED`;
4. each required immutable capture existed;
5. required attributable C18/C20/C21 Evidence was carried;
6. same-cycle associations were complete under package correspondence checks;
7. cross-cycle mechanics completed with opaque pass;
8. campaign C20/C21 Evidence was produced;
9. the resulting package was frozen.

Governed `COMPLETED` still does not mean:

1. semantic truth;
2. candidate or held-out correctness;
3. evaluator correctness or universal evaluator-condition satisfaction;
4. complete or permanent Current Understanding;
5. preservation success;
6. human review or acceptance;
7. Action Authority;
8. execution readiness, deployment, certification, or capability.

Campaign `COMPLETED`, `PRESERVATION_VERIFIED`, evaluator-condition status, later Current Understanding, Judgement, and Authority remain separate.

### 5.3 Governed `STOPPED`

A `STOPPED` package returned through the authorised `runCase001Campaign` path is also governed campaign Evidence. It truthfully records the reached campaign state and need not be converted into `COMPLETED` to become reviewable.

If its exact bytes pass authorised preservation, the result is durably preserved stop Evidence. Preservation success does not turn the stopped campaign into a completed campaign.

## 6. Shared-Type Safety Analysis

### 6.1 Finding

`Case001CampaignEvidence` may safely represent both:

1. synthetic/non-execution coordination output;
2. governed production campaign output;

provided provenance is established externally by the invoking Authority path and exact package handoff.

### 6.2 Why the shared type is safe

The type describes package structure and status. It does not claim to encode origin.

Safety follows because:

1. execution Authority names `runCase001Campaign`, not any structurally compatible object;
2. the production entry binds fixed owner dependencies;
3. preservation Authority admits the exact package returned by the authorised campaign;
4. the receipt links attempt, campaign, Authority, package contract, and destination;
5. separate review Authority governs human interpretation;
6. existing Authority already refuses public shape, naming, caller assertion, or self-consistency as proof of origin.

The same structural type may therefore describe different provenance contexts, just as identical bytes may be test data in one Context and governed Evidence in another. Context and provenance change the permitted claim; they do not change the bytes or structural type.

### 6.3 Confusion risk

The shared type becomes unsafe only if a downstream process:

1. treats any `Case001CampaignEvidence` value as governed by shape alone;
2. accepts direct coordinator output into governed preservation without the separately authorised campaign transition;
3. presents synthetic `COMPLETED` without its synthetic Context;
4. omits the attempt, Authority, entry-point, and preservation lineage required for human reliance.

Those uses are already outside accepted Authority. Preventing all unauthorised or arbitrary in-memory use is not a proportionate production obligation for C18's limited consequence.

### 6.4 Shared-type decision

**Decision:** The shared type is safe under this explicit Authority clarification. No distinct type, marker, brand, signature, token, registry, seal, or provenance service is required by current Understanding.

This does not prohibit a later naming or type clarification if evidence shows actual confusion. It means such a change is not currently required to establish governed origin.

## 8. Missing / Malformed Mechanics Owner Analysis

### 8.1 Provenance and totality are separate

Classifying direct coordinator output as synthetic does not excuse exception-only evidence loss.

The coordinator still promises to coordinate a supplied scenario and produce campaign `COMPLETED` or `STOPPED`. Synthetic Evidence must also truthfully record what the coordinator did. A malformed scenario must not masquerade as completion or disappear into silence where existing stopped Evidence can express the reached state.

### 8.2 Boundary that currently admits runtime outcomes

The current boundary is `coordinateCase001CampaignMechanically` at:

```text
dependencies.runCycle(cycleId)
  -> CampaignCycleOutcome
  -> campaign continuation or STOPPED decision
```

That boundary:

1. accepts caller-supplied `Case001CampaignDependencies`;
2. invokes `runCycle` for each expected cycle;
3. receives runtime values before campaign completion is decided;
4. currently dereferences outcome, evidence, status, and mechanics;
5. owns the existing campaign `STOPPED` decision at the affected cycle.

Therefore, while this injected surface remains exposed, the coordinator is the exact existing runtime-totality owner for absent, malformed, incomplete, or structurally unexpected cycle outcomes.

### 8.3 Required fail-closed consequence

At that boundary, inability to establish any required continuation fact must produce:

1. existing campaign `mechanical.status = "STOPPED"`;
2. existing `stoppedAt = <affected cycle identity>`;
3. no addition of that cycle to `completedCycles`;
4. no later cycle invocation;
5. no cross-cycle evaluation;
6. campaign C20/C21 finalization where safely reachable under the existing design;
7. a frozen returned campaign package rather than exception-only loss.

Required continuation facts include, at minimum:

1. an outcome value capable of being assessed;
2. opaque outcome passing status;
3. cycle Evidence capable of being assessed;
4. cycle mechanical Evidence present with `status === "COMPLETED"`;
5. capture and association requirements already fixed by package Authority.

This uses the existing status and cycle identity. It creates no new failure reason or semantic conclusion.

### 8.4 Evidence retention

The returned stopped package should retain only Evidence actually and safely available at the reached boundary:

1. prior completed cycle Evidence remains unchanged;
2. if an affected cycle Evidence object was actually returned, that exact object remains attributable to the affected cycle and must not be semantically repaired, default-filled, or presented as completed;
3. if no cycle Evidence object was returned, no cycle Evidence may be invented;
4. campaign C20 may preserve that the cycle dependency was invoked and the campaign stopped at that cycle;
5. no missing cycle mechanical record, status, C18 finding, C20 event, C21 finding, capture, association, or rationale may be synthesized.

If a received malformed value cannot safely enter the immutable package under the existing contract, the campaign-level `STOPPED`, affected cycle identity, completed-cycle list, and campaign C20/C21 Evidence remain the minimum attributable facts. A later correction design must not widen the package contract merely to preserve arbitrary invalid values unless separate Authority justifies it.

### 8.5 If the coordinator later becomes internal

This review does not require the coordinator to become internal. If later Authority selects that option, runtime-totality ownership follows the boundary that still admits variable outcomes:

1. if a separate exported synthetic helper accepts caller-supplied outcomes, that helper owns synthetic fail-closed totality;
2. if only the fixed production adapter remains, `runCase001Campaign` and its cycle adapter own truthful conversion of actual `runCase001Experiment` results into campaign continuation or `STOPPED`;
3. no generalized validation of impossible arbitrary objects is required inside owner-total internal functions merely to defend against hypothetical simulation;
4. ordinary cycle fail-closed behavior must remain unchanged.

The rule is:

> Runtime-totality responsibility belongs to the outermost existing boundary that admits the value and claims a closed campaign outcome from it.
