## 3. Analysis 1 - What Is Fact Semantic Meaning?

Fact semantic meaning is the governed assertion, condition, classification, or description that the governed fact itself represents. It answers what the fact means, not where its representation is stored, who owns it, or which other identities it carries.

A governed fact is not defined merely by record layout. Fields can provide components or Evidence for interpretation, but layout alone does not answer which assertion the fact itself makes or what boundary that assertion has.

```text
FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING=GOVERNED_ASSERTION_CONDITION_CLASSIFICATION_OR_DESCRIPTION_REPRESENTED_BY_THE_FACT_ITSELF
RECORD_LAYOUT_ALONE_DEFINES_FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING=false
FACT_MEANING_IS_STORAGE_LOCATION=false
FACT_MEANING_IS_ACCESS_PATH=false
FACT_MEANING_IS_REPRESENTATION_FORM=false
```

## 4. Analysis 2 - Record Identity

A record ID identifies the governed representation. It supports reproducible reference to that representation but does not, by itself, define the semantic assertion or condition the fact means.

```text
RECORD_IDENTITY_IS_FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING=false
RECORD_IDENTITY_IS_FACT_MEANING_EVIDENCE=true
RECORD_IDENTITY_ALONE_DEFINES_FACT_ASSERTION=false
```

## 5. Analysis 3 - Ownership

Ownership answers who is authoritative for a governed fact or who accounts for its governance. Semantic meaning answers what the fact asserts, describes, or classifies. Ownership may be required Evidence for closure, but it is not the meaning itself.

```text
FACT_OWNERSHIP_IS_FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING=false
FACT_OWNERSHIP_IS_FACT_MEANING_EVIDENCE=true
OWNER_IDENTITY_IS_FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING=false
```

## 6. Analysis 4 - Field Values

Fields carry fact information, but semantic meaning cannot be reduced mechanically to the inventory of field values. A record may contain identity values, classifications, links, booleans, module values, or references. Their interpretation depends on the governed definition of the fact kind and the role each field has in that definition.

```text
FIELDS_CARRY_FACT_INFORMATION=true
FIELD_INVENTORY_ALONE_DOES_NOT_DEFINE_FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING=true
FIELD_SLOT_IS_FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING=false
FIELD_VALUE_IS_FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_BY_DEFAULT=false
FIELD_ROLE_AND_INTERPRETATION_REQUIRE_GOVERNED_DEFINITION=true
```

## 7. Analysis 5 - Facts and Other Semantic Subjects

A governed fact may contain, reference, describe, or carry identities of other semantic subjects. That does not make the fact identical to those subjects.

```text
FACT_CONTAINS_OR_REFERENCES_SUBJECT != FACT_IS_THAT_SUBJECT
FACT_CONTAINS_OR_REFERENCES_SUBJECT=true
CONTAINED_IDENTITY_COLLAPSES_INTO_FACT_MEANING=false
REFERENCED_SUBJECT_IDENTITY_COLLAPSES_INTO_FACT_MEANING=false
```

The framework therefore permits one fact to have its own meaning while fields also denote other subjects. No namespace record is assessed.

## 8. Analysis 6 - Fact Meaning Independent of Carried Identities

A fact may have its own semantic meaning independently of identities carried by its fields. Fields may provide Evidence or components of that meaning, while other fields may denote source identities, imported identities, local identities, or linkage subjects. The fact-specific closure must determine which assertion belongs to the fact itself without collapsing those carried meanings into it.

```text
FACT_MAY_HAVE_OWN_SEMANTIC_MEANING_INDEPENDENT_OF_CARRIED_IDENTITIES=true
CARRIED_IDENTITY_ALONE_DEFINES_FACT_MEANING=false
LINKAGE_ALONE_DEFINES_FACT_MEANING=false
FACT_MEANING_AND_CARRIED_SUBJECT_MEANINGS_REMAIN_DISTINCT=true
```

## 9. Analysis 7 - Independently Addressable Fact Meaning

Fact meaning is independently addressable when later governed Evidence can identify and interpret the same fact meaning without borrowing the identity of another subject, storage location, access path, or representation.

The minimum general conditions are:

1. stable governed or governably derived fact identity;
2. a governed definition of the assertion, classification, condition, or description represented by the fact;
3. a distinguishable boundary for the fact meaning;
4. reproducible interpretation before relationship-instance observation;
5. independence from storage location and incidental serialization;
6. distinction from identities merely carried by fields;
7. distinction from linkage, ownership, provenance, and derivation; and
8. no controlling contradiction.

No concrete persistent semantic-subject ID is selected.

```text
FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_REQUIRES_STABLE_GOVERNED_FACT_IDENTITY=true
FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_REQUIRES_GOVERNED_ASSERTION_DEFINITION=true

FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_REQUIRES_REPRODUCIBLE_INTERPRETATION=true
```

## 10. Analysis 8 - Equivalent Representations

When canonical or successor representations are governed as equivalent representations of the same fact, the fact semantic meaning must survive representation change. A representation change does not automatically create a new meaning.

```text
REPRESENTATION_EQUIVALENCE_MAY_PRESERVE_ONE_FACT_MEANING=true
REPRESENTATION_CHANGE_AUTOMATICALLY_CREATES_NEW_FACT_MEANING=false
FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_PERSISTS_ACROSS_GOVERNED_EQUIVALENCE=true
REPRESENTATION_EQUIVALENCE_REQUIRES_GOVERNED_SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE=true
```

Equivalent serialization, field ordering, storage relocation, or canonical-versus-successor form is insufficient by itself; the governed equivalence must preserve the assertion, interpretation, and semantic boundary.

## 11. Analysis 9 - Two Records and One Meaning

The possible treatments are:

### Treatment A - never

Rejected. A single fact meaning may have canonical and consistency representations or a governed successor representation.

### Treatment B - always when values match

Rejected. Equal field values do not prove equal semantic meaning.

### Treatment C - conditionally under governed semantic equivalence

**Selected.** Two governed records may represent the same semantic meaning only when Evidence establishes one governed fact identity or referent, equivalent interpretation, equivalent semantic boundary, and no contradiction.

### Treatment D - unresolved

Not selected. Conditional equivalence resolves the general possibility without applying it to namespace records.

```text
TWO_GOVERNED_RECORDS_MAY_REPRESENT_ONE_FACT_MEANING_CONDITIONALLY=true
FIELD_VALUE_EQUALITY_ALONE_ESTABLISHES_ONE_FACT_MEANING=false
GOVERNED_SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE_REQUIRED_FOR_ONE_MEANING=true
```

## 12. Analysis 10 - More Than One Assertion in One Record

One record may contain several fields supporting one most-specific governed fact meaning. It may also carry references or identities of other subjects. A record must not be treated as one semantic meaning merely because it has one storage object, nor split into one fact per field.

If a record contains multiple independently governed assertions, the governing model must distinguish them as separate facts or provide a specific compound-fact definition. The existing one-fact/one-most-specific-owner discipline remains intact.

```text
ONE_RECORD_MAY_HAVE_MULTIPLE_SUPPORTING_FIELDS_FOR_ONE_FACT=true
ONE_FIELD_EQUALS_ONE_FACT=false
ONE_RECORD_STORAGE_OBJECT_EQUALS_ONE_SEMANTIC_ASSERTION=false
MULTIPLE_INDEPENDENT_ASSERTIONS_REQUIRE_GOVERNED_FACT_DISTINCTION=true
ONE_MOST_SPECIFIC_OWNER_DISCIPLINE_PRESERVED=true
```

## 15. Analysis 13 - Closure Evidence for a Specific Fact

Future fact-specific closure requires the minimum Evidence needed to interpret the candidate itself:

1. exact fact identity and `RecordKind`;
2. a governed semantic definition of that fact kind;
3. exact interpretation of mandatory fields and their roles;
4. distinction between the fact itself and identities or subjects carried or referenced by fields;
5. ownership and provenance treatment sufficient to establish authority and origin without substituting them for meaning;
6. representation-equivalence treatment;
7. semantic-boundary treatment;
8. reproducible interpretation before relationship-instance observation; and
9. non-contradiction.

Not every field must itself be semantic meaning. Each field must instead have a governed interpretation sufficient to establish the fact’s own meaning or its non-semantic supporting role.

## 16. Analysis 14 - Semantic-Meaning States

The minimum faithful state set is:

```text
SEMANTIC_MEANING_DEFINED
SEMANTIC_MEANING_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_DEFINED
UNKNOWN
```

`SEMANTIC_MEANING_DEFINED` means the fact’s own governed meaning is closed by all mandatory Evidence. `SEMANTIC_MEANING_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_DEFINED` means positive Evidence shows that the representation does not itself have an independently defined fact meaning, for example because it is only a carrier, access structure, or reference representation. `UNKNOWN` means available Evidence establishes neither result.

```text
FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_CLASSIFICATION_STATES=SEMANTIC_MEANING_DEFINED_SEMANTIC_MEANING_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_DEFINED_UNKNOWN
UNKNOWN_IS_NOT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_DEFINED=false
MISSING_EVIDENCE_IS_NOT_POSITIVE_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE=true
```

## 17. Analysis 15 - Ordered General Semantic-Meaning Test

A later fact-specific review may apply this ordered test:

1. **fact identity:** identify the governed fact and its `RecordKind`;
2. **fact-kind meaning:** establish the governed definition of what that fact kind can assert, describe, classify, or condition;
3. **field interpretation:** interpret mandatory fields and distinguish fact-semantic components from carried identities, linkage, provenance, derivation, and context;
4. **self-meaning:** establish that the candidate fact itself has a defined semantic assertion rather than merely storing or exposing another referent;
5. **semantic boundary:** distinguish the fact meaning from its owner, representation, storage, access path, and contained subjects;
6. **equivalence:** establish how equivalent representations preserve the same fact meaning;
7. **reproducibility:** derive the interpretation before relationship-instance observation; and
8. **non-contradiction:** confirm no controlling Evidence contradicts the interpretation.

```text
FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_TEST=EXACT_FACT_IDENTITY_AND_RECORDKIND_AND_GOVERNED_FACT_KIND_MEANING_AND_FIELD_INTERPRETATION_AND_SELF_MEANING_AND_SEMANTIC_BOUNDARY_AND_REPRESENTATION_EQUIVALENCE_AND_PREOBSERVATION_REPRODUCIBILITY_AND_NONCONTRADICTION
FACT_SEMANTIC_MEANING_REQUIRES_ALL_CONDITIONS=true
FACT_INFORMATION_PRESENCE_IS_NOT_FACT_MEANING_CLOSURE=true
FACT_MEANING_CLOSURE_IS_NOT_SEMANTIC_SUBJECT_CLOSURE=true
```
