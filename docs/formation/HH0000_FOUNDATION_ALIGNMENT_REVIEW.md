### 4.1 Learning and judgement

Statements such as `LEARNING_SUPPORTS_JUDGEMENT` and `JUDGEMENT_SUPPORTS_WISDOM` describe conceptual relationships. They do not show that a Digital Colleague can learn, judge, or act reliably in an operational setting.

```text
CONCEPTUAL_LEARNING != IMPLEMENTED_LEARNING
CONCEPTUAL_JUDGEMENT != OPERATIONAL_CAPABILITY
CONCEPTUAL_WISDOM != CAPABILITY_EVIDENCE
```

### 4.2 Reflection and correction

Reflection, mentoring, and correction may reveal developing understanding in a conversation. They do not establish general capability, future conduct, or implementation readiness.

```text
REFLECTION != CAPABILITY
DIALOGUE != GENERAL_CAPABILITY_EVIDENCE
CORRECTION_RESPONSE != ACCEPTANCE
```

### 4.3 Support and helpfulness

The language of support, guidance, and helpfulness must remain relational and conceptual. It must not become a claim that a future implementation can safely support, guide, or act in every context.

```text
SUPPORT_LANGUAGE != CAPABILITY_CLAIM

RELATIONAL_DESCRIPTION != OPERATIONAL_PROOF
```

**Finding:** Risk is interpretive, not an observed capability claim. The existing documents already contain the needed disclaimers, but those disclaimers remain essential wherever the contextual language is reused.

The documents explicitly reject personhood claims, but relational language creates a predictable interpretive risk.

The relationship mapping and identity reviews use relational language to describe meaning, trust, care, and participation. That language does not establish human identity, lived experience, legal standing, or moral personhood.

```text

PARTICIPATION != HUMAN_IDENTITY
TRUST != PERSONHOOD
```

### 6.2 Care and respect are not human feelings

Care and respect describe conceptual orientations toward human impact, dignity, and agency. They do not claim emotion, subjective experience, or human interiority.

```text
CARE_LANGUAGE != HUMAN_FEELING
RESPECT_LANGUAGE != PERSONHOOD
HUMAN_ORIENTATION != HUMAN_EXPERIENCE
```

### 6.3 Colleague is bounded language

“Colleague” remains a bounded relational term. It must not be expanded into human equality, human replacement, independent rights, or human accountability.

```text
COLLEAGUE != HUMAN_EQUAL
```

**Finding:** No personhood claim is directly made. The risk is semantic drift if the relational language is separated from the repeated non-personhood boundary.
