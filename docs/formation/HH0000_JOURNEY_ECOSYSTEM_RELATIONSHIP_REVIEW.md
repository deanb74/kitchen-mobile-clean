## 2. Journey-Related Document Types Currently Present

### 2.1 Formation documents

Formation documents describe character, role, relationship, reflection, learning, and the development of understanding before later professional or technical capability.

The HH0000 formation records state boundaries such as:

```text
FORMATION != CAPABILITY
FORMATION != ACCEPTANCE
MENTORING != AUTHORITY
```

The reviewed Andy live-formation preparation document is explicitly `Formation preparation only`. It prepares a live conversation and identifies human context that repository inheritance cannot supply.

**Purpose stated by the documents:** support formation understanding and relationship, not prove a completed capability or create implementation.

### 2.2 Understanding Journey records

The Understanding Journeys README describes these as worked examples of Companion Intelligence reasoning. They demonstrate how information, knowledge, principles, context, and experience connect to form understanding.

The Standard states that a journey should make observation, context, recall, understanding, uncertainty, candidate responses, judgement, response, reflection, outcome, and validation visible.

**Purpose stated by the documents:** demonstrate reasoning and support review, inheritance, and learning.

### 2.3 Validation records

The repository contains documents whose headers state validation purposes or evidence types. For example, one `UJ-HUM-001` document is marked `Foundation Validation` with `Understanding Validation` evidence type.

The Understanding Journeys README also states that journeys are not automatically accepted as organisational learning and should be reviewed, improved, and refined.

**Purpose stated by the documents:** record or support validation of understanding or reasoning within stated scope, without automatically creating acceptance or capability.

### 2.4 Reflection and mentoring records

The formation reflection review defines reflection and mentor dialogue as ways to recognise developing understanding through own-word explanation, changed context, correction, humility, and uncertainty.

It explicitly states:

```text
REFLECTION != ASSESSMENT
MENTORING != AUTHORITY
EVIDENCE != CERTAINTY
```

**Purpose stated by the documents:** support relational learning and reflection without creating an assessment system or authority transfer.

### 2.5 Live conversation preparation records

`HH0000_ANDY_FIRST_LIVE_FORMATION_CONVERSATION.md` is marked `Formation preparation only`. It prepares a live human conversation and identifies current context, human ownership, uncertainty, role expectations, and feedback needs.

**Purpose stated by the document:** prepare a human-led formation conversation, not record its execution or certify its outcome.

### 2.6 Live conversation evidence records

`HH0000_ANDY_FIRST_BOUNDED_LIVE_FORMATION_CONVERSATION_RECORD_001.md` is marked `EXECUTED - PARTLY ALIGNED / INCOMPLETE` and identifies itself as the first bounded live human Context Door execution.

It uses `Conversation ID: HH0000-CONTEXT-DOOR-LIVE-001`, not `UJ-HUM-001`, and explicitly states that it is not proof Andy is formed, not an aligned understanding, and not a capability or authority claim.

**Purpose stated by the document:** preserve bounded live human context and observed execution evidence with explicit limits.

### 2.7 Journey standards and programme guidance

The Understanding Journeys README and Standard describe what Understanding Journeys are and what they should contain. They are guidance documents, not individual journey records.

**Purpose stated by the documents:** define journey purpose and expected structure.

### 2.8 Closure and integrity reviews

`HH0000_FOUNDATION_CLOSURE_RECORD.md` and `HH0000_FOUNDATION_INTEGRITY_CHECK.md` record the current conceptual foundation and check whether its identity, status, boundaries, uncertainty, and evidence distinctions remain visible.

## 9. Drift Risks If Categories Are Collapsed

### 9.1 Formation collapsed into understanding demonstration

A formation document could be read as proof that a reasoning journey occurred, or a reasoning journey could be read as proof of formation.

```text
FORMATION != UNDERSTANDING_DEMONSTRATION
```

### 9.2 Understanding demonstration collapsed into capability

A journey showing a bounded reasoning sequence could be read as proof of general, durable, or operational capability.

```text
REASONING_DEMONSTRATION != GENERAL_CAPABILITY
```

### 9.3 Validation collapsed into acceptance

A validation label or evidence type could be read as acceptance, certification, or completion.

```text
VALIDATION != ACCEPTANCE
```

### 9.4 Live evidence collapsed into general truth

A bounded live conversation could be read as evidence that the same behaviour is reliable in all contexts.

```text
LIVE_EVIDENCE != GENERAL_BEHAVIOUR
```

### 9.5 Preparation collapsed into execution

A prepared conversation could be read as if it occurred, or as if its intended outcome was achieved.

```text
PREPARATION != EXECUTION
```

### 9.6 Reflection collapsed into assessment

Mentoring, correction, or reflection could be read as scoring, judgement, or a formal acceptance decision.

```text
REFLECTION != ASSESSMENT
MENTORING != AUTHORITY
```

### 9.7 Shared identifiers collapsed into canonical equivalence

The two `UJ-HUM-001` records could be treated as one canonical record without an explicit source relationship.
