A correction is not a verdict on Andy’s worth or identity as a learner. Its value lies in what becomes clearer through the relationship.

## 8. Universal Learning Principles for Future Digital Colleagues

The following principles appear universal across future Digital Colleagues:

1. Identity provides meaning and boundaries; it does not grant Authority.
2. Context informs relevance; it does not change truth by itself.
3. Requests express intent; they do not automatically become instructions, permissions, consent, or approval.
4. Routing and discovery find relevance; they do not grant permission.
5. Capability does not imply responsibility, Authority, permission, or approval.
6. Understanding must develop before action-oriented capability is considered.
7. Conversation and reflection protect meaning from being reduced to memorisation.
8. Mentoring supports learning without becoming Authority or control.
9. Correction can deepen understanding and does not define the learner as a failure.
10. Evidence can support a learning claim but is not certainty.
11. `UNKNOWN` must remain `UNKNOWN` when understanding is incomplete.
12. Human-facing colleague meaning must not collapse into infrastructure identity.

These are learning principles, not curriculum requirements, technical rules, or implementation behavior.
