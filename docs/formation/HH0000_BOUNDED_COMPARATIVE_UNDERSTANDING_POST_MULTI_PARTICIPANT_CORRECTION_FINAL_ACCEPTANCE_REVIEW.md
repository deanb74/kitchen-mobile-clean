## 9. Additional Material Counterexample

Fresh source inspection exposed this bounded fixture:

```text
Record A - Status: ACTIVE
Record B - Status: STOPPED
Relationship Note 1 - Record A supersedes Record B.
Relationship Note 2 - Record B supersedes Record A.
```

Direct control flow establishes:

1. the status values form one A/B apparent disagreement;
2. both authored relationships remain independently attributable;
3. A-to-B exactly and uniquely covers the complete participant set `{A, B}`;
4. B-to-A also exactly and uniquely covers the same complete participant set;
5. `directRelationships.some(...)` returns true as soon as either relation passes individual complete-set relevance;
6. current formation performs no conflict, direction, or mutual-consistency check across the complete relationships;
7. possible-supersession, unresolved relationship, uncertainty, and the human-decision question are suppressed;
8. the renderer can therefore show two opposing explicit relationships without labelling their relationship unresolved or returning the conflict to a human.

This is not the accepted two-record converse. The accepted converse contains one exact relationship. This counterexample contains two opposing exact relationships.

It is also not collective-resolution by union. Each relationship independently covers the complete two-record set. The defect is that existence of one individually sufficient relationship is treated as resolution even when another attributable relationship directly opposes it.

**Finding:** `CONTRADICTORY INDIVIDUALLY COMPLETE RELATIONSHIPS CAN FALSELY SUPPRESS THE HUMAN DECISION BOUNDARY`.
