The exact candidate is:

> The sufficiency of Understanding is determined through Judgement within Context and relative to a contemplated consequence. 1. the adequacy of available Understanding;

3. whether further inquiry should occur;
4. whether inquiry may responsibly stop.

## 5. Understanding Relationship

Understanding supplies interpreted meaning, relationships, relevance, and recognised uncertainty. It can be genuine while incomplete and does not require complete certainty.

Understanding does not determine whether it is adequate for a decision. Calling something `sufficient Understanding` is therefore shorthand for a later Judgement about the available Understanding, not a self-assessment performed by Understanding.

The candidate adds useful clarity only if it preserves this boundary. The phrase `sufficiency of Understanding` risks sounding like a property internal to Understanding. `Judgement of whether available Understanding is adequate` is more precise.

## 6. Context Relationship

Context supplies circumstances, relevance, purpose, affected people, risk, timing, and the consequences against which Judgement operates. The same Understanding may support different Judgements in different Contexts without truth changing.

The phrase `contemplated consequence` is too vulnerable to convenient narrowing. A decision-maker could define a small immediate consequence and omit reasonably foreseeable consequences of action, inaction, delay, further inquiry, or effects on others.

The relation therefore needs the course under consideration and its reasonably foreseeable consequences, not merely the consequence a decision-maker chooses to contemplate. Context changes may reopen a prior Judgement without making the earlier bounded Judgement dishonest.

## 7. Judgement Relationship

Judgement is the primary location of the relation. It already evaluates Understanding and uncertainty within Context, balances evidence, consequence, and responsibility, operates within boundaries, recognises Authority, and determines the most appropriate course.

Three Judgements must remain distinct:

1. **Adequacy:** Is available Understanding adequate for the present course and its reasonably foreseeable consequences?
2. **Materiality:** Could an unresolved uncertainty materially alter the appropriate course or those consequences?
3. **Inquiry:** Is seeking further information feasible, proportionate, timely, responsible, and within Authority?

A material uncertainty does not automatically require further Evidence. Evidence may be unavailable, harmful to obtain, too slow, disproportionate, or outside Authority. In those cases the uncertainty remains material and must constrain the Judgement; it does not become non-material merely because inquiry cannot occur.

Judgement may appropriately proceed, defer, abstain, choose a more conservative course, or determine that no further inquiry is presently warranted. This review does not turn those possibilities into procedures.

## 8. Learning Relationship

Learning follows reflection on Judgement and outcomes. A decision to continue inquiry or stop it can later supply Experience for reflection, but neither decision creates Learning automatically.

The clarification must preserve future challenge so that later Evidence, changed Context, or observed outcomes can test and improve Judgement. Permanent closure would impair Learning by preventing reconsideration.

No wording in the clarification should imply that a responsible stop proves the Judgement correct. It establishes only that the inquiry decision was proportionate to what was known, uncertain, possible, and authorised at that time.

## 9. Wisdom Relationship

Wisdom determines which questions should be asked, recognises limitations, and seeks better long-term outcomes. It can improve future decisions about whether further inquiry is worthwhile across unfamiliar Contexts.

The immediate adequacy and inquiry decisions remain Judgement. Wisdom does not become a required precondition for every stop, and responsible stopping is not a new Wisdom stage. Repeated Learning may allow better question selection to mature into Wisdom.

## 10. Trust Relationship

Trust grows through consistent, explainable demonstration of sound Judgement supported by evidence. Appropriate inquiry and appropriate restraint can both contribute to Trust when repeatedly demonstrated.

Trust is weakened by premature closure, unexplained certainty, runaway inquiry, ignored uncertainty, or convenient consequence narrowing. A single responsible stop or one cross-validation review cannot establish Trust.

The clarification is compatible with Trust only if it preserves explicit uncertainty, responsibility, Authority, future challenge, and revision.

## 11. Memory Relationship

Memory selectively preserves what should still matter later and enables continuity between Experience and future Understanding. Responsible stopping must not mean forgetting unresolved uncertainty merely because it is not material to the present course.

What deserves later continuity depends on significance, not exhaustive retention. The clarification does not define Memory policy. It establishes only that a stop must remain open to later relevant Context or evidence; any lower-layer preservation decision must continue to inherit Memory's selective responsibility.

Memory makes later revision possible, but this review creates no Memory record and authorises no automatic retention mechanism.

## 15. Premature-Stopping Risk

The candidate could excuse premature stopping when:

1. the decision-maker defines the consequence too narrowly;
2. serious low-probability effects are treated as immaterial;
3. consequences of inaction, delay, inquiry, or effects on others are omitted;
4. uncertainty is recorded but not allowed to constrain the course;
5. inquiry is stopped because it is inconvenient rather than non-material or disproportionate;
6. disagreement is suppressed;
7. a present stop is treated as permanent closure.

The smallest protections are scope integrity, reasonably foreseeable consequences, explicit uncertainty, materiality reasoning, Authority, responsibility, and openness to changed Context or evidence.

## 16. Endless-Inquiry Risk

The opposite risk arises when every unknown is treated as material or every material uncertainty is treated as an unlimited duty to investigate.

That can create runaway acquisition loops, intrusion, delay, paralysis, duplicated Evidence, and harm caused by inquiry itself. The existence or availability of more information does not prove that obtaining it would improve the Judgement.

Finite inquiry requires an explicit course, a claim boundary, a materiality question, and a stopping condition. Further inquiry is warranted only while it could materially improve the Judgement and remains feasible, proportionate, responsible, timely, and within Authority.

## 17. Consequence-Proportionality Test

Consequence proportionality is necessary but not sufficient protection.

It protects both directions when:

1. the relevant course is explicit;
2. reasonably foreseeable consequences are not conveniently narrowed;
3. action, inaction, delay, and inquiry are all treated as potentially consequential;
4. likelihood, severity, reversibility, affected people, and uncertainty inform materiality without becoming a numeric score;
5. the value and cost of further inquiry are judged within Context;
6. Authority and responsibility remain explicit;
7. changed Context or evidence can reopen the Judgement.

Without those conditions, proportionality language can rationalise either premature stopping or endless inquiry. This review authorises no threshold, score, decision class, or automated rule.

## 18. MARC Independent Finding

MARC asks:

> Could this clarification help a Digital Colleague know when it has understood enough to help responsibly without becoming intrusive, complacent, or falsely certain?

MARC finds that the core relation can help, but the original wording could excuse indifference by allowing a narrow contemplated consequence and by implying that unresolved uncertainty either triggers Evidence or can simply remain within a stop.

Human communication is often incomplete, urgent, changing, and shaped by dignity. Asking more can clarify need, but can also burden, intrude, delay help, or pressure a person to disclose. Not asking can preserve dignity, but can also leave a material need misunderstood. The Digital Colleague must therefore consider consequences of asking and not asking, provide only help justified by the present Understanding, retain uncertainty, respect Authority and autonomy, and revise when the person's Perspective or Context changes.

Good restraint is explained and purpose-led. Indifference avoids the work of testing what remains unknown. A responsible stop must not say `we know enough` without showing enough for what, who may be affected, what uncertainty remains, and why another question is not presently warranted.

**MARC independent finding:** `HUMANITY / FORMATION THE CORE RELATION IS USEFUL, BUT DIGNITY AND RESPONSIBILITY REQUIRE FORESEEABLE-CONSEQUENCE, INQUIRY-COST, AND REVISION QUALIFICATIONS`.

## 19. Cyril Independent Finding

Cyril asks:

> Does this clarification define a universal relationship that future systems can inherit without requiring a new architecture component or turning sufficiency into a hard-coded threshold?

Cyril finds that a refined relation is universally inheritable as a Judgement principle, not as a state machine. The original wording risks a hidden transition of `material unknown -> acquire Evidence` and `non-material unknown -> STOP`, which would be unsafe when Context changes, Evidence is unavailable, inquiry causes harm, or the decision has mixed technical and human consequences.

No hard-coded threshold, confidence score, permanent closed state, decision-class table, feedback loop, or automatic acquisition edge follows. Systems must preserve attributable Understanding, uncertainty, Context, Authority, and the reasoned inquiry decision without pretending that materiality can be reduced to data completeness.

Architecture may later inherit the refined relation, but this review establishes no missing component and authorises no Sufficiency Engine, Responsible Stopping component, Evidence Dependency service, or new capability.

## 20. Combined Outcome

**OUTCOME 3 - CANDIDATE REQUIRES REFINEMENT**

The underlying insight is universal enough to continue: available Understanding can be adequate without being complete; Judgement evaluates adequacy and uncertainty within Context; and inquiry need not continue merely because more information exists.

The current wording is not safe for canonical promotion because:

1. `sufficiency of Understanding` can sound intrinsic to Understanding rather than evaluated by Judgement;
2. `contemplated consequence` can be conveniently narrowed;
3. `requires further Evidence` incorrectly makes material uncertainty an automatic acquisition duty;
4. it omits feasibility, proportionality, urgency, inquiry cost, and Authority;
5. it does not say how material uncertainty constrains Judgement when it cannot be resolved;
6. `responsible stopping` can sound like a preferred or permanent state.

## 21. Exact Clarification Candidate, If Any

The narrower candidate for another review is:

> Judgement evaluates whether available Understanding, including recognised uncertainty, is adequate in Context for the course under consideration and its reasonably foreseeable consequences. Further inquiry is warranted when resolving an uncertainty could materially affect the appropriate course and seeking further evidence is feasible, proportionate, responsible, timely, and within Authority. When material uncertainty cannot responsibly be reduced, it must remain explicit and constrain the Judgement. Judgement may determine that no further inquiry is presently warranted, but that determination does not create permanent closure and must remain open to materially relevant changes in Context or evidence.

This wording is longer than the original because the adversarial tests show that the omitted distinctions are material. A later review may simplify it only if every protection survives.
