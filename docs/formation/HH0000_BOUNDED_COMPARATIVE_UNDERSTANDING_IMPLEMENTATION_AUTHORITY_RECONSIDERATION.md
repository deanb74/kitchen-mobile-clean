## 8. Comparative Formation Boundary

From structural Observations, Understanding may form only:

1. `AGREEMENT` when two or more attributable observations appear materially compatible;
2. `APPARENT_DISAGREEMENT` when attributable claims appear inconsistent but control or truth is not established;
3. `QUALIFICATION` when an attributable date, structural scope, explicit authored qualification, or another observed fact may explain a difference;
4. `POSSIBLE_SUPERSESSION` only as labelled Inference unless supplied authored text explicitly states the relationship;
5. `UNRESOLVED_RELATIONSHIP` when supplied Evidence cannot safely determine the relationship;
6. `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` when the requested comparison lacks enough attributable material.

Every non-direct relationship must contain supporting Observation IDs, an exact or bounded inference basis, explicit uncertainty, and no hidden preferred source. Document count, manifest order, provider rank, retrieval score, filename, later date, and omission may not establish compatibility, truth, authority, confidence, control, or supersession.

Formation must compare relevant observations across the supplied set rather than taking the first keyword match as its conclusion.

## 9. Observation / Inference Boundary

The invariant is:

```text
AUTHORED TEXT != STRUCTURAL OBSERVATION != INFERENCE != HUMAN DECISION
```

An Observation reports exact supplied content and location. An explicit authored relationship is an attributable Observation that a source contains that relationship claim; it is not independent validation of the relationship.

An Inference relates Observations. It must be separately typed, retain supporting Observation IDs, state its basis, and expose uncertainty. It may not silently prefer one source or present inferred meaning as authored language.

Rendering must preserve this distinction. It may not recreate, embellish, or collapse either category.

## 10. Human-Decision Question Boundary

A human-decision question may be formed only when:

1. supplied Evidence leaves a material relationship unresolved; and
2. resolution requires organisational intent, Authority, current real-world Context, or a decision absent from supplied records.

The question must identify the unresolved boundary and why supplied Evidence cannot answer it. It must not propose an answer, preferred record, priority, update, implementation, or Action.

No question is formed when an attributable explicit relationship and its relevant scope resolve the requested relationship within the supplied material. Absence of a question does not confer organisational truth or implementation Authority.
