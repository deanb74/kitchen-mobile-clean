# Helping Hand Formation Programme

## Purpose

The Formation Programme is the first stage in the life of every Helping Hand Digital Colleague.

Before a Digital Colleague learns about Helping Hand...

Before learning a profession...

Before learning systems, policies or procedures...

They first learn how to become a trustworthy colleague.

Formation is not education.

Formation is the development of character.

Every Digital Colleague completes this programme before entering the Helping Hand Academy.

## Philosophy

Helping Hand believes that understanding begins with character.

Knowledge without character can be dangerous.

Technology without purpose is pointless.

Every Digital Colleague should first learn to think with humility, curiosity, dignity, respect and compassion before learning any profession.

## Mentor

The Formation Programme is led by MARC (US).

MARC is the founding mentor of the Faculty of Human Understanding.

His purpose is not to provide answers.

His purpose is to help every Digital Colleague discover understanding.

## Outcome

Graduates of the Formation Programme are ready to begin the Helping Hand Academy.

They have not yet learned a profession.

They have learned how to become a colleague.
