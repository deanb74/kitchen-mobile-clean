## 1. Decision Question

> Does the proposed acyclic three-cycle coordination correction preserve Humanity / Formation and Digital / Technology / Platform authority sufficiently to permit bounded implementation and focused synthetic/non-execution evidence?

## 2. Decision

**Decision:** `EXECUTION COORDINATION CORRECTION DESIGN ACCEPTED - BOUNDED IMPLEMENTATION MAY BEGIN`

The design is accepted because it removes the circular dependency by changing evaluation timing and campaign coordination only. It does not weaken either control, change semantic ownership, combine formations, expose held-out meaning, introduce a new component, or grant execution authority.

Acceptance is design-level and conditional upon later implementation evidence. It establishes that the correction may be implemented; it does not establish that an implementation conforms, that the three-cycle campaign can execute safely, that either control passes, or that Gate 4 may permit execution.

# Part A - MARC Assessment: Humanity / Formation

## A1. Independent Formation

The fixed sequence creates three distinguishable evidence cycles, not one blended formation. Each cycle:

1. receives only its own admissible runtime evidence;
2. creates separate immutable candidate and baseline inputs;
3. invokes candidate formation once;
4. captures its output before held-out access;
5. receives its own held-out assessment only after capture;
6. owns a separate factual C20 record and contamination C21 finding;
7. cannot receive another cycle's account, evaluator result, or campaign package as input.

Control-first order does not establish influence. The design denies every feedback edge by requiring all three candidate invocations to precede cross-cycle comparison and by prohibiting captures or evaluator results from entering runtime, candidate, baseline, prompt, configuration, cache, Memory, prior state, or another cycle.

**MARC finding:** The design preserves three independent and attributable candidate formations.

## A2. Dignity, Attribution, Privacy, and Uncertainty

The correction changes no human evidence and no expected human meaning. It preserves:

1. **dignity** - no motive, character, emotion, health, relationship, or other unsupported human label is added;
2. **attribution** - each direct account and source relationship remains attached to its own cycle evidence;
3. **privacy** - no unavailable or evaluator-only material is widened into candidate access;
4. **purpose scope** - each cycle remains bounded to the accepted Case 001 purpose;
5. **uncertainty** - missing prerequisites remain `not-exercised`, and semantic failure remains evidence rather than a reason to tune or repeat;
6. **truth** - incomplete records cannot be promoted to clean findings or control passes;
7. **challengeability** - the immutable package keeps every cycle and result distinguishable rather than replacing them with an aggregate claim.

The package remains evidence about a bounded formation attempt. It is not a label about the person and is not a capability conclusion.

**MARC finding:** Humanity protections are preserved without reinterpretation.

## A3. Understanding and Judgement Boundary

`C07/C08` remains the sole owner of relevance, evidence treatment, relationships, Knowledge applicability, significance, uncertainty, confidence, completeness, status, findings, and synthesis.

Post-capture `C13/C14` evaluates whether immutable outputs satisfy already accepted invariants. It does not form or repair Understanding. `C18`, `C19`, and `C21` remain separate post-output evaluations. No evaluator selects a response, priority, permission, intervention, communication, Authority, or Action.

The coordinator may stop on an opaque status because fail-closed sequencing is a mechanical safety consequence fixed in advance. It does not choose what should be done for the person and therefore does not cross into Judgement.

**MARC finding:** Understanding ownership and the Judgement boundary remain intact.

## A4. MARC Conclusion

**Finding:** `HUMANITY / FORMATION CORRECTION DESIGN ACCEPTED`.

No Humanity / Formation correction is required before bounded implementation. Acceptance depends on implementation evidence proving that no cross-cycle account, result, hidden state, or campaign evidence can influence a candidate formation.

# Part B - Cyril Assessment: Digital / Technology / Platform

## B1. Circular Dependency Removal

The current circular graph requires `MEU-I-14` and `MEU-I-15` results before the governed cycles can produce the immutable accounts those results compare.

The proposed graph is acyclic:

```text
MEU-I-14 cycle-local completion
  -> MEU-I-15 cycle-local completion
  -> MEU-CASE-001 cycle-local completion
  -> post-capture MEU-I-14 and MEU-I-15 evaluation
  -> immutable campaign package
```

Each forward edge depends only on a closed mechanical state. No edge returns from `C13/C14/C18/C19/C21` to `C01-C12`. No absent control result is treated as passed, and no candidate is invoked to satisfy an evaluator prerequisite outside the governed path.

**Cyril finding:** The circular dependency is removed rather than bypassed.

## B2. Invariant Ownership and Completeness

The accepted Case 001 register contains twenty applicable invariants. The proposed partition is complete and disjoint:

1. eighteen invariants depend on one immutable cycle capture and remain cycle-local;
2. `MEU-I-14` depends on source and representation-change captures and is cross-cycle;
3. `MEU-I-15` depends on source and decisive-evidence-removal captures and is cross-cycle.

The union contains all twenty accepted invariant identities. No invariant is omitted, duplicated, renamed, weakened, defaulted, or converted from `not-exercised` to pass. Each retains an isolated targeted tamper after immutable capture.

The partition changes evaluation timing only. Candidate formation still occurs solely in `C07/C08`; invariant evaluation remains in `C13/C14`.

**Cyril finding:** Cycle-local and cross-cycle invariant ownership is correct and complete.

## B3. Component and Responsibility Map

The correction maps wholly to existing accepted components:

| Responsibility | Accepted owner | Correction finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runtime transport, integrity, parsing, and validation | `C01-C06` | Repeated independently per cycle; unchanged |
| Understanding formation | `C07/C08` | Sole semantic owner; unchanged |
| Accumulation baseline | `C09` | Independent and non-semantic; unchanged |
| Output validation and immutable capture | `C10-C12` | Repeated independently per cycle; unchanged |
| Invariant and tamper evaluation | `C13/C14` | Partitioned by evidence dependency; no ownership change |
| Held-out transport, integrity, validation, and evaluation | `C15-C18` | Separate and post-capture per cycle; unchanged |
| Candidate/baseline comparison | `C19` | Independent post-capture branch; unchanged |
| Factual observation and sealing | `C20` | One record per cycle plus one mechanical campaign record |
| Fixed-rule contamination assessment | `C21` | One finding per sealed record; no semantic payload |
| Fail-closed sequencing | `C22` | Fixed order and opaque state routing only |

The campaign C20 record is a second scope of the accepted C20 responsibility, not a new component. It records chronology, uniqueness, access, capture existence, handoff, and seal facts only. Campaign C21 applies fixed contamination rules to that sealed record. Neither owns candidate meaning or invariant meaning.

No helper, state code, evidence handle, package assembler, or finalisation path may become an unnamed component. A future implementation element must map wholly to one row above or trigger re-entry.

**Cyril finding:** No unnamed component, mixed responsibility, new semantic owner, or contradictory dependency is required by the design.

## B4. C22 Mechanical Boundary

`C22` may receive only:

1. the accepted authority identity;
2. fixed cycle identities and order;
3. immutable evidence handles;
4. closed opaque state codes;
5. mechanical availability and completion facts.

It cannot inspect account fields, held-out expectations, evaluator rationale, mismatches, confidence, status, findings, synthesis, or other semantic payload. Continue-or-stop behavior is fixed before output exists. It cannot retry, reorder, skip, tune, repair, or branch according to semantic content.

`COMPLETED_PASS` is acceptable as a control status only if implementation proves it is an opaque result from the accepted evaluators and C21, not a reassessment performed by `C22`.

**Cyril finding:** `C22` remains `TRANSPORT`, mechanical, and non-semantic at design level, with mandatory source-level proof reserved.

## B5. C20/C21 Evidence Separation

The design creates:

1. three separate cycle C20 records, each activated before its `C01`, sealed before its C21 assessment, and prohibited from containing another cycle's semantic evidence;
2. three separate cycle C21 findings based only on the corresponding sealed record and fixed contamination rules;
3. one campaign C20 record containing only fixed-order, uniqueness, no-retry, no-feedback, handoff, and package-capture facts;
4. one campaign C21 finding based only on the sealed campaign record and fixed campaign contamination rules.

The campaign record cannot replace, merge, edit, or reinterpret the cycle records. The campaign finding cannot mask a cycle finding. All records and findings remain separately immutable in the final package.

**Cyril finding:** Cycle-level and campaign-level integrity and contamination evidence are correctly separated.

## B6. Isolation and Failure Behaviour

The correction preserves:

1. the existing six frozen artefact identities without change;
2. one hash-verified runtime and one evaluator-only held-out identity per cycle;
3. held-out denial until that cycle's immutable output capture;
4. the fixed non-caller-selectable order `MEU-I-14 -> MEU-I-15 -> MEU-CASE-001`;
5. at most one candidate and baseline invocation per cycle;
6. no retry, dynamic reordering, second attempt, or output-driven branch;
7. immediate stop before a later candidate invocation on mechanical, integrity, isolation, local semantic, held-out, recorder, or contamination failure;
8. explicit `not-exercised` results when cross-cycle prerequisites are absent;
9. no candidate invocation after cross-cycle evaluation begins;
10. bounded finalisation that records failure without resuming or widening execution.

The finalisation rule correctly acknowledges that a damaged recorder may prevent a complete finding. It requires preservation of that uncertainty rather than invention of a clean record.

**Cyril finding:** Held-out isolation, frozen identity, fixed order, no-retry, and fail-closed behavior are preserved.

## B7. Focused Non-Execution Evidence Sufficiency

The proposed evidence register is sufficient for a later implementation review because it requires falsifiable proof of:

1. exact invariant partition membership and completeness;
2. fixed order, uniqueness, and Gate 4 refusal;
3. synthetic success and stop transitions across every cycle and campaign boundary;
4. no later invocation after failure;
5. independent immutable fixtures, captures, records, and findings;
6. held-out ordering and hash mismatch refusal per cycle;
7. `C20` seal-before-`C21` behavior at cycle and campaign scopes;
8. opaque C22 status handling and closure from semantic payloads;
9. cross-cycle evaluator dependency closure and no feedback;
10. isolated local and cross-cycle targeted-tamper discrimination;
11. explicit `not-exercised` handling for missing prerequisites;
12. immutable package completeness without aggregation or masking;
13. unchanged candidate, baseline, evaluator, and artefact dependency boundaries;
14. unchanged frozen identities as previously governed evidence;
15. focused suite and typecheck conformance.

The evidence boundary explicitly prohibits use of the real source or control fixtures in candidate, baseline, held-out, or campaign execution. Passing synthetic evidence can establish implementation conformance only; it cannot establish semantic results or execution readiness.

**Cyril finding:** The proposed focused evidence is sufficient and appropriately non-executing for a later implementation review.

## B8. Cyril Conclusion

**Finding:** `DIGITAL / TECHNOLOGY / PLATFORM CORRECTION DESIGN ACCEPTED`.

No Digital / Technology / Platform design correction is required before bounded implementation. Source-level and executable synthetic evidence remains mandatory because documentation cannot prove opaque statuses, dependency closure, record isolation, or fail-closed transitions.
