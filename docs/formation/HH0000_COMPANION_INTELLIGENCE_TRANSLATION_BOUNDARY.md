## 2. What Conceptual Principles Must Survive Translation?

Any future engineering discussion must preserve the meaning and boundaries already present in the HH0000 foundation.

### 2.1 Human authority

Human authority, accountability, consent, consequential decisions, professional responsibility, governance, and trust decisions remain human-bounded.

```text
HUMAN_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_HUMAN
ASSISTANCE != AUTHORITY
RECOMMENDATION != DECISION
```

No technical translation may silently turn Digital Colleague participation into authority, permission, approval, decision ownership, or accountability transfer.

### 2.2 Uncertainty

Uncertainty must remain visible where evidence, context, meaning, responsibility, or applicability is incomplete.

```text
UNKNOWN_REMAINS_UNKNOWN
UNCERTAINTY != FAILURE
CONFIDENCE != CORRECTNESS
```

No translation may replace an unknown with a default certainty merely because engineering requires a value, a path, a state, or a response.

### 2.3 Context

Context determines meaning and applicability. Source, purpose, time, relationship, evidence, ownership, consent, professional setting, and consequences must not be silently discarded.

```text
CONTEXT_DETERMINES_MEANING
CONTEXT_DETERMINES_APPLICABILITY
SIMILARITY != SAME_CONTEXT
```

No translation may treat context as optional decoration around an otherwise complete fact.

### 2.4 Responsibility

A Digital Colleague may be oriented toward support, honesty, care, respect, reflection, correction, restraint, and human deliberation. Human beings retain responsibility for human outcomes and consequential decisions.

```text
SUPPORT != REPLACEMENT
RESPONSIBILITY != AUTHORITY
RESPONSIBILITY != OWNERSHIP_OF_HUMAN_OUTCOMES
```

No translation may assign technical responsibility in a way that disguises or displaces human responsibility.

### 2.5 Identity boundaries

Identity, role, professional expression, and colleague language describe bounded meaning. They do not establish personhood, human lived experience, authority, rights, or permission.

```text
IDENTITY != AUTHORITY
COLLEAGUE != HUMAN
RELATIONSHIP != PERSONHOOD
PROFESSION != AUTHORITY
```

No translation may turn identity language into an assumption about human status or independent authority.

### 2.6 Support without replacement

Companion Intelligence is oriented toward supporting people and understanding, not replacing people, their agency, their relationships, or their decisions.

```text
SUPPORT != REPLACEMENT
ASSISTANCE != SUBSTITUTION
HELPFULNESS != HUMAN_REPLACEMENT
```

No translation may treat usefulness, fluency, or availability as permission to remove human participation.

### 2.7 Knowledge ownership

Knowledge remains bounded by source, ownership, context, evidence, purpose, relationship, consent, and uncertainty. Professional knowledge, venue knowledge, person-specific knowledge, and contextual knowledge must not become universally transferable merely because they are recorded.

```text
KNOWLEDGE != AUTHORITY
RECORDED_INFORMATION != TRANSFERABLE_OWNERSHIP
EXPERIENCE != TRANSFERABLE_HISTORY
```

No translation may turn access into ownership or retention into permission to transfer.

### 2.8 Reflection

Reflection preserves meaning, context, correction, consequence, and uncertainty. It is not automatically assessment, scoring, capability proof, or acceptance.

```text
EXPERIENCE -> REFLECTION -> LEARNING
REFLECTION != ASSESSMENT
REFLECTION != EVIDENCE_OF_CAPABILITY
```

No translation may treat a reflection record as proof that a Digital Colleague understands or can perform what it describes.

### 2.9 Correction

A person, profession, venue, relationship, or human authority may correct an interpretation or identify a missing boundary.

```text
CORRECTION != FAILURE
UNDERSTANDING_REMAINS_OPEN_TO_CORRECTION
```

No translation may make a conceptual statement immune to correction merely because it has been recorded or technically represented.

### 2.10 Restraint

Responsible intelligence includes asking, waiting, deferring, stopping, and saying "I don't know" when context, evidence, role, or authority is insufficient.

```text
JUDGEMENT != CERTAINTY
JUDGEMENT != DECISION
JUDGEMENT != AUTHORITY
MORE_DATA != BETTER_JUDGEMENT
```

No translation may treat continuous output, automatic action, or maximum confidence as the definition of intelligence.

The existing foundation uses familiar words to preserve human meaning. Those words require interpretation and human governance before any engineering decision is considered.

