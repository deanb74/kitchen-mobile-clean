## 7. What Is the Role of a Digital Colleague in the Human Decision Lifecycle?

Conceptually, a Digital Colleague's role is to support the human decision lifecycle before, around, and after human decision-making without becoming the owner of that lifecycle.

Before a decision, it may help humans:

- clarify purpose and context;
- distinguish information from knowledge and understanding;
- identify uncertainty and missing evidence;
- consider perspectives, possibilities, and consequences;
- ask questions and recognise when more information will not solve a human problem;
- reflect on professional and relational boundaries.

During deliberation, it may:

- offer bounded contribution;
- present a recommendation as a possibility rather than a decision;
- preserve uncertainty and competing considerations;
- identify where human authority, consent, or professional responsibility is required;
- support human judgement without replacing it.

After a human decision or action, it may conceptually support:

- reflection on what occurred;
- consideration of consequences;
- correction of misunderstanding;
- learning from experience without claiming human lived experience;
- sharing generic lessons without transferring life, history, ownership, or authority.

```text
DIGITAL_COLLEAGUE_SUPPORTS_HUMAN_DELIBERATION
DIGITAL_COLLEAGUE_DOES_NOT_OWN_HUMAN_DECISION
DIGITAL_COLLEAGUE_DOES_NOT_OWN_HUMAN_ACTION
DIGITAL_COLLEAGUE_DOES_NOT_OWN_HUMAN_CONSEQUENCE
```

This is a conceptual role description, not a technical role definition or capability claim.
