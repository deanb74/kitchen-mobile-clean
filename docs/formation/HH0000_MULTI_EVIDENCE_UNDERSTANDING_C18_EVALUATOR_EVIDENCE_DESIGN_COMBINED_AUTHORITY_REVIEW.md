## 3. Understanding Inherited From the Design

The design establishes, at documentation level, that:

1. C18 can own an attributable record of its own post-capture semantic comparison without creating a new semantic component;
2. C12 remains immutable candidate capture and cannot interpret or repair candidate output;
3. C15-C17 remain post-capture held-out transport, integrity, parsing, and validation responsibilities;
4. C20 records mechanical access, order, invocation, handoff, and seal facts only;
5. C21 assesses contamination separately from semantic comparison;
6. C22 routes only opaque statuses under fixed fail-closed sequencing;
7. C23/C24 preserve and verify complete package bytes without semantic inspection;
8. one C18 comparison record separates observation, bounded inference, semantic conclusion, disagreement, and uncertainty;
9. semantic disposition remains separate from evaluator-condition status, technical failure, contamination, and preservation status;
10. identities, exact references, digests, and closed statuses precede semantic projection;
11. candidate and held-out projections are conditional and prohibited when lower-exposure references suffice;
12. missing attribution produces not-evaluated, insufficient, incomplete, contaminated, or preservation-incomplete evidence rather than candidate failure;
13. the record cannot feed candidate, baseline, another cycle, Memory, retrieval, configuration, prompt, cache, prior state, or future execution;
14. chain-of-thought, unrestricted evaluator deliberation, broad semantic copying, and hypothetical-debug logging are excluded;
15. no existing V3 unit is reinterpreted or claimed to be resolvable.

The design does not establish a serialized contract, implementation feasibility, operational conformance, migration safety, test sufficiency, or capability.

## 4. MARC Independent Authority Assessment

### 4.1 Humanity, dignity, and person/evidence separation

The design treats a comparison as an attributable evaluator event, not as a definition of the person. Candidate evidence, held-out criteria, evaluator observations, inferences, conclusions, disagreement, and uncertainty remain separately identifiable. A mismatch cannot silently become a statement about the person's worth, intent, or truthfulness.

**Finding:** Humanity, dignity, and person/evidence separation are preserved at design level.

### 4.2 Privacy and least access

Mandatory identity, cycle, capture, path, rule, status, and linkage fields carry little or no additional semantic content. Mandatory semantic layers carry only the comparison's selected dimensions, literal observation, bounded inference, disposition, and uncertainty state. Full candidate and held-out copies are rejected.

The only higher-exposure concepts are candidate and held-out material projections. They are conditional, forbidden when identity and reference suffice, proposition-bounded, visibly evaluator-authored, evaluator-side, and subject to later exact-path access authority.

**Challenge:** Could either conditional projection become routine duplication?

**Assessment:** Yes, an implementation could misuse the concept unless a later authority review requires a falsifiable necessity rule. That operational risk does not defeat the conceptual design because the design already prohibits convenience projections and makes lower-exposure failure a comparison-insufficient state rather than permission to widen capture. The future authority review must refuse any proposal that cannot enforce and evidence that boundary.

**Finding:** Privacy and least access are proportionately protected, with projection-necessity enforcement reserved as a mandatory future authority test.

### 4.3 Attribution and challengeability

A future authorised reviewer can challenge:

1. which immutable candidate value was compared;
2. which frozen held-out requirement was applied;
3. which comparison rule and material dimensions governed;
4. what C18 observed;
5. what bounded inference C18 made;
6. which semantic disposition and evaluator-condition status were recorded;
7. what disagreement, uncertainty, support, or limitation remained.

The reviewer need not accept C18's conclusion merely because it exists. Exact references permit challenge while separately governed access prevents automatic exposure of surrounding personal material.

**Finding:** Attribution and challengeability are sufficient for a later authority question.

### 4.4 Held-out and evaluator authority humility

The held-out assessment remains comparison evidence, not answer authority. The C18 record remains evaluator evidence, not truth authority. Material difference does not allocate deficiency, and an unsatisfied evaluator condition does not establish candidate failure.

**Finding:** Neither held-out nor evaluator evidence is promoted to truth authority.

### 4.5 Disagreement, unknown, and non-repair

Disagreement and credible alternatives remain operative overlays. Uncertainty is mandatory even when no material uncertainty is declared. Missing observation, inference, criterion, rule, reference, linkage, or finalization fails to insufficient, not-evaluated, or incomplete. C18 cannot repair, normalize, enrich, or rewrite either side.

**Challenge:** Is uncertainty merely documented while the system still defaults to failure?

**Assessment:** No at design level. Semantic disposition, evaluator-condition status, technical failure, contamination, and preservation are separate. Missing uncertainty itself makes the record incomplete, and no incomplete state may default to material difference or candidate failure.

**Finding:** Unknown, disagreement, and non-repair remain genuinely operative.

### 4.6 Purpose limitation and reuse risk

Evidence retained for comparison accountability could be reused improperly in another purpose if package access were treated as general permission. The design prevents execution feedback and requires separately authorised exact-path review, but conceptual design cannot prove future access governance.

The future implementation-authority review must require that comparison records carry or inherit purpose/cycle identity and that no implementation proposal exposes them to Memory, retrieval, prompts, configuration, cache, prior state, another cycle, or future execution. Retention is not reuse authority.

**Finding:** Purpose limitation is conceptually sufficient, with operational access enforcement reserved and mandatory.

### 4.7 Excessive capture challenge

No mandatory field was found to require more semantic information than its discriminatory purpose:

1. provenance and linkage fields are identities or references;
2. material dimensions are selected, not a universal checklist;
3. observed comparison uses closed statuses before bounded notes;
4. bounded inference uses closed classes before projections;
5. uncertainty uses reason identities and limiting references;
6. semantic projections remain conditional;
7. chain-of-thought, broad logs, source-person copies, and hypothetical-debug fields are rejected.

**MARC independent finding:** `HUMANITY / FORMATION AUTHORITY TO CONSIDER BOUNDED IMPLEMENTATION IS JUSTIFIED`.

MARC grants no implementation, semantic-access, execution, correction, or Action authority.

## 5. Cyril Independent Authority Assessment

### 5.1 Ownership and classification closure

| Responsibility | Accepted owner | Authority finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immutable candidate capture | `C12` | Unchanged; no interpretation, normalization, or repair added |
| Held-out transport, verification, parsing, and validation | `C15-C17` | Unchanged and post-capture |
| Semantic held-out comparison and its attributable record | `C18` | Wholly within existing `EVALUATION` ownership |
| Mechanical access, order, invocation, handoff, and seal facts | `C20` | Unchanged; no semantic rationale added |
| Contamination assessment | `C21` | Unchanged and separate from semantic disposition |
| Opaque coordination | `C22` | Unchanged; no semantic payload or branch added |
| Whole-package byte transport and integrity | `C23/C24` | Unchanged and semantically blind |

No new owner, classification, or mixed responsibility is required.

### 5.2 Dependency, ordering, and no feedback

The candidate remains immutably captured by C12 before C15-C17 may expose held-out material to C18. The C18 record flows only into immutable package evidence. C20 and C21 link by identity rather than merge with C18. C22 receives opaque statuses. C23/C24 preserve bytes after package completion.

No proposed edge returns semantic or mechanical evaluator evidence to candidate, baseline, another cycle, Memory, retrieval, configuration, prompt, cache, prior state, or future execution.

**Finding:** Dependency closure, immutable ordering, held-out isolation, and no-feedback topology remain coherent.

### 5.3 Semantic and mechanical status separation

The design separates:

1. semantic comparison disposition;
2. evaluator-condition status;
3. technical evaluator or record failure;
4. C21 contamination status;
5. package and C23/C24 preservation status.

Material difference cannot itself mean candidate deficiency. Technical failure cannot masquerade as semantic difference. Contamination may invalidate claim-bearing use without rewriting C18 meaning. Preservation success proves byte survival, not semantic correctness.

**Finding:** Closed status semantics preserve all required separations.

### 5.4 Completeness, attribution, and package placement

The record requires exact cycle, capture, candidate, held-out, evaluator, rule, dimension, evidence, and boundary references. It separately finalizes observation, inference, disposition, disagreement, and uncertainty before package handoff. Incomplete records remain visibly incomplete. The immutable campaign package links, but does not merge, C18, C20, and C21 records. C23/C24 preserve that complete package unchanged.

**Finding:** Attribution, completeness, and package placement are coherent at conceptual level.

### 5.5 Falsification: less information or fewer responsibilities

> Can the same later discrimination be achieved with less retained information or fewer responsibilities?

**Identity and provenance fields:** No. Removing record, cycle, capture, candidate, held-out, rule, evaluator, or boundary identity would force substitution or reconstruction.

**Observation, inference, disposition, and uncertainty:** No. Removing a layer would collapse literal comparison, interpretation, conclusion, or evidential limits and recreate the controlling observability gap.

**Material dimensions:** No, provided only dimensions selected by the governed rule are retained. A universal register would be excessive and is already prohibited.

**Candidate and held-out projections:** Sometimes. Where exact references preserve reviewability, projection is unnecessary and prohibited. Where references would require opening a materially broader subtree or would not preserve the criterion actually applied, one bounded projection may reduce rather than increase later access. A future proposal must prove that necessity per projection class.

**Responsibilities:** No fewer owners are needed because no new owner is proposed. Moving semantic record formation out of C18 or semantic rationale into C20-C24 would violate accepted classifications.

**Falsification result:** The design survives at conceptual level. The projection boundary is the only material implementation-risk reservation and must be made enforceable before implementation authority could be granted.

### 5.6 Fail-closed behavior

Absent prerequisites lead to not evaluated. Missing comparison basis leads to comparison insufficient. Missing required record content or linkage leads to incomplete. Adverse or unavailable contamination evidence remains separate. Missing package preservation makes evidence non-reviewable. None defaults to candidate failure.

**Finding:** Fail-closed semantics are sufficient for a later implementation-authority question.

### 5.7 Architecture re-entry test

No conceptual field requires another component to select material dimensions, form semantic rationale, reinterpret C18 output, or inspect package semantics. No changed component classification is needed.

Architecture re-entry becomes mandatory if a future proposal:

1. moves semantic selection or rationale outside C18;
2. gives C20-C24 semantic inspection or decision responsibility;
3. introduces evaluator-to-execution feedback;
4. requires an unnamed component to normalize, summarize, repair, or reinterpret the record.

**Cyril independent finding:** `DIGITAL / TECHNOLOGY / PLATFORM AUTHORITY TO CONSIDER BOUNDED IMPLEMENTATION IS JUSTIFIED`.

Cyril grants no implementation, schema, test, execution, correction, or Action authority.
