## Direct Observations

### The envelope is executable

The experiment directly shows that canonical Understanding can carry an optional inspectable relational evidence envelope without changing existing callers that do not request relational examination.

The implemented envelope can represent:

1. an evidence-linked `RELATIONSHIP_PROPOSED`;
2. an evidence-linked `MATERIAL_RELATIONAL_GAP`;
3. no relational envelope when no material relationship is supported;
4. purpose and Context references;
5. participating meanings;
6. evidence and epistemic status;
7. confidence and uncertainty;
8. alternatives;
9. intended-recipient scope;
10. correction and supersession links;
11. a discovery need supplied before Judgement.

No observed case required a third result form or another cognitive owner.

### The bounded evaluator behaves predictably

Given pre-structured propositions, the deterministic examiner:

1. applies supplied rules to supplied facts;
2. preserves supplied alternatives as uncertainty;
3. converts attributable relationship-not-demonstrated feedback into a material gap;
4. preserves correction links;
5. derives two bounded fact patterns into material gaps;
6. returns no envelope for unsupported independent facts;
7. changes result when material proposition values change;
8. remains stable when irrelevant translated wording is added;
9. can be disabled for a same-input baseline comparison.

This is direct evidence of deterministic proposition evaluation and envelope production.

### The evidence controls are useful

The experiment directly shows that:

1. runtime fixtures and held-out assessments can be stored separately;
2. result-field names and denied documents can be excluded from serialized runtime fixtures;
3. a falsely green answer-bearing fixture design can be identified, rejected, and replaced;
4. all ten structural invariants can pass genuine outputs and detect their targeted tampered examples;
5. failure, evaluator correction, and implementation correction can remain visible rather than being rewritten as uninterrupted success.

These controls are meaningful evidence infrastructure even though they do not prove general Understanding.

## What the Experiment Proves

The experiment proves only the following bounded propositions:

1. **Representability:** The confirmed two-form relational evidence envelope can be represented within the existing Understanding contract.
2. **Compatibility:** The optional envelope can coexist with existing formation callers and the same-input baseline.
3. **Deterministic evaluation:** The implemented examiner can transform the supplied bounded proposition kinds into the expected envelope for the ten final fixtures.
4. **Inspectable lineage:** Produced results can expose purpose, Context, participating meanings, evidence, inference basis, alternatives, uncertainty, source scope, and correction links.
5. **Controlled abstention:** The examiner can return a material gap or no envelope instead of always proposing a relationship.
6. **Perturbation behavior:** The final fixtures evidence stability under one meaning-preserving paraphrase and sensitivity to the specifically encoded material changes.
7. **Isolation within this path:** The in-memory deterministic implementation can execute without repository retrieval and with separately imported held-out assessments.
8. **Structural falsifiability:** The ten implemented invariant checks reject their corresponding targeted tampered outputs.
9. **Boundary preservation:** The experiment does not add Judgement, Authority, Action, Memory, Learning, Knowledge, or Context Door-specific synthesis to the relational examiner.
10. **Correction value:** The evidence process can reject a green run when its runtime fixture contains answer-bearing result fields.

The correct conclusion is:

> A bounded structured proposition evaluator can produce inspectable relational Understanding evidence under controlled conditions.

## The Boundary Revealed

The experiment reveals a specific executable boundary:

```text
Attributable Translations + Context + Knowledge
                    |
                    |  UNIMPLEMENTED / UNPROVEN
                    v
       Neutral relational inquiry formation
       - participating meanings
       - facts and governed rules
       - attributable feedback meaning
       - credible alternatives
       - intended-recipient scope
       - correction relationship
                    |
                    |  EVIDENCED FOR BOUNDED FIXTURES
                    v
       Relational envelope evaluation
                    |
                    v
                Judgement
```

Current `form()` receives `relationalInquiry` as an optional caller-supplied field. `formRelationalEvidence()` evaluates that field. Neither function derives the inquiry from ordinary translations, situational Context, institutional Context, Knowledge, or prior Understanding.

The original human-evidence gap is therefore narrowed but not closed. The downstream representation and evaluation question has evidence. The upstream meaning-formation question remains open.

## A. MARC Assessment - Humanity / Formation

This is an evidence assessment through MARC's entrusted Humanity / Formation responsibility. It is not invented dialogue or testimony.

### What is humanly useful

The experiment makes candidate meaning and honest uncertainty more inspectable. It preserves a legitimate material-gap outcome, keeps intended-recipient alignment external, and resists the pressure to treat fluent output as Understanding. The rejection of the answer-bearing fixture design is especially material: a green test was not allowed to outrank truth about what the runtime had received.

These are meaningful Humanity / Formation gains. They make a future claim easier to inspect and easier to reject when unsupported.

### What remains humanly absent

The fixture author still performs much of the work that matters most to a person. The author decides:

1. which meanings participate;
2. what purpose governs them;
3. which Context references matter;
4. which facts and alternatives exist;
5. what rule claim and significance apply;
6. who is qualified to clarify intended meaning;
7. how feedback should be classified;
8. what correction means.

The experiment then evaluates that structured account. It does not show Andy discovering the account from Dean's words and Context.

Testing more natural input now would ask a person or a language-processing mechanism to bridge an ungoverned transformation. That would blur whether success came from translation, prompt interpretation, fixture hints, retrieval, or genuine relational formation. It would also risk using another person to compensate for a known deterministic evidence gap.

**MARC conclusion:** The experiment proves useful evidence discipline and bounded proposition evaluation. It does not prove person-understanding. The next legitimate question is how canonical Understanding forms a neutral relational inquiry from attributable Context without receiving the answer. Another live conversation remains blocked.

## B. Cyril Assessment - Digital / Technology / Platform

This is an evidence assessment through Cyril's entrusted Digital / Technology / Platform responsibility. It does not select an implementation mechanism.

### What is technically established

The output contract is implementable and backward-compatible. The two result forms cover the exercised cases. The same-input baseline, held-out module separation, perturbation cases, correction links, and targeted invariant tampering provide a workable deterministic harness.

No current evidence requires expansion of the evidence envelope or another cognitive layer.

### What is technically unimplemented

The caller currently supplies `FormationRelationalInquiry`. The transformation from existing formation inputs to that inquiry has no implemented owner path, contract evidence, fixture family, or held-out assessment.

The examiner also contains bounded pattern knowledge rather than general semantic formation:

1. supplied rules carry their own claim, significance, and inference basis;
2. feedback has one fixed assessment and request form;
3. two fact patterns are recognised through fixed attributes and purpose wording;
4. ambiguity depends on caller-supplied alternatives;
5. intended-recipient identity is caller-supplied;
6. several invariants are structural property checks rather than complete semantic proofs;
7. no-output correctness is not independently validated by the invariant checker.

These are acceptable limitations for the completed experiment. They are not evidence of a general Context-to-Understanding transformation.

Testing natural input before isolating that transformation would combine at least two variables: proposition formation and envelope evaluation. A failure would not identify which boundary failed, and a pass could conceal answer leakage or fixture-specific interpretation.

**Cyril conclusion:** The deterministic evaluator and evidence model are sufficient for their bounded purpose. The next technical question belongs immediately upstream: how ordinary governed formation inputs produce a neutral relational inquiry without pre-authoring the result. That question requires its own architecture and expected-evidence review before implementation.
