## Direct Assessment of the Question

### The question is correctly upstream

The controlled experiment showed that a deterministic mechanism can evaluate caller-supplied relational propositions and represent its result in the confirmed Understanding envelope.

It did not show how Understanding decides:

1. which attributable meanings belong in the examination;
2. which apparently relevant meanings should be excluded;
3. why their relationship is material to the current human purpose;
4. what is direct, inferred, disputed, stale, or unknown;
5. which source scope follows from provenance;
6. whether to form an inquiry, record no material inquiry, or preserve a formation gap.

The CTRI architecture places exactly those questions immediately upstream of relational-envelope evaluation. It therefore addresses the unresolved responsibility rather than expanding an already adequate result representation.

### The question is correctly owned

Canonical Understanding asks what knowledge means, how it relates, why it matters, and what remains uncertain. Context governs interpretation and relevance but does not own synthesis. Judgement receives formed Understanding and selects a response.

The CTRI transformation is therefore correctly defined as an inspectable internal phase of Understanding. It does not require:

1. a new cognitive layer;
2. Context to become a reasoning owner;
3. Judgement to invent missing meaning;
4. Context Door to acquire a private synthesis path;
5. a caller to author the inquiry.

### The question is correctly neutral

The architecture does not ask whether the runtime can reproduce Dean's later answer. It asks whether the runtime can recognise, from admissible evidence available before that answer, what relationship requires examination and why.

That distinction is material. A neutral inquiry may be correct while its answer remains unknown. Conversely, an answer-like sentence may be fluent and still provide no evidence that the system selected the right meanings or understood why they matter.

### The question is correctly falsifiable

The architecture does not claim that finite tests can prove the absence of pattern matching. It instead defines comparisons capable of falsifying shallow substitutes:

1. meaning-preserving paraphrase and reorder;
2. purpose change with words preserved;
3. material evidence removal;
4. fluent and emotional distractors;
5. keyword lures;
6. the same correction phrase under different Context;
7. source and recipient permutation;
8. direct relationship evidence;
9. credible ambiguity;
10. correction and supersession;
11. independent facts;
12. uncertain rule applicability;
13. stale evidence;
14. missing provenance.

These comparisons test whether selection changes with evidence and Context while remaining stable under irrelevant wording changes. That is the right evidential distinction between a bounded meaning-formation claim and better pattern completion.

## A. MARC Assessment - Humanity / Formation

This is an evidence assessment through MARC's entrusted Humanity / Formation responsibility. It is not invented dialogue or testimony.

### Human meaning remains the subject

The architecture begins with the person's current purpose, attributable meaning, correction, and source scope. It does not begin with a technical trigger or a desired output. The primary case ends before Dean's later explanation and requires the runtime to identify the question without possessing his answer.

This protects a person's meaning in three ways:

1. directly supplied meaning remains attributable rather than becoming machine discovery;
2. intended meaning is not guessed merely to produce an impressive account;
3. a formation gap remains preferable to invented relational confidence.

### Inquiry does not become interrogation

The architecture forms internal Understanding evidence. It does not decide to ask a person a question. Judgement, Authority, and Action retain responsibility for whether any discovery need is communicated.

This distinction protects human agency. Identifying Dean or another person as the relevant source does not grant that source broader Authority and does not grant Andy permission to continue a conversation.

### Human inspection is stronger than fluent resemblance

The minimum evidence requires selected and excluded meanings, material Context, direct and inferred status, alternatives, source scope, uncertainty, and correction lineage. A human assessor can therefore reject a persuasive question whose evidence path is wrong.

The keyword, distractor, purpose-change, and same-phrase/different-Context cases specifically prevent familiar wording from standing in for attention to the person.

### MARC blocker assessment

| Humanity / Formation blocker | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| The expected human relationship is supplied to runtime | Not permitted; Dean's later answer and held-out assessment are denied |
| The architecture rewards a plausible question without evidence | Not permitted; selection, exclusion, purpose, Context, provenance, and uncertainty are assessed together |
| Honest uncertainty is treated as failure | Not present; no-material-inquiry and formation-gap outcomes are legitimate and inspectable |
| Source relevance becomes permission to question or act | Not present; response selection remains outside Understanding |
| A human secretly performs inquiry formation for the runtime | Not permitted; caller-authored selection, rationale, question, alternatives, and source decision are prohibited |
| Correction or disagreement is erased | Not permitted; append-only correction and supersession are invariant requirements |
| Better wording can substitute for better Understanding | Not permitted; paired semantic and evidence perturbations control acceptance |

**MARC conclusion:** The architecture asks the right human question. It examines whether Andy can notice what he does not yet understand about a person's supplied meanings without guessing that person's answer or using the person to cover an unevidenced system boundary. No material Humanity / Formation blocker remains for a bounded implementation experiment.

## B. Cyril Assessment - Digital / Technology / Platform

This is an evidence assessment through Cyril's entrusted Digital / Technology / Platform responsibility. It does not select an implementation mechanism.

### The responsibility boundary is technically coherent

The transformation begins with governed inputs already admissible to Understanding and ends with inspectable inquiry evidence supplied to the existing relational-envelope stage. Its three internal statuses distinguish:

1. an inquiry that can be responsibly formed;
2. evidence that no relational inquiry is material;
3. inability to form an inquiry responsibly.

Those statuses do not add a third relational-envelope form. They prevent a missing runtime result from passing vacuously as correct abstention.

### The evidence surface is independently inspectable

The thirteen common concepts and status-specific concepts expose the decisions that a pattern matcher would otherwise hide behind prose. The twenty invariants cover ownership, leakage, trigger evidence, selection lineage, Context and provenance sensitivity, semantic invariance, abstention, correction, confidence, and no-output correctness.

The baseline and candidate can receive the same admissible inputs. Inquiry formation can be assessed before downstream envelope evaluation, so a failure can be attributed to the correct boundary.

### Isolation is materially stronger than the prior experiment

The architecture denies not only Dean's later answer and held-out assessments but also generated-index snippets, examples, prompts, configuration, prior state, and human-authored semantic adapters that could carry answer-bearing structure.

Contamination invalidates the execution rather than becoming a partial pass. This directly responds to the answer-bearing fixture defect found during the prior experiment.

## D. Reservation - Held-Out Determinacy

The architecture correctly preserves semantic flexibility, but four illustrative held-out assessments do not yet select one unconditional status:

1. `CTRI-HA-003` permits no material inquiry or a different archival inquiry;
2. `CTRI-HA-004` permits no material inquiry or a formation gap;
3. `CTRI-HA-009` permits an inquiry formed or directly resolved examination evidence;
4. `CTRI-HA-011` depends on the concrete corrected purpose.

This is not an architecture blocker because the correct status genuinely depends on evidence not fully instantiated in the illustrative fixture descriptions. It is an execution blocker if left unresolved after concrete fixtures exist.

Before the first candidate execution:

1. each concrete input fixture must be frozen;
2. its held-out assessment must select one expected status, or state an evidence-based decision rule that uniquely selects the status from the frozen input;
3. the decision rationale must be recorded without reference to candidate output;
4. acceptable semantic variation must not permit materially contradictory outcomes;
5. any later fixture or assessment change must begin a new attributable evaluation cycle.

This condition prevents evaluator discretion from becoming a hidden way to accept whichever plausible result the implementation produces.

## F. Combined Authority Outcome

**Outcome:** `PASSED FOR BOUNDED IMPLEMENTATION`

MARC and Cyril independently support the same narrow decision:

1. the architecture asks the right next question;
2. the question belongs inside canonical Understanding;
3. it isolates inquiry formation from downstream proposition evaluation;
4. it protects human meaning from being guessed, pre-authored, or attributed falsely;
5. it makes selection and abstention inspectable rather than rewarding final prose;
6. it defines falsifiable controls against tokens, wording, names, order, distractors, changed Context, and leaked answers;
7. it preserves Judgement, Authority, Action, recipient alignment, and correction boundaries;
8. no runtime evidence yet establishes that the transformation works.

The architecture may therefore proceed to the smallest controlled implementation experiment under Parts G through J.

## K. Re-entry and Stop Triggers

Return to Combined Authority before proceeding if:

1. the architecture's owner, statuses, concepts, process, invariants, or handoff must change;
2. a held-out assessment cannot be made determinate from its frozen input;
3. inquiry formation requires a caller-authored semantic adapter;
4. held-out evidence cannot be isolated from runtime;
5. a third relational-envelope form or another cognitive owner becomes necessary;
6. Judgement must invent meaning or Understanding must select a response;
7. Context Door must acquire a private synthesis path;
8. correction cannot remain append-only;
9. a model, prompt, evaluator, score, trust rule, storage authority, or shared platform mechanism introduces material consequences beyond this experiment;
10. another responsibility becomes materially implicated;
11. any fixture or assessment is changed after candidate output is observed;
12. natural input, live use, durable writes, contribution, execution, deployment, or external action is proposed.

Stop rather than widen the experiment if:

1. every viable path requires pre-authoring the expected inquiry;
2. selection cannot be made inspectable independently from polished prose;
3. honest abstention cannot be distinguished from transformation failure;
4. the only observed improvement is a more convincing question;
5. source, correction, uncertainty, or human agency cannot be preserved.
