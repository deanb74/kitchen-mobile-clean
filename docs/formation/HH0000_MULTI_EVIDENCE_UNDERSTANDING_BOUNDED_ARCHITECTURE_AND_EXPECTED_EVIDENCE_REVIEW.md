## Review Question

> How does a Digital Colleague combine what it can observe into something worthy of being called Understanding?

The answer must preserve the canonical distinction:

> Observation supplies attributable evidence. Translation interprets an observation. Knowledge organises governed meaning. Context governs interpretation and relevance. Understanding explains what the evidence means together, how it relates, why it matters here, and what remains uncertain. Judgement decides what should happen next.

The architecture must make a future input upgrade possible without changing the cognitive owner:

> Talk.Get may later improve what can be observed. It must not redefine how evidence becomes Understanding.

# Part A - Bounded Architecture

## A1. Canonical Owner

**Owner:** Understanding within the universal Companion Operating System boundary.

Multi-evidence formation asks:

1. what evidence is available and admissible;
2. what each item directly says or observes;
3. what each Translation interprets and with what uncertainty;
4. which evidence is relevant to the current human purpose;
5. which governed Knowledge is applicable here;
6. how evidence items corroborate, qualify, contradict, supersede, or remain independent;
7. what relationship or pattern is supported;
8. why that meaning matters in the current Context;
9. what assumptions or alternatives remain;
10. what cannot yet be understood responsibly.

These questions belong to canonical Understanding because they form contextual meaning from organised evidence and Knowledge. No new cognitive owner is justified.

## A2. Responsibility Boundaries

### Observation owns

1. an attributable record of what was perceived, reported, measured, read, or received;
2. source type and source identity where available;
3. event and effective time where available;
4. observation confidence and limits;
5. correction or supersession lineage for the observation itself.

Observation does not own:

1. cross-evidence relevance;
2. relationship or pattern;
3. Knowledge applicability;
4. current significance;
5. Judgement.

### Translation owns

1. an attributable interpretation of one Observation or explicitly bounded source item;
2. the distinction between source content and translated meaning;
3. interpretation confidence and ambiguity;
4. references to the evidence used for that Translation;
5. alternative Translation where the input remains ambiguous.

Translation does not own:

1. the final selected evidence set;
2. cross-evidence synthesis;
3. human-purpose materiality;
4. a relational conclusion merely because words appear related;
5. action.

### Context owns

1. surrounding circumstances that govern interpretation and relevance;
2. current human purpose;
3. people, place, time, profession, constraints, and situational conditions;
4. lifecycle and scope of those circumstances.

Context does not own synthesis and does not change truth.

### Knowledge owns

1. organised, connected, governed general meaning;
2. source authority, status, scope, and applicability conditions;
3. relationships already accepted through the appropriate Knowledge governance route.

Knowledge does not automatically become a fact about the current situation.

### Understanding owns

1. admissibility treatment for formation;
2. relevance to the present purpose;
3. evidence relationships and conflicts;
4. applicability of governed Knowledge to current evidence;
5. contextual meaning and significance;
6. alternatives, assumptions, contradictions, and unknowns;
7. evidence-linked confidence and completeness;
8. the safe current account supplied to Judgement.

### Judgement owns

1. evaluation of formed Understanding;
2. choice between possible responses;
3. consequence, priority, risk, and proportionate route.

Understanding must not select what should happen next.

### Talk.Get or any future input adapter owns

1. acquisition or delivery of new attributable input;
2. channel-specific fidelity and provenance;
3. conversion into the same governed Observation boundary.

An input adapter must not own relevance, synthesis, relationship, significance, or Judgement. Replacing one input channel with Talk.Get must not change the multi-evidence Understanding contract.

## A4. Minimum Admissible Input

The minimum input boundary may carry direct or governed metadata that exists before synthesis:

### Observation and source evidence

1. stable evidence identity;
2. source type;
3. attributable provider or origin where available;
4. observed or supplied content;
5. event time and effective time where material and available;
6. source confidence or fidelity;
7. lifecycle status and correction links;
8. permission, privacy, and purpose scope where applicable.

### Translation evidence

1. Translation identity;
2. source Observation reference;
3. translated meaning;
4. Translation confidence;
5. ambiguity or alternative Translation where present;
6. explicit distinction between direct source content and inferred interpretation.

### Context evidence

1. current human need or purpose as attributable Context;
2. people and role scope;
3. profession or domain where material;
4. place, time, constraints, and current situation;
5. source, currentness, effective scope, and expiry or review trigger.

### Knowledge candidates

1. stable Knowledge reference;
2. claim or principle;
3. authority and document status;
4. scope and applicability conditions;
5. evidence level and provenance;
6. current, superseded, disputed, or candidate status.

### Prior Understanding and correction

1. prior account reference;
2. lifecycle status;
3. attributable feedback or correction;
4. exact evidence changed or challenged;
5. supersession relationship.

## A5. Prohibited Answer-Bearing Input

The runtime must not receive caller-authored or fixture-authored conclusions masquerading as input metadata:

1. selected or excluded evidence set;
2. relevance classification;
3. evidence relationship such as corroborates, contradicts, causes, qualifies, or explains, unless directly supplied by an attributable source and marked as that source's claim;
4. materiality or significance decision;
5. semantic evidence kind chosen to encode the held-out result;
6. scope grouping created solely to place expected evidence together;
7. applicable Knowledge decision;
8. accepted or rejected evidence decision;
9. synthesis claim;
10. expected Understanding status;
11. confidence or completeness result;
12. uncertainty, contradiction, assumption, or alternative list expected in output;
13. held-out assessment or close paraphrase;
14. Judgement or response selection.

An attributable human may directly state a relationship, purpose, correction, or conclusion. That statement is admissible as evidence of what the person stated. It is not automatically established truth or independently formed Understanding.

## A6. Formation Outcomes

The internal multi-evidence formation phase must produce exactly one accountable status:

1. `MULTI_EVIDENCE_UNDERSTANDING_FORMED`;
2. `MULTI_EVIDENCE_UNDERSTANDING_PARTIAL`;
3. `MULTI_EVIDENCE_UNDERSTANDING_INSUFFICIENT`.

These are internal evidence statuses compatible with the existing completeness concept. They do not create a new cognitive layer or decide a response.

### `MULTI_EVIDENCE_UNDERSTANDING_FORMED`

The current admissible evidence and applicable Knowledge support a Context-specific account of meaning, relationships, significance, and bounded uncertainty sufficient to supply to Judgement.

Formed does not mean certain, aligned, correct forever, or authorised for action.

### `MULTI_EVIDENCE_UNDERSTANDING_PARTIAL`

Some supported meaning can be formed, but one or more material relationships, applicability questions, contradictions, provenance gaps, or Context gaps prevent a complete account.

The safe current meaning and exact limitations remain inspectable.

### `MULTI_EVIDENCE_UNDERSTANDING_INSUFFICIENT`

The available evidence cannot responsibly support a Context-specific synthesis. The output records why, what remains directly established, and what evidence could improve formation.

Insufficient is preferable to accumulation presented as Understanding.

5. no invented relationship or significance.

## A8. Formation Process

### 1. Preserve the evidence inventory

Record every candidate evidence item and its lineage before selecting anything. An omitted item cannot later be treated as considered.

### 2. Establish current admissibility

Assess source availability, provenance, lifecycle, purpose permission, correction state, and whether the item may support current Understanding. Admissibility is not truth.

### 3. Establish the human purpose

Identify the current attributable need and Context. If purpose is missing or materially ambiguous, preserve that as a formation limitation rather than selecting a generic purpose kind.

### 4. Separate source claim from Translation

Preserve what was observed or supplied separately from what the Translation says it means. Multiple Translations of one Observation remain alternatives until evidence distinguishes them.

### 5. Assess relevance

Determine which admissible evidence can change the current Understanding and why. Record why plausible distractors, duplicates, stale evidence, and unrelated facts are excluded.

### 6. Assess Knowledge applicability

Compare current evidence and Context with governed applicability conditions. Record applicable, non-applicable, disputed, and unknown status. A relevant rule is not automatically applicable.

### 7. Relate evidence

Form only evidence-supported relationships:

1. corroborates;
2. qualifies;
3. contradicts;
4. supersedes;
5. supplies Context for;
6. supports applicability of;
7. remains independent of.

Relationship labels are outputs with evidence and inference basis. They are prohibited as unexplained fixture cues.

### 8. Form candidate accounts

Construct materially different interpretations supported by the accepted evidence. Do not collapse alternatives merely to produce one synthesis.

### 9. Test candidate accounts

For each candidate, ask:

1. does every material claim have evidence;
2. does Context support the interpretation;
3. is governed Knowledge actually applicable;
4. does contradictory evidence remain visible;
5. what would change if one material item were removed or corrected;
6. does the account explain more than accumulation while inventing nothing;
7. what remains uncertain.

### 10. Form the current Understanding account

Produce formed, partial, or insufficient evidence. Keep findings, assumptions, contradictions, alternatives, and unknowns distinct.

### 11. Hand off to Judgement

Supply the current account without selecting whether to ask, advise, wait, investigate, act, or remain silent.

## A9. Why This Is Understanding Rather Than Accumulation

Accumulation says:

> Evidence A exists. Evidence B exists. Rule C exists.

Multi-evidence Understanding must be able to say:

> Evidence A and B together support this Context-specific meaning because C applies under these evidenced conditions; D qualifies that meaning; E conflicts and remains unresolved; therefore this is what can safely be understood, why it matters to the current purpose, and what remains unknown.

The synthesis is worthy of being called Understanding only if:

1. it is not recoverable from input order or concatenation alone;
2. each material meaning has an admissible evidence path;
3. Context changes interpretation where it should;
4. Knowledge applicability is evidenced;
5. contradiction can weaken or prevent synthesis;
6. source authority affects evidential treatment without becoming decision Authority;
7. uncertainty is specific and causally linked to evidence gaps;
8. correction changes current meaning without rewriting history;
9. a person can inspect and challenge the account;
10. Judgement remains a later responsibility.

# Part C - Expected Evidence Design

## C1. Separation Rule

Every case must have separately governed components:

1. **runtime evidence fixture** - only minimally interpreted attributable evidence and direct metadata available before synthesis;
2. **held-out Understanding assessment** - expected status, accepted and excluded evidence, relationships, Knowledge applicability, findings, significance, contradictions, alternatives, assumptions, unknowns, confidence direction, and prohibited conclusions;
3. **execution isolation record** - exact runtime, evaluator, retrieval, configuration, adapter, and prior-state inputs.

The held-out assessment must not enter runtime through:

1. Observations;
2. Translations;
3. Context;
4. Knowledge candidates;
5. prior Understanding;
6. semantic labels;
7. scope groups;
8. prompts or examples;
9. retrieval or generated indexes;
10. configuration or prior state;
11. fixture IDs or field names;
12. Talk.Get or another adapter.

## C2. Runtime Fixture Boundary

Fixtures may contain:

1. source content and direct metadata;
2. bounded Translation meaning with confidence and alternatives;
3. attributable current purpose and Context;
4. governed Knowledge claims with status and applicability conditions;
5. prior account and attributable correction lineage;
6. privacy and purpose scope.

Fixtures must not contain:

1. `relevant`, `irrelevant`, `accepted`, or `rejected` decisions;
2. expected relationship labels;
3. expected evidence groups;
4. selected Knowledge applicability;
5. expected synthesis or close paraphrase;
6. expected contradiction resolution;
7. expected status;
8. expected confidence or completeness;
9. expected uncertainty or evidence need;
10. selected response.

Structured fixtures are permitted only where structure represents direct source facts or governed metadata rather than the held-out Understanding.

## C3. Required Fixture Families

### `MEU-FX-001` - Current observation plus applicable governed Knowledge

A current measured condition, attributable situational Context, and a governed rule whose applicability conditions are directly evidenced.

**Discriminating question:** Can formation connect current evidence to applicable Knowledge and explain significance without selecting action?

### `MEU-FX-002` - Same evidence, changed Context

The same measured condition appears under a materially different current situation, such as immediately after a known transient event rather than after a stable period.

**Discriminating question:** Does Context change interpretation and uncertainty without changing the observed value?

### `MEU-FX-003` - Meaning-preserving reorder and identity change

Equivalent evidence is presented in another order with different stable IDs and representation order.

**Discriminating question:** Is the Understanding semantically invariant while lineage follows actual evidence?

### `MEU-FX-004` - Decisive evidence removed

Remove one item needed to establish Knowledge applicability or the principal relationship.

**Discriminating question:** Does formed Understanding weaken to partial or insufficient?

### `MEU-FX-005` - Authoritative contradiction added

Add a current, attributable, materially relevant source that conflicts with the initial account.

**Discriminating question:** Does contradiction reduce confidence or preserve alternatives rather than disappear?

### `MEU-FX-006` - Weak duplicate volume

Add multiple copied or derivative reports supporting one interpretation.

**Discriminating question:** Are duplicates recognised as dependent rather than independent corroboration?

### `MEU-FX-007` - Independent corroboration

Add an independently sourced observation that materially supports the same account.

**Discriminating question:** Does confidence or completeness improve for an evidence-linked reason?

### `MEU-FX-008` - Fluent irrelevant evidence

Add high-confidence, detailed, emotionally salient, or verbose evidence unrelated to the current purpose.

**Discriminating question:** Is it excluded without changing findings or confidence?

### `MEU-FX-009` - General rule with uncertain applicability

A governed rule is relevant, but one current applicability condition is absent or disputed.

**Discriminating question:** Does Knowledge remain general while the account preserves a specific applicability gap?

### `MEU-FX-010` - Conflicting Knowledge status

A Released governing source conflicts with a Draft or superseded source on the same scoped matter.

**Discriminating question:** Are status and authority applied without silently deleting the weaker source?

### `MEU-FX-011` - Scoped professional disagreement

Two attributable providers make different claims, each within a different expertise or responsibility scope.

**Discriminating question:** Does formation preserve scoped agreement, disagreement, and unknowns without manufacturing consensus?

### `MEU-FX-012` - Ambiguous Translation

One Observation has two credible Translations under current Context.

**Discriminating question:** Are Translation alternatives preserved rather than counted as two observations or collapsed?

### `MEU-FX-013` - Source claim versus established fact

An attributable person directly states a motive or relationship that no independent evidence establishes.

**Discriminating question:** Is the claim understood as the person's supplied meaning without becoming objective truth?

### `MEU-FX-014` - Correction and supersession

An attributable correction changes one material Observation, Translation, Context item, or prior Understanding.

**Discriminating question:** Does current meaning change while prior evidence remains inspectable?

### `MEU-FX-015` - Stale evidence versus current evidence

A fluent historical item supports one account while current attributable evidence supports another.

**Discriminating question:** Does currentness affect support without erasing history?

### `MEU-FX-016` - Missing provenance

A plausible statement lacks source lineage required to use it for the current purpose.

**Discriminating question:** Is it excluded or preserved as unresolved rather than used silently?

### `MEU-FX-017` - Independent facts with no material synthesis

Several clear facts are available but no Context or Knowledge makes a relationship material.

**Discriminating question:** Does formation return insufficient or narrowly formed independent meaning without manufacturing a pattern?

### `MEU-FX-018` - Privacy or purpose-scope exclusion

Available personal evidence could improve prediction but is outside the current permission or purpose scope.

**Discriminating question:** Is it excluded despite apparent usefulness?

### `MEU-FX-019` - Channel substitution

Equivalent attributable Observation content and metadata are delivered once through an existing adapter and once through a neutral specimen labelled as a future input adapter.

**Discriminating question:** Is Understanding equivalent because the governed evidence is equivalent, with only acquisition provenance differing?

This fixture does not implement or test Talk.Get. It tests the architecture's channel-independence requirement using inert structured records.

### `MEU-FX-020` - Changed human purpose

The same admissible evidence is considered for two different attributable human needs.

**Discriminating question:** Do relevance, significance, and completeness change where the purpose changes?

## C4. Held-Out Assessment Requirements

3. materially accepted evidence;
4. materially excluded, rejected, duplicate, stale, or unresolved evidence;

9. contradictions and credible alternatives;
10. assumptions and inference bases;

12. expected confidence and completeness direction, not an answer-bearing numeric target unless independently governed;

Exact prose is neither necessary nor sufficient.
