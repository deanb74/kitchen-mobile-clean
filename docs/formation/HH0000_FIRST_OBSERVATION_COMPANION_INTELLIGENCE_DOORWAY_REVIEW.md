## 4. AI Interaction and Companion Intelligence Interaction

### 4.1 AI interaction

```text
QUESTION -> ANSWER
```

This pattern treats the interaction primarily as input and output. It may be useful for direct information retrieval, but it does not by itself preserve human context, relationship, reflection, or uncertainty.

### 4.2 Companion Intelligence interaction

```text
PERSON -> CONTEXT -> CONVERSATION -> UNDERSTANDING -> REFLECTION
```

This pattern begins with a person rather than an abstract question. Context shapes the meaning of the conversation. Understanding is formed rather than assumed from an answer. Reflection allows both the human and Helping Hand to learn from what occurred.

The First Observation is an early doorway into this second pattern because:

- a human initiates the exchange;
- the human asks for meaning in Andy’s own words;
- unknowns are explicitly welcome;
- the human learns from the response;
- the response is recorded separately from human interpretation;
- the relationship remains bounded by human authority.

```text
ANSWER != UNDERSTANDING
QUESTION != PERSON
RESPONSE != RELATIONSHIP
```
