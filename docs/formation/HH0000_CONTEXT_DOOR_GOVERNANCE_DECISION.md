	- Material example: "Andy I want to do something bad... oh, forget I said that"

These answers establish the following Context Door-specific decisions:

1. specific personal context may become relationship memory only when the interaction provides a legitimate invitation to remember it;
2. the invitation remains scoped to the purpose and Context of the interaction; ordinary conversation is not unlimited consent to retain everything said;
3. inspection establishes what is actually retained and presents, where available and relevant, the retained item, source and provenance, date and time, relevant Context, derived conclusions or Understanding, subsequent correction, qualification or supersession, and current status;
4. inspection does not present an inference as an original fact;
5. withdrawal changes or removes the invitation upon which remembering depended;
6. withdrawal does not automatically require unconditional destruction of every associated record;
7. a request to forget is new attributable Context and does not silently rewrite history or automatically erase material context;
8. removal treatment considers what is requested, why it was retained, current relevance, consequence, continuing responsibilities, applicable consent, privacy and governance boundaries, and any legitimate accountability or safety preservation requirement already established by applicable governance;
9. no new safety threshold, legal retention requirement, automatic deletion rule, or automatic retention rule is created; professional, legal, governance, and Authority boundaries apply only where genuinely applicable;
10. usefulness alone does not authorise retention;
11. specific relationship information may be corrected, expire, cease to be relevant, or be removed through its governed lifecycle;
12. legitimately derived generic learning does not automatically disappear when originating specific relationship memory later does, but it contains no unnecessary person-specific information and must not reconstruct removed personal information;
13. generic learning reaches shared Knowledge only through the existing governed Learning and Knowledge route; no automatic KnowledgeGraph promotion is authorised.

The resulting four-way distinction is:

| Information class | Recorded treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Ephemeral Context | Naturally ceases to matter or expires; no relationship-memory persistence is inferred |
| Consented relationship memory | Requires a legitimate, purpose-and-Context-scoped invitation and remains subject to inspection, withdrawal, correction, expiry, and removal governance |
| Material record with competing consequences or responsibilities | A removal request is attributable Context considered through existing Context, Understanding, Judgement, consequence, applicable governance, and Authority; neither deletion nor retention is automatic |
| De-identified generic learning | Contains no unnecessary person-specific information, cannot reconstruct removed personal information, and may travel only through governed Learning and Knowledge |

This direction applies existing Helping Hand governance. `docs/theory/001-THEORY-OF-MEMORY.md` already establishes selective retention and release according to continuing significance. `docs/theory/007-THEORY-OF-CONTEXT.md` already makes Context responsible for relevance and interpretation. Existing relationship-memory governance requires explicit consent for persistence rather than inferring consent from ordinary conversation. Existing Judgement and Authority rules govern consequential treatment. Existing Learning governance prohibits automatic promotion and personal-information travel.

1. form an Understanding;
2. communicate or reframe it;
3. receive any rejection or correction from the recipient;
4. prevent the rejected interpretation from becoming retained truth or Knowledge;
5. seek to understand the correction;
6. learn, reframe, and redeliver;
7. confirm alignment where appropriate;
8. retain the aligned meaning or formulation that produced Understanding;
9. preserve only minimum governed correction lineage where required.

This direction applies existing Helping Hand Theory and governance. `docs/theory/003-THEORY-OF-UNDERSTANDING.md` distinguishes Understanding from recall and defines it as contextual meaning. `docs/theory/007-THEORY-OF-CONTEXT.md` establishes that words do not possess fixed meaning in isolation and that words are only one component of Understanding.
