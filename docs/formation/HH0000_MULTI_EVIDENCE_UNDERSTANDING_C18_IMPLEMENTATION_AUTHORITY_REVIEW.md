## 4. Projection-Necessity Enforcement

### 4.1 Reference-only default

Every candidate and held-out side begins in reference-only form. Exact immutable identity, path/reference, available digest, selected material dimensions, and closed observation/inference statuses must be attempted before semantic projection is admissible.

Absence of a projection means minimisation succeeded. It must not mean missing required evidence.

### 4.2 Smallest acceptable admission mechanism

A projection is valid only when the same comparison record includes a separately validated projection-admission decision establishing all of the following:

1. the side requiring projection: candidate or held-out;
2. the exact immutable reference already available;
3. the governed comparison rule and selected material dimensions;
4. a closed necessity reason explaining why reference-only review cannot preserve the applied material basis;
5. the exact scope that may be projected;
6. confirmation that the projection contains no surrounding account, requirement, or source-person content;
7. confirmation that the projection is evaluator-authored and does not replace the referenced value;
8. a closed admission result reached before record finalization.

The closed necessity reasons may encode only these settled concepts:

1. the available exact reference necessarily opens a materially broader semantic scope than the proposition being compared;
2. the referenced representation does not itself preserve the bounded material meaning applied by the evaluator, although its immutable identity remains linked;
3. a separately governed comparison rule requires a bounded material projection for later challengeability and identifies the required scope.

Exact serialized labels may be selected during implementation. No generic `useful`, `helpful`, `clearer`, `debug`, or evaluator-preference reason is permitted.

### 4.3 Validation rule

Validation must reject a projection when:

1. no admission decision exists;
2. the reason is open text or outside the closed necessity concepts;
3. identity, exact reference, digest, and closed statuses already preserve the same discrimination;
4. projected content exceeds the selected material dimensions or admitted proposition scope;
5. the projection copies a whole candidate account, held-out assessment, requirement subtree, or source-person item;
6. the projection cannot be traced to both its immutable reference and comparison rule;
7. candidate and held-out content are combined into one unattributable projection;
8. projection admission is added or changed after finalization.

If reference-only review is insufficient and a valid bounded projection cannot be formed, the semantic disposition is comparison insufficient. Implementation must not widen capture automatically.

### 4.4 Reviewability

Projection necessity is reviewable through the admission decision, not through developer intent. Synthetic evidence must prove reference-only success, unjustified-projection rejection, justified bounded-projection acceptance, over-broad rejection, and failure to comparison-insufficient when projection cannot be admitted.

## 5. Purpose-Limitation / Non-Reuse Enforcement

### 5.1 One-way evidence path

The only authorised data direction is:

```text
C12 immutable candidate capture + C15-C17 validated held-out assessment
  -> C18 comparison
  -> finalized C18 evidence record
  -> immutable campaign package inclusion
  -> C22 opaque package/status routing
  -> unchanged C23/C24 preservation
```

The comparison record is an evaluation/accountability artefact. Retention does not grant reuse authority.

### 5.2 Source and API closure

Implementation must enforce:

1. C18 construction and validation accept only the already accepted post-capture inputs and provenance identities;
2. finalized C18 records expose no callback, adapter, service, event, query, or context-builder interface usable by candidate, baseline, another cycle, prompt, retrieval, Memory, Learning, cache, configuration, prior state, or future execution;
3. package assembly may receive finalized records but may only include them unchanged;
4. C22 receives only an opaque status necessary for fixed continuation or stop behavior;
5. no general-purpose log, analytics stream, semantic index, repository-generated context, or persistent runtime store receives record content;
6. no denied component imports the C18 evidence constructor, validator, semantic values, projections, or record reader;
7. any future read/review access remains outside execution and requires separate governed exact-path authority.

### 5.3 Proof boundary

Purpose limitation must be proven by static dependency closure, public API/export inspection, denied-import checks, denied-flow synthetic evidence, and package-boundary evidence. Naming a field `evidence-only`, documenting intent, or relying on review convention is insufficient.

Any required bidirectional API, shared semantic helper, runtime record reader, or reuse-capable store triggers architecture re-entry.

## 6. Concrete Type / Schema Authority Boundary

Authority is granted to define one concrete record type and its validating serialized representation, subject to these constraints:

1. one record equals one independently attributable comparison;

Authority is not granted to invent new semantic meanings in type or schema code. Ambiguous semantics must return to design review.

## 7. Closed Semantic / Status Model

Implementation may define exact closed labels for the following already settled concepts:

| Model | Required closed concepts | Separation requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Semantic disposition | material equivalence; material difference; comparison insufficient; not evaluated | Does not allocate deficiency or express campaign success |
| Evaluator-condition status | accepted condition satisfied; not satisfied; not determinable; not evaluated | Separate from semantic disposition and visible to C22 only as an opaque projection |
| Observation class | dimension-level match, difference, absence, contradiction, or inability to observe | Records literal detection, not material interpretation |
| Inference class | material, immaterial, unsupported, contradictory, or indeterminate interpretation | Cannot exist without attributable observation |
| Uncertainty / insufficiency reason | missing or limiting identity, reference, criterion, rule, dimension, observation, inference, evidence support, linkage, or record completion | Must not default to candidate failure |
| Material-dimension identity | only governed dimensions selected by the comparison rule | No universal emitted checklist and no post-observation invention |
| Technical record state | constructing, incomplete, finalized, or refused concepts as needed | Separate from semantic and evaluator-condition status |
| Projection admission | reference-only; projection admitted under closed necessity; projection refused | Separate from semantic disposition |

Disagreement and credible alternatives remain explicit overlays rather than additional dispositions. Chain-of-thought, unrestricted rationale, arbitrary scores, and open semantic status strings are prohibited.

### 8.2 Distinct seals

2. C20 sealing freezes mechanical access and order evidence.
3. C24 sealing proves persisted package and receipt integrity.

No implementation may merge these responsibilities, reuse one seal as proof of another, or let C18 claim preservation integrity.

### 8.3 Incomplete records

An incomplete record must remain visibly incomplete and preserve only facts actually established. It cannot be finalized as complete, supplied with a default disposition, reconstructed after handoff, or repaired using C21 or package results.

The package may preserve an incomplete record only under the accepted fail-closed package state. It must not present it as a complete evaluator finding.

## 9. Package and C20/C21 Linkage

3. C20 records no candidate projection, held-out criterion, material dimension, observation, inference, disposition, uncertainty, or rationale.
4. C21 receives only sealed C20 evidence and fixed contamination rules.
5. The package separately associates the C18 record identity, C20 record identity, and C21 finding identity.
6. C18 must not receive C21 findings to revise, endorse, suppress, or rewrite its semantic record.
7. Adverse or unavailable C21 evidence affects claim-bearing campaign use, not the stored C18 semantic disposition.
8. Package assembly may validate presence and identity linkage only; it may not transform, summarize, select, or interpret C18 content.

### 15.3 Purpose and secondary-use falsification

**Finding:** Yes, if and only if the record has one downstream package path, no generalized reader/store/event surface, denied components cannot import or receive it, and static plus synthetic evidence proves closure.
