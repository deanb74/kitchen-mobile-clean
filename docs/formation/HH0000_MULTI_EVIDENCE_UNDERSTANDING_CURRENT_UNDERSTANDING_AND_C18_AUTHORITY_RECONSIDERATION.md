## 3. Theory-Derived Conclusions

### 3.1 Current Understanding

The best-supported current conclusion is:

> Current Understanding is a Context-scoped, evidence-linked, perspective-aware and uncertainty-honest interpretation that is sufficiently justified for consideration by Judgement in relation to a presently proposed consequence.

Current Understanding is:

1. stable enough to use where its justification remains sufficient;
2. persistent enough to provide continuity and avoid perpetual rediscovery;
3. open to attributable challenge and re-examination;
4. revisable without rewriting the historical Evidence or prior Understanding from which it developed;
5. distinct from truth, permanent proof, Judgement, Authority, and Action.

`Current` does not mean casual, speculative, unreliable, or disposable. It means operative within a defined Context, evidence state, Perspective set, purpose, time, and consequence envelope.

### 3.2 Established truth versus current Understanding

The repository supports the following distinction:

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Established truth | A claim about what is true, carrying a stronger epistemic meaning than C18 is authorised to grant |
| Current Understanding | What relevant Evidence, Knowledge, Context, and presently available Perspectives reasonably support now, with visible uncertainty and scope |
| Established evidential fact | A bounded fact actually produced or observed by its responsible source, such as record identity, event membership, or byte preservation |
| Current usability | Judgement finds the Understanding sufficient for the present consequence, within Authority and known limits |
| Permanent proof | A claim that future Evidence or Perspective cannot overturn the conclusion; neither Understanding nor C18 requires this |

The word `established` is not inherently wrong. It is appropriate for bounded evidential facts and provenance. It becomes misleading when allowed to imply permanent semantic truth or owner-independent certainty.

### 3.3 Evidence and Perspective

The most defensible working distinction is:

> Evidence is attributable material available for consideration.

> Perspective is an attributable standpoint or interpretive position from which Evidence, relevance, Context, meaning, uncertainty, and consequence may presently be considered.

> Understanding is the current synthesis of relevant Evidence and Perspectives within Context.

Perspective does not alter underlying Evidence. It may change what relationships, omissions, meanings, or consequences become visible.

Not every Perspective is equally valid or materially relevant. A Perspective must remain attributable, in scope, evidence-responsive, honest about uncertainty, and proportionate to the consequence. Confidence, novelty, status, or disagreement alone does not make a Perspective authoritative.

A new Perspective creates a reason to challenge or re-examine when it is materially relevant. It does not automatically supersede current Understanding.

### 3.4 Open Understanding and finite decisions

Understanding remaining open does not require every task or decision to remain unresolved.

The stopping condition supported by current Theory and downstream governance is:

> Judgement may conclude that Helping Hand knows enough for the consequence presently proposed when materially relevant Evidence, Context, and Perspectives have been considered; material disagreement and uncertainty are preserved; no unresolved limitation on which the proposed consequence depends has been hidden; the resulting Understanding is proportionate to the decision class; and the correct Authority boundary is known.

This is not closure against future knowledge. It is closure of the present Judgement or engineering question within an explicit consequence envelope and review conditions.

Consensus, exhaustive Perspective collection, complete certainty, and immunity from future discovery are not required. Higher, irreversible, wider, or more personal consequences require stronger justification and broader relevant Perspective coverage. Low-consequence, reversible, bounded steps should not inherit the evidential burden of permanent or high-consequence decisions.

4. materially relevant Perspectives and their attributable owners/scopes;

7. correction, contradiction, challenge, supersession, and re-examination events;
8. the resulting Current Understanding and its lineage;
9. Judgement, Authority, Action, and outcome records where separately governed;
10. review triggers and any confirmed Learning.

Current Understanding may change. Historical Evidence and prior Understanding must not be rewritten to make the present appear inevitable.

Superseded Understanding remains historically real but must not be presented as concurrently current. Disputed Understanding is not automatically superseded; unresolved positions remain visible until a governed basis supports confirmation, refinement, reframing, or revision.

## 6. Re-examination Triggers

Re-examination is justified when one or more of the following is materially relevant to the current purpose or proposed consequence:

1. new attributable Evidence or Observation;
2. correction, invalidation, or material incompleteness of existing Evidence;
3. changed Context, purpose, timeframe, people, environment, or applicable Knowledge;
4. a newly available relevant Perspective that exposes an omission, alternative interpretation, or consequence;
5. contradiction between Current Understanding and a present observation or outcome;
6. reduced confidence that current inputs remain valid;
7. experience, reflection, or outcomes revealing something previously unseen;
8. a proposed consequence becoming broader, less reversible, more personal, or higher risk;
9. a material assumption or unknown becoming outcome-determinative;
10. expiry or review conditions attached to the Current Understanding.

The theoretical possibility that some unknown future Evidence or Perspective may exist is not by itself a trigger and does not make Current Understanding unusable.

## 7. Conditions for Current Usability

Current Understanding remains usable when:

1. its Evidence and provenance are sufficient for the claim it actually makes;
2. materially relevant presently available Perspectives have been represented or their absence is explicitly accepted within the consequence envelope;
3. current Context, purpose, and timeframe remain applicable;
4. material contradictions, alternatives, assumptions, and unknowns are visible;
5. no unresolved issue on which the proposed consequence depends has been silently bypassed;
6. confidence and completeness are proportionate rather than manufactured;
7. Judgement can explain why the Understanding is sufficient for this consequence now;
8. Authority independently permits the proposed response or Action;
9. review triggers and revision paths remain available;
10. historical lineage remains inspectable.

Current Understanding need not survive every possible future discovery. It must survive the presently relevant challenge appropriate to the consequence now proposed.

## 8. Analysis-Paralysis Assessment

### 8.1 Observation

The C18 correction loop repeatedly found genuine bounded defects, authorised a narrow correction, then applied a stronger adversarial provenance challenge at another public boundary. The reviews had precise defect-level stop conditions but no explicit epistemic exit condition tied to C18's actual consequence.

The phrase `established understanding` was often operationally read as `facts actually established by owners`, which is legitimate for provenance. Across repeated acceptance loops, however, exact conformance, owner establishment, and package completeness began to carry an implied burden closer to globally unforgeable or permanently proven Understanding, even though C18 grants neither truth nor Action Authority.

### 8.2 Conclusion

The loop has lacked a sufficiently explicit stopping condition at the theory/authority level.

That does not mean prior safeguards were pointless or that the counterexamples were invented. MARC and Cyril exposed real differences between semantic claims, evidential provenance, public construction, package completion, and attributable failure. Their foresight protected future systems from consequences accidentally authorised today.

The missing condition was:

> What exact consequence is being considered, which boundary can authoritatively produce the claim for that consequence, and what minimum justification is sufficient there?

Without that condition, every structurally reachable constructor could be treated as though it independently conferred the same governed consequence as the accepted owner path, and every theoretical future substitution risk could become a present completion blocker.

### 8.3 Valid stopping rule

Understanding is open-ended. A decision or engineering task does not have to be.

An engineering acceptance loop may stop when:

1. the authorised claim and consequence are explicit;
2. the claim-bearing boundary is explicit;
3. evidence integrity is sufficient at that boundary;
4. known defects cannot materially alter or over-authorise the stated consequence;
5. residual uncertainty and review triggers are recorded;
6. tests discriminate the authorised consequence rather than hypothetical stronger ones;
7. Judgement and Authority remain separate from the evidence result.

## 9. MARC and Cyril Reconsidered Without Weakening

MARC and Cyril should not be weakened, removed, or converted into automatic approval roles.

Their more precise shared responsibility is:

> Protect the integrity of today's Understanding without preventing tomorrow from changing it.

MARC protects people, dignity, truthful claims, proportionality, challengeability, and the preservation of unknown. Cyril protects ownership, provenance, runtime integrity, dependency boundaries, and the technical conditions under which claims can be relied upon.

Their foresight should:

1. identify materially missing Evidence or Perspective;
2. expose consequences hidden inside present claims;
3. prevent today's evidence from accidentally authorising tomorrow's capability;
4. preserve open review and correction paths;
5. require stronger justification as consequences grow;
6. permit closure when the present consequence is sufficiently justified.

Foresight is not fortune telling. It should not require Current Understanding to remain correct under unknown future Evidence before it can serve today.

> Understanding serves today. Foresight protects tomorrow. Judgement determines what Helping Hand may responsibly do now.

## 10. C18 Consequence Analysis

### 10.1 What C18 actually authorises

C18 itself authorises no Action.

Its allowed consequence is narrower:

1. produce an attributable evaluator record of what was compared and what finding was formed;
2. make that record reviewable without reconstructing meaning or widening access;
3. provide a separate opaque evaluator-condition state to fixed package coordination;
4. preserve uncertainty, disagreement, insufficiency, and technical limits;
5. support a later governed reviewer in challenging the evaluator finding.

C18 does not establish permanent truth, candidate deficiency, held-out correctness, evaluator correctness, Understanding capability, execution readiness, or execution Authority.

### 10.2 Minimum justification for that consequence

The minimum remains:

1. exact identity of the actual candidate capture and held-out criterion compared;
2. attributable observation, inference, disposition, disagreement, uncertainty, evaluator, and rule;
3. visible incomplete or insufficient states where material inputs are absent;
4. enough C20/C21 provenance to establish that the claimed evaluation occurred after capture under the accepted isolated sequence;
5. immutable and reviewable package carriage at the boundary that actually claims governed completion;
6. no feedback, semantic repair, truth claim, or unauthorised downstream use.

These are not demands for permanent truth. They are minimum accountability conditions for a claim about what evaluation actually occurred.

## 11. Reassessment of the Two Remaining Counterexamples

### 11.1 Self-consistent surrogate package

**Observation:** The exported synthetic coordinator can accept a wholly caller-built, internally self-consistent cycle package and return `COMPLETED`.

**What it threatens:** If that exported result is itself a claim-bearing governed campaign package, it can falsely claim that owner-produced evaluation, mechanical recording, and contamination assessment occurred. That materially threatens reviewability and trust even under Flexible Understanding.

**What it does not threaten:** It does not show that C18's semantic Current Understanding is permanently wrong. It does not bypass the ordinary `runCase001Campaign -> runCase001Experiment` owner path. It does not authorise Action or execution.

**Reconsidered conclusion:** The counterexample remains material only if the exported dependency-injection coordinator is intended or permitted to produce claim-bearing governed evidence. If it is a synthetic/non-execution construction seam, the appropriate response may be to reclassify or constrain its evidential status rather than demand owner authenticity from arbitrary in-memory test dependencies. The current authority assumes every exported completion path is claim-bearing; that assumption now requires explicit reconsideration.

### 11.2 Absent mechanical evidence exception

**Observation:** Absent `evidence.mechanical` causes an exception rather than a returned `STOPPED` campaign record.

**What it threatens:** At any runtime boundary authorised to accept externally constructed or malformed outcomes, it loses attributable failure evidence and violates the existing fail-closed package contract.

**What it does not threaten:** It does not make a semantic finding false or imply permanent uncertainty.

**Reconsidered conclusion:** This remains a material integrity defect for any public or claim-bearing boundary that accepts runtime-unvalidated outcomes. It may require a smaller totality correction independent of the broader owner-provenance question. If the coordinator is made private and receives only an internally total owner result, the relevant runtime boundary and required failure behavior must be identified explicitly rather than assumed.

## 12. Proportionality of Current C18 Requirements

The C18 semantic record requirements remain proportionate to their limited consequence. They preserve attribution, uncertainty, challengeability, and reviewer access without granting truth or Action.

The package requirements divide into two classes:

1. **Still proportionate:** exact identity correspondence, visible incompleteness, mechanical completion, C20/C21 separation, immutable package carriage, and attributable failure at the actual claim-bearing boundary.
2. **Potentially over-broad:** requiring every exported synthetic constructor to prove actual owner origin merely because it can return the same structural package type, without first deciding whether that surface is authoritative evidence or a non-claim-bearing test seam.

Flexible Understanding does not justify weakening evidence integrity. It requires evidence integrity to be calibrated to the actual claim, boundary, and consequence.

## 13. Proposed Theory Clarifications

No canonical Theory file is changed by this review. Under Theory Governance, the following are proposals requiring separate cross-validation.

### 13.1 Theory of Understanding

Consider clarifying that:

1. Understanding is a current, Context-scoped interpretation of relevant Knowledge, Evidence, and Perspectives;
2. it is persistent but revisable;
3. it remains usable while sufficiently justified for the presently proposed consequence;
4. uncertainty and review triggers are part of Understanding rather than proof of failure;
5. new relevant Evidence or Perspective triggers re-examination, not automatic replacement;
6. Understanding is neither permanent truth nor Authority.

### 13.2 Theory of Context and Perspective

Consider defining Perspective explicitly within or alongside Context as an attributable standpoint that influences relevance and interpretation without changing Evidence or truth.

Do not create a new core stage unless cross-validation establishes that Perspective performs a distinct universal transformation rather than governing interpretation as part of Context and multi-evidence synthesis.

### 13.3 Theory of Judgement

Consider adding a consequence-proportionate sufficiency theorem:

> Judgement may responsibly conclude that Understanding is sufficient when it is sufficiently justified for the consequence presently proposed, material uncertainty remains visible, and the correct Authority boundary is known.

### 13.4 Theory Map and Synthesis

Consider showing an open challenge loop without replacing the existing inheritance chain:

```text
Current Understanding
        |
        v
Judgement -> Action -> Outcome -> Reflection -> Learning
        ^                                  |
        |                                  v
        +-- New Evidence / Context / Perspective / Challenge
```

The map should preserve that no stage bypasses another and that historical Evidence remains unchanged when interpretation evolves.

### 13.5 Memory, Learning, Wisdom, and Trust

Cross-validation should confirm:

1. Memory preserves prior Understanding and correction lineage without retaining every detail or presenting superseded meaning as current;
2. Learning can improve future Understanding without rewriting the triggering history;
3. Wisdom includes knowing when present Understanding is sufficient and when consequences require further inquiry;
4. Trust can be strengthened by attributable revision rather than depending on never changing one's mind.
