# Volume VII Validation Plan

> Nothing is invented twice. Everything is inherited.

## Purpose

Volume VII begins with validation, not implementation.

Volume VII's purpose is evidence-based learning.

Validation is the activity.

Evidence-based learning is the organisational outcome.

Its purpose is to prove that Helping Hand can teach its own way of thinking before it teaches any profession.

The primary proof path is Candidate 0 — HH-0000 (Andy).

---

## Volume VII Stages

### Stage 1 — Validate Andy's reasoning

Goal:

> Demonstrate that Helping Hand can teach its own way of thinking.

Success criterion:

A body of validation evidence organised into Validation Sets across different reasoning situations.

### Stage 2 — Derive Andy's reasoning engine

Goal:

> Derive the explicit reasoning architecture from Candidate 0 evidence.

Success criterion:

The reasoning flow is documented as an inherited architecture and remains faithful to constitutional, theoretical and governance foundations.

Reference: [Andy Reasoning Engine](../architecture/ANDY_REASONING_ENGINE.md)

### Stage 3 — Build Andy

Goal:

> Implement HH-0000 from the validated and derived foundations.

Success criterion:

Implementation feels mechanical rather than exploratory because constitutional, operating, theoretical, governance and reasoning foundations are already established.

### Stage 4 — Validate the inheritance model

Goal:

> Demonstrate that Andy's reasoning remains stable across different conversational contexts.

Success criterion:

Candidate 0 continues to reason consistently when context changes while remaining faithful to constitutional, theoretical and governance foundations.

### Stage 5 — Prove transferability to future Digital Colleagues

Goal:

> Demonstrate that the reasoning model can be inherited by profession-specific Digital Colleagues without domain lock-in.

Success criterion:

Lightweight transferability exercises show the same reasoning architecture can transfer before any profession-specific implementation is built.

### Stage 6 — Certify the foundations

Goal:

> Establish whether the foundations are sufficient for MILESTONE_005.

Success criterion:

The Candidate 0 Validation Report demonstrates that the body of evidence is strong enough to certify that the foundations are stable, reusable and fit for purpose.

## Candidate 0 Validation Set Sequence

The first validation sets should cover the following themes:

| Validation Set | Theme | What it proves |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| 001 | Governance | Protects principles under pressure |
| 002 | Theory conflict | Resolves competing principles |
| 003 | Uncertainty | Recognises when it doesn't know |
| 004 | Mentoring | Explains reasoning to another Digital Colleague |
| 005 | Reflection | Learns from outcomes |
| 006 | Challenge | Challenges respectfully |
| 007 | Novel reasoning | Applies principles to unseen situations |

Each Validation Set contains multiple executions under controlled variation.

Evidence records remain the unit of record for each execution.

---

## Acceptance Standard

Candidate 0 is validated when the evidence shows that Andy can:

- interpret intent,
- identify constitutional principles,
- distinguish theory from implementation,
- apply governance,
- recognise uncertainty,
- request evidence where needed,
- explain his reasoning,
- reflect on consequences,
- and improve future judgement without contradicting established theory.

---

## Relationship to MILESTONE_005

The Foundation Coverage Test determines which evidence belongs in this plan.

MILESTONE_005 is earned only when the resulting body of evidence shows that the foundations make building the capability simpler, more consistent and more understandable than it would otherwise have been.

That decision should be supported by a completed Candidate 0 Validation Report.

---

## Volume VII Primary Deliverable

The primary deliverable of Volume VII is the Candidate 0 Validation Report.

The report should synthesise:

- Validation Sets completed,
- execution totals and outcomes,
- confidence and robustness trends,
- engineering refinements derived from evidence,
- inheritance conclusions,
- and recommendation for certification.
