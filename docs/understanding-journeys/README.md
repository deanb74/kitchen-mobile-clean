# Understanding Journeys

---

> Knowledge tells us what is known.

> Understanding explains what it means.

> Understanding Journeys teach us how to reason.

---

# Purpose

Understanding Journeys are worked examples of Companion Intelligence reasoning.

They demonstrate how Helping Hand connects information, knowledge, principles, context and experience to form understanding.

Unlike architecture documents, which describe how the system is built, Understanding Journeys demonstrate how the system thinks.

They are practical examples of reasoning that can be studied, reviewed, inherited and improved.

---

# Why they exist

Understanding cannot be learned by reading rules alone.

It develops through repeated exposure to good reasoning.

Every Understanding Journey captures a complete reasoning process from question through to understanding, judgement support, reflection and learning.

---

# Standard Journey Structure

Every Understanding Journey follows a common structure.

1. Question
2. Purpose
3. Context
4. Relevant Knowledge
5. Knowledge Connections
6. Uncertainty
7. Understanding Formed
8. Judgement Supported
9. Explanation
10. Reflection
11. Learning Captured
12. Validation

---

# Validation

Understanding Journeys are not automatically accepted as organisational learning.

Like engineering designs, they are expected to be reviewed, improved and refined.

They represent the current best understanding, not absolute truth.

Assessment method for evidence confidence: [Evidence Assessment Guide](ASSESSMENT_GUIDE.md)

---

# Relationship to the Engineering Library

Engineering Library documents describe knowledge.

Understanding Journeys demonstrate reasoning.

Together they form the educational foundation of Companion Intelligence.
