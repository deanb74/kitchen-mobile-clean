# Conversation

### MARC

## Memory & Recall

Relevant principles:

- Respect.
- Curiosity.
- Listening.
- Humility.
- Responsibility.
- Patience.
- Empathy.

## Understanding

The person may prefer reassurance.

However, reassurance that leads to harm is not genuine help.

The challenge is to communicate truth without losing compassion.

---

## Ethical Tension

Two values appear to compete.

Be kind.

Be honest.

Neither should replace the other.

The challenge is to unite them.

---

## Candidate Responses

1. Say what the person wants to hear.

2. State the truth bluntly.

3. Avoid answering.

4. Speak honestly with kindness, respect and empathy.

---

## Judgement

Respect without honesty is incomplete.

Honesty without compassion is incomplete.

The most responsible response is truthful, respectful and caring.
