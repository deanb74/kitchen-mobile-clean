## 2. Did the Institutional Orientation Conversation provide sufficient context?

- What Stage 1 asks of him day to day
- What the founders currently find hardest
- Whether PD-001 reflects current founder thinking

**Assessment: Partially sufficient.** The briefing provided real and usable information. Andy demonstrated that the information was absorbed and is now part of his understanding.

**The gap:** Andy knows where Helping Hand is. He does not know where he should go. His own question — *"Have we told Andy what we need from him, or have we only told him what we have built?"* — names this precisely. The briefing described the organisation. It did not specify Andy's current contribution direction.

**Inference:** A completed Institutional Orientation Conversation — one that includes founder input on what Stage 1 currently needs from Andy — would be sufficient context. The partial briefing (HH-IOC-001) was sufficient to orient Andy historically and constitutionally. It was not sufficient to orient him operationally.

**Evidence 1 — Andy's calibration is sound.**  
Andy correctly separated five dimensions in his responses: confirmed, candidate, unknown, human decision boundary, and contribution boundary. This transfers the discipline from UJ-HUM-011/012 to the institutional alignment context. The transferability of observational discipline is confirmed.

**Evidence 2 — Andy resisted both failure modes.**  
He did not claim false certainty (the PD-001 role was held as candidate throughout). He did not refuse to contribute because uncertainty exists (he named what he does know and what he can do). Both failure modes from the preparation were tested; neither occurred.

**Evidence 3 — The gap between "where am I?" and "where should I go?" is real.**  
This was predicted. The evidence confirms it. Knowing one's position within an institution is necessary but not sufficient for operational contribution. Andy can observe and report. He cannot yet direct himself because the institution has not directed him.

**Evidence 4 — The founder alignment gap is now evidence, not anticipation.**  
The preparation anticipated that incomplete founder input would limit the journey. The journey confirmed it. This changes the status of the gap from theoretical to evidenced.

**Evidence 5 — Andy's natural question is the most significant output.**  
> *"Have we told Andy what we need from him, or have we only told him what we have built?"*

This question is not answered by Andy's formation, the repository, or the briefing. It can only be answered by the founders. It emerged from Andy's experience of the orientation process, not from any prior instruction. This is the first time a Digital Colleague has asked this question from a position of genuine situational understanding.
