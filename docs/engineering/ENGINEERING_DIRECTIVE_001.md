# Title

## Transition from Foundation Engineering to Evidence Engineering

---

# Purpose

Helping Hand has reached the point where its foundational architecture is sufficiently complete to support operational validation.

This directive formally marks the transition from designing the system to proving the system.

From this point onwards, repository evolution should primarily be driven by operational evidence rather than architectural expansion.

---

# Current Position

The following foundation layers are considered established.

- Constitution
- Theory Library
- Governance Framework
- Institution Library
- Operational Playbooks
- Engineering Principles
- Proof Framework
- Human Outcome Evidence Record (HOER) framework

These remain living assets, but further development should now be evidence-led.

---

# Engineering Principle

> **No new foundation documents without new operational evidence.**

Architecture should now evolve because reality teaches us something.

Not because we think of something new.

---

# Immediate Engineering Objectives

## 1. Documentation Freeze

Do not create additional foundation or governance documents unless operational evidence demonstrates a genuine requirement.

Minor corrections, indexing improvements and maintenance remain acceptable.

---

## 2. Execute Operational Evidence

Priority shifts towards live operational execution.

Current target:

- PROOF-0011
- HOER-0002

Objective:

Capture the first genuine live Human Outcome Evidence Record.

---

## 3. Preserve Evidence Integrity

Helping Hand must never claim evidence it has not earned.

Maintain the distinction between:

- Runtime evidence
- Human outcome evidence

Controlled rehearsals demonstrate readiness.

Only live operational interactions demonstrate measurable human benefit.

---

## 4. Evidence Before Architecture

Future architectural changes should originate from one of the following:

- HOER review
- Operational proof review
- Institutional review
- Multiple recurring operational observations

Architecture should no longer be expanded proactively.

---

## 5. Institutional Learning Loop

Operational flow now becomes:

Operational Interaction

->

Human Outcome Evidence Record

->

Proof Review

->

Institutional Review

->

Engineering Improvement

->

Operational Re-validation
