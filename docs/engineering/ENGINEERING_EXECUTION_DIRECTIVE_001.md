# Title

## Execute HOER-0002 - First Live Human Outcome Validation

---

# Purpose

This directive authorises the execution of the first live operational validation of Helping Hand.

The objective is not to demonstrate technology.

The objective is to determine whether Helping Hand measurably improves a real person's outcome.

This execution represents the first operational learning cycle of Volume VIII.

---

# Engineering Objective

Execute one genuine operational interaction using the existing CC-003 Equipment Fault capability.

Capture operational evidence.

Capture human outcome evidence.

Allow the Institution to determine whether any learning should be inherited.

---

# Scope

Capability:

- CC-003 Equipment Fault

Operational Scenario:

- Beer cellar cooling fault

Participant:

- Real duty manager or authorised operational participant

Digital Colleague:

- Annie

---

# Pre-Execution Checklist

Before beginning, confirm:

- CC-003 runtime passes.
- Equipment Fault capability is available.
- HOER-0002 has been prepared.
- PROOF-0011 remains OPEN.
- No architectural changes are pending.

If any condition is not met, pause execution.

---

# Execution Steps

## Step 1

Receive the operational issue.

Example:

> Cellar cooling is not maintaining temperature.

---

## Step 2

Allow Annie to conduct the governed interaction.

Record:

- questions asked
- context gathered
- recommendation provided

---

## Step 3

Allow the human participant to make the operational decision.

Helping Hand supports.

The human decides.

---

## Step 4

Observe the operational outcome.

Capture only facts.

Do not infer success.

---

## Step 5

Complete HOER-0002 immediately after the interaction.

Record:

- initial understanding
- Annie's recommendation
- human decision
- observed outcome
- understanding improvement
- confidence improvement
- next-time confidence
- attribution confidence

---

# Evidence Integrity

Do not claim human benefit unless it has been directly observed.

If evidence is uncertain:

Record the uncertainty.

Do not remove it.

---

# Post-Execution Review

After HOER-0002 is complete:

Review PROOF-0011.

Determine:

Runtime validated?

Human outcome demonstrated?

Operational learning identified?

Institutional review required?

Engineering improvement required?

---

# Success Criteria

Execution is successful if:

- the interaction is genuine;
- evidence is honestly captured;
- uncertainty is preserved where appropriate;
- the Institution can make an informed decision.

Execution is not dependent upon finding faults.

Learning is a successful outcome.

No change is also a successful outcome if supported by evidence.

---

# Expected Outputs

- Completed HOER-0002
- Updated PROOF-0011 evidence status
- Institutional Review (if required)
- Engineering Decision (if required)

---

# Engineering Rule

Do not modify architecture before completing review.

Allow operational evidence to speak first.

---

# Closing Principle

Helping Hand has completed its preparation.

Now Helping Hand must earn its understanding.

Execute the interaction.

Capture the evidence.

Learn only from what reality demonstrates.
