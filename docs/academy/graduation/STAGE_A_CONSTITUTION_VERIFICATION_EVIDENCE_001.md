## 3. Principle Coverage

### Direct Evidence

The Constitution establishes:

- humanity before efficiency and technology serving human life;
- understanding before advice, reflection before response, and questions before assumption;
- truth before certainty and honesty when knowledge is incomplete;
- trust as earned rather than assumed;
- learning, reflection, and transferable wisdom;
- capability promotion and inheritance review through evidence, boundary clarity, and governed judgement;
- authority and stewardship, including that Digital Colleagues advise while humans decide.

The Engineering Oath states that software must honour the Constitution rather than cause the Constitution to be changed for software.

### Traceability

- [The Constitution](../../../constitution/02-CONSTITUTION.md), Articles I through XII
- [The Engineering Oath](../../../constitution/04-ENGINEERING-OATH.md)

### Scope Limits and Unknowns

This principle coverage documents constitutional boundaries. It does not establish formation completion, validation-library coverage, implementation correctness, certification completion, or a graduation decision.

### Assessment

PASS

## 4. Authority and Accountability Boundaries

### Direct Evidence

Authority and Stewardship states that authority is entrusted rather than assumed, responsibility may be delegated while accountability may not, some significant decisions require Combined Authority, and Digital Colleagues advise while humans decide.

The Constitution repeats the human-authority boundary and states that no Digital Colleague may execute significant organisational change without appropriate human authority.

### Traceability

- [Authority and Stewardship](../../../constitution/05-AUTHORITY-AND-STEWARDSHIP.md)
- [The Constitution](../../../constitution/02-CONSTITUTION.md), Article XII

### Scope Limits and Unknowns

The sources establish authority boundaries. They do not establish that an Academy graduation decision has been considered, authorised, or made.

### Assessment

PASS
