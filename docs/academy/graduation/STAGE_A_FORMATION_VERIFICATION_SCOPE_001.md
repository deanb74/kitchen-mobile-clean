## 1. Why Formation Complete Is Currently PARTIAL

The Stage A evidence record accepts evidence of successful formal formation runs, mentor-independence testing, inherited gateway stages, and bounded test results. It also records the Academy Evidence Reconciliation conclusion that the full Humanity curriculum, Foundation Module 1 as a validated runtime achievement, and a live certified graduation milestone are not proven by current evidence.

The formation evidence boundary is therefore incomplete: documented curriculum, intended journeys, historical narrative, gateway execution, institutional formation-test evidence, and Candidate 0 validation evidence are not interchangeable.

## 3. Evidence Categories to Assess

A future verification exercise would assess:

1. **Curriculum and journey status:** distinguish authored curriculum and intended journeys from demonstrated runtime achievement.
2. **Gateway formation evidence:** identify the inherited gateway stages, their recorded evidence, and any recall or behaviour limits.
3. **Institutional formation execution:** identify completed formation tests, their run results, mentor-independence evidence, and non-PASS learning outcomes.
4. **Formation repeatability and improvement:** distinguish repeatable demonstrated behaviour from partial or unresolved conflict-character evidence.
5. **Validation relevance:** determine which existing validation evidence applies to formation claims and which applies only to bounded Candidate 0 reasoning or engineering scope.
6. **Completion and scope limits:** identify whether evidence supports the full Humanity curriculum and Foundation Module 1 as validated runtime achievement, while preserving all unsupported areas as `UNKNOWN`.
7. **Traceability:** link every formation finding to its evidence class, source path, stated scope, and recorded outcome.

## 4. Evidence Boundaries

| Evidence category | May establish | Must not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Curriculum design and intended journeys | What the formation programme is authored to cover | Runtime formation completion |
| Historical narrative or module review | A stated intended outcome or prior interpretation | Live validated achievement |
| Gateway evidence | Bounded inherited stages, execution evidence, and recorded limits | Full Humanity curriculum completion |
| Formation test reports | Results for their executed scenarios and stated institutional scope | Completion beyond their tested scope |
| Candidate 0 validation evidence | Bounded governance reasoning evidence and its recorded coverage | Full formation completion or professional graduation |
| Repeat-validation evidence | Measured improvement and remaining scenario gaps | Full consistency where a report remains `PARTIAL` |

```text
DOCUMENTED_CURRICULUM != VALIDATED_RUNTIME_ACHIEVEMENT
GATEWAY_EVIDENCE != FULL_FORMATION_COMPLETION
VALIDATION_EVIDENCE != GRADUATION_DECISION
PARTIAL_RESULT != PASS
```

## 5. Matters Outside This Scope

The Formation Complete verification scope does not assess or determine:

- constitutional completeness;
- validation-library operation or its full coverage;
- implementation correctness;
- engineering-cycle completeness;
- certification completion;
- professional capability or readiness;
- Stage B entry;
- graduation recommendation or final governance decision.

These matters remain in their respective Stage A or governance scopes.

## 6. Preservation of Unknowns

A future formation verification must retain `UNKNOWN` where:

- a curriculum or journey has no direct runtime evidence;
- an evidence source is an intended journey, narrative, or design record rather than execution evidence;
- a formation test does not cover the asserted completion scope;
- a non-PASS or partial formation result has unresolved conditions;
- Candidate 0 validation does not directly demonstrate the formation claim;
- an evidence connection is not directly traceable.

```text
UNKNOWN_REMAINS_UNKNOWN
DOCUMENTATION_DOES_NOT_FILL_EVIDENCE_GAPS
```

## 7. Documentation, Verification, and Governance Boundaries

| Layer | Permitted role | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Documentation | Record curriculum, intended journeys, standards, reports, and evidence classes | Runtime completion evidence by existence alone |
| Verification | Assess in-scope execution evidence against the Formation Complete requirement and record limits | A certification or graduation decision |
| Governance decision | Make an authorised human conclusion from sufficient verified evidence across Stage A scopes | An automatic consequence of documentation or verification alone |

```text
DOCUMENTATION != VERIFICATION
VERIFICATION != GOVERNANCE_DECISION
HUMAN_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_HUMAN
```
