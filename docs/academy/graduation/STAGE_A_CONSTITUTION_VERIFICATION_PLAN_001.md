# Stage A Constitution Verification Plan 001

**Volume:** VIII - Graduation
**Document ID:** STAGE_A_CONSTITUTION_VERIFICATION_PLAN_001
**Status:** Verification Plan Only
**Source records:** [Stage A Constitution Verification Scope 001](STAGE_A_CONSTITUTION_VERIFICATION_SCOPE_001.md), [Stage A Constitution Verification Criteria 001](STAGE_A_CONSTITUTION_VERIFICATION_CRITERIA_001.md), [Stage A Academy Readiness Evidence 001](STAGE_A_ACADEMY_READINESS_EVIDENCE_001.md), and [Stage A Readiness Gap Analysis 001](STAGE_A_READINESS_GAP_ANALYSIS_001.md)
**Purpose:** Define the future verification exercise for the Stage A gap, `Constitution Complete`.
**Decision authority:** None. This plan does not execute verification, assess evidence, create a PASS outcome, modify constitutional documents, or alter the Stage A `HOLD` outcome.

## 1. Verification Objective

The future verification exercise will assess whether the defined constitutional source set directly supports the Stage A professional-graduation scope for the Helping Hand Academy, with applicable boundaries, dependencies, limits, and unknowns made visible.

The exercise does not determine whether the Academy is ready to graduate a professional Digital Colleague. It assesses only the constitutional basis required for later Stage A formation and validation evidence to be interpreted within a visible scope.

## 2. Evidence Sources in Scope

The future exercise will collect evidence only from the constitutional source set identified by the scope record:

- [The Constitution](../../../constitution/02-CONSTITUTION.md)
- [Authority and Stewardship](../../../constitution/05-AUTHORITY-AND-STEWARDSHIP.md)
- [The Engineering Oath](../../../constitution/04-ENGINEERING-OATH.md)
- [Constitutional Document Register](../../../constitution/DOCUMENT_REGISTER.md)

The Stage A scope and criteria records are planning inputs, not constitutional evidence. They define what the exercise must assess and how its limits must be recorded.

## 3. Assessment Questions

The exercise will assess the following questions derived from the criteria record.

| Evidence category | Assessment question |
|---|---|
| Source identity and recorded status | Are the constitutional sources in scope identifiable with their recorded document and register status? |
| Principle coverage | Do the sources visibly address human purpose, understanding, truth, trust, learning, authority, stewardship, capability promotion, and inheritance boundaries relevant to Stage A? |
| Authority and accountability boundaries | Do the sources preserve human decision ownership, accountability, combined authority where relevant, and the advisory boundary for Digital Colleagues? |
| Graduation-scope relevance | Do the sources provide a visible constitutional basis for the Academy's professional-graduation question? |
| Traceability and scope limits | Can each assessment statement be traced to a source while recording what constitutional sources do not establish? |
| Unknowns and dependencies | Are unsupported interpretation, evidence, and governance dependencies retained as `UNKNOWN` rather than inferred? |

## 4. Evidence Collection Method

The future exercise will:

1. identify the in-scope constitutional source and its recorded status;
2. capture the direct source statements relevant to each assessment question;
3. record the source path and the specific constitutional principle or boundary relied upon;
4. distinguish direct source statements from interpretation about Stage A relevance;
5. record scope limits and unresolved dependencies alongside each finding;
6. exclude evidence that belongs to formation, validation, implementation, certification, or governance decision scopes;
7. preserve `UNKNOWN` where a conclusion is not directly supported by the constitutional evidence.

The exercise will not alter, annotate, rewrite, or otherwise modify any constitutional source.

## 5. Outcome Classification Approach

The future evidence record may classify each assessment question as follows:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PASS | Direct constitutional evidence supports the specific assessment question for the defined Stage A scope, with traceability and applicable limits recorded. |
| PARTIAL | Some direct constitutional evidence supports the question, while required scope relevance, boundary coverage, traceability, or limits remain unverified. |
| UNKNOWN | The constitutional sources do not directly establish the required conclusion, the source is unavailable or unclear, or a dependency belongs outside constitutional scope. |

A classification applies only to the assessment question it addresses. No individual classification is a Stage A outcome, certification result, or graduation decision.

## 6. Required Evidence Record Structure

A future verification evidence record will contain:

1. verification metadata and stated scope;
2. each in-scope constitutional source with its recorded status;
3. one section for each assessment question;
4. direct evidence available for that question;
5. source location and traceability;
6. documented interpretation limited to the Stage A constitutional scope;
7. recorded scope limits, dependencies, and unknowns;
8. the question-level classification of `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, or `UNKNOWN`;
9. a summary table of classifications and evidence confidence;
10. an explicit statement that the record does not assess formation completion, validation coverage, implementation correctness, certification, or graduation decision.

## 7. Explicit Exclusions

The future verification exercise must not assess or determine:

- formation completion;
- validation coverage or validation-library operation;
- implementation correctness;
- certification completion;
- graduation recommendation, Stage B entry, or a graduation decision.

These exclusions preserve the Stage A dependency sequence:

```text
CONSTITUTIONAL_SCOPE
        ↓
FORMATION_EVIDENCE_SCOPE
        ↓
VALIDATION_COVERAGE_AND_REPEATABILITY
```
