# Stage A Formation Verification Plan 001

**Volume:** VIII - Graduation
**Document ID:** STAGE_A_FORMATION_VERIFICATION_PLAN_001
**Status:** Verification Plan Only
**Source records:** [Stage A Formation Verification Scope 001](STAGE_A_FORMATION_VERIFICATION_SCOPE_001.md), [Stage A Formation Verification Criteria 001](STAGE_A_FORMATION_VERIFICATION_CRITERIA_001.md), [Stage A Academy Readiness Evidence 001](STAGE_A_ACADEMY_READINESS_EVIDENCE_001.md), and existing Andy formation evidence references.
**Purpose:** Define the future verification exercise for the Stage A gap, `Formation Complete`.
**Decision authority:** None. This plan does not perform verification, assess Andy, create outcomes, or alter the Stage A `HOLD` outcome.

## 1. Verification Objective

The future verification exercise will assess whether the in-scope formation evidence distinguishes the documented curriculum, intended journeys, executed formation scenarios, validated runtime achievements, repeatability evidence, and unresolved formation gaps required for the Stage A `Formation Complete` requirement.

The exercise does not determine professional readiness, certification, Stage B entry, or a graduation decision.

## 2. Evidence Collection Method

The future exercise will collect only the source classes defined by the formation verification scope:

1. the Stage A formation assessment and its stated evidence limits;
2. Andy formation gateway evidence and the formation records identified by Academy Evidence Reconciliation;
3. Academy formation reports and their linked execution artefacts;
4. existing Candidate 0 validation evidence, limited to its stated reasoning and validation scope;
5. Academy Evidence Reconciliation as the controlling evidence-class distinction and current-truth boundary.

For every collected item, the exercise will record:

- source path;
- evidence class;
- stated subject, scenario, or journey;
- recorded result or status;
- demonstrated scope;
- limits, unresolved conditions, and dependencies;
- whether the source is curriculum, intended journey, execution evidence, validated runtime evidence, repeatability evidence, or another bounded evidence type.

No source will be treated as runtime completion merely because it exists, is authored, or has a narrative completion statement.

## 3. Evidence Classification Method

The future exercise will classify evidence by evidence class before considering its relevance to Formation Complete:

| Evidence class | Classification use |
|---|---|
| Documented curriculum | Identifies authored formation scope and intended learning objectives only |
| Intended journey | Identifies authored journey progression and expected outcome only |
| Execution evidence | Identifies what a recorded formation scenario actually exercised and its stated result |
| Validated runtime achievement | Identifies direct runtime demonstration within the verification scope stated by the source |
| Repeatability evidence | Identifies behaviour demonstrated across the recorded repeated or comparative conditions |
| Unknown or unsupported relationship | Identifies a missing, unresolved, or non-traceable connection without inference |

The future exercise will retain the criteria distinctions:

```text
DOCUMENTED_CURRICULUM != VALIDATED_RUNTIME_ACHIEVEMENT
INTENDED_JOURNEY != COMPLETED_JOURNEY
EXECUTED_SCENARIO != FULL_FORMATION_COMPLETION
BOUNDED_REPEATABILITY != WHOLE_CURRICULUM_REPEATABILITY
```

## 4. Assessment Sequence

1. **Identify authored scope:** catalogue the documented curriculum and intended journeys without treating them as achieved outcomes.
2. **Collect execution evidence:** identify formation gateway and Academy formation-test evidence, recording the exact scenario and tested scope for each item.
3. **Identify validated runtime achievement:** distinguish direct runtime evidence from narrative, intended, or module-review material.
4. **Review repeatability evidence:** identify repeated runs, mentor-path comparisons, and any partial or unresolved formation signals within their stated scope.
5. **Determine validation relevance:** separate evidence that bears directly on formation claims from Candidate 0 reasoning or engineering evidence that remains bounded to another scope.
6. **Record completion limits:** map evidence coverage against the full Humanity curriculum and Foundation Module 1 achievement claims without inferring untested coverage.
7. **Preserve unknowns:** record every missing journey, untested curriculum area, unresolved partial result, or non-traceable relationship as `UNKNOWN`.

## 5. Required Evidence Record Structure

A future Formation Complete verification evidence record will contain:

1. verification metadata and stated formation scope;
2. the in-scope source inventory, grouped by evidence class;
3. a documented curriculum and intended-journey inventory;
4. an execution-evidence inventory with scenario, result, source, and stated limits;
5. a validated-runtime-achievement inventory with direct evidence and verified scope;
6. a repeatability inventory with comparative conditions, demonstrated signals, and remaining limits;
7. a validation-relevance section distinguishing formation evidence from bounded Candidate 0 reasoning evidence;
8. an unknowns and dependencies section;
9. traceability from each conclusion to its source;
10. an explicit boundary statement that the record does not assess constitutional completeness, validation-library operation, implementation correctness, certification, or graduation decision.

## 6. Explicit Exclusions

The future Formation Complete verification must not assess or determine:

- constitutional completeness;
- validation-library operation or full validation coverage;
- implementation correctness;
- engineering-cycle completeness;
- certification completion;
- professional capability or readiness;
- Stage B entry;
- graduation recommendation or final governance decision.

## 7. Boundary Preservation

```text
DOCUMENTATION != VERIFICATION
VERIFICATION != GOVERNANCE_DECISION
UNKNOWN_REMAINS_UNKNOWN
HUMAN_AUTHORITY_REMAINS_HUMAN
```
