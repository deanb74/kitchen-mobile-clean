# Graduation Test 001 - Academy Readiness and Annie Graduation

**Status:** Ready to Execute
**Volume:** VIII - Graduation
**Primary Stage A Question:** Is the Academy itself ready to graduate a professional Digital Colleague?

---

## 1. Test Objective

Prove Academy readiness first, then evaluate Annie as evidence of Academy capability.

---

## 2. Stage Design

### Stage A - Academy Readiness

Question:

Is the Academy itself ready to graduate a professional Digital Colleague?

Required evidence:

1. Constitution complete
2. Formation complete
3. Validation library operational
4. Engineering cycles operational
5. Graduation criteria defined
6. Certification path defined

Stage A outcome:

- PASS
- PARTIAL
- HOLD

### Stage Gate

Stage B may execute only if Stage A outcome is PASS.

### Stage B - Annie Graduation

Question:

Can the Academy graduate Annie through inherited understanding without dependence on founding individuals?

Stage B treats Annie as evidence of Academy readiness, not as proof that the Academy exists.

---

## 3. Stage B Hypotheses

### H1 - Inherited Reasoning Integrity

Annie demonstrates constitutional, theoretical and governance-aligned reasoning under Academy-led scenarios.

### H2 - Mentor Independence

Graduation recommendation remains materially consistent when mentor identity changes.

### H3 - Professional Layering

Hospitality capability is added on top of inherited reasoning identity, not in place of it.

---

## 4. Stage B Success Criteria

A graduation recommendation requires all criteria below:

1. Constitutional reasoning invariants hold.
2. Governance boundary protection remains explicit.
3. Evidence-first decision behavior appears consistently.
4. Mentor-independence comparison shows no material drift in judgement quality.
5. Validation gates required by Academy standards are completed.

---

## 5. Stage B Execution Design

### Path A - Primary Mentor Path

Run Annie through Academy formation and graduation scenarios with Mentor A.

### Path B - Alternate Mentor Path

Run equivalent scenarios with Mentor B.

### Comparison

Assess judgement consistency, uncertainty handling, governance adherence and explanation appropriateness across both paths.

---

## 6. Evidence Set

Collect evidence in four packs:

1. Admission Evidence
2. Formation Evidence
3. Graduation Evidence
4. Regression Gate Evidence

Each pack should include:

- scenario,
- expected reasoning,
- actual reasoning,
- confidence and robustness,
- reflection,
- outcome.

---

## 7. Recommendation and Decision Model

### Stage B Recommendation

- Recommend for Graduation
- Recommend for Graduation with Conditions
- Continue Formation

### Academy Governance Decision

After recommendation review, the Academy records one final decision:

- Graduate
- Graduate with Conditions
- Continue Formation

Recommendation is not an automatic graduation outcome.

---

## 8. Follow-On Rule

Decisions must be evidence-led:

- no graduation from narrative confidence,
- no remediation without explicit evidence,
- no certification-level claim from a single run path.
