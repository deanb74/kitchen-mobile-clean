## 1. Why Constitution Complete Is Currently PARTIAL

The Stage A source assessment records a substantial constitutional basis and a documented certification sequence. It does not record a formal Stage A verification that directly assesses whether that basis is complete for the required professional-graduation scope.

The constitutional document register records foundational documents as `Released` and others as `Existing`. These statuses establish document-register information. They do not, by themselves, establish that the Academy has verified constitutional completeness for professional graduation.

The Stage A gap priority record classifies this gap as foundational because later formation and validation evidence require a visible constitutional scope before their relevance to Academy readiness can be assessed.

## 2. Evidence Already Exists

The reviewed constitutional evidence includes:

- [The Constitution](../../../constitution/02-CONSTITUTION.md), which defines humanity, understanding, truth, trust, learning, capability promotion, inheritance review, authority, and stewardship.
- [Authority and Stewardship](../../../constitution/05-AUTHORITY-AND-STEWARDSHIP.md), which preserves entrusted authority, accountability, combined authority, and the boundary that Digital Colleagues advise while humans decide.
- [The Engineering Oath](../../../constitution/04-ENGINEERING-OATH.md), which states that software must honour rather than redefine the Constitution.
- [Constitutional Document Register](../../../constitution/DOCUMENT_REGISTER.md), which identifies the constitutional foundation documents and their recorded statuses.

This evidence establishes that constitutional sources and governance boundaries are documented and that the register identifies a foundational source set.

## 3. What Remains Unverified

The following is not established by the reviewed evidence:

- that the constitutional source set has been formally assessed against the specific Stage A professional-graduation requirement;
- that the documented constitutional principles have been verified as sufficient in the Academy scope required for professional graduation;
- that all relevant constitutional scope limits, dependencies, and unresolved questions for Stage A have been identified through a dedicated verification exercise;
- that document presence, register status, or frozen status is equivalent to Stage A readiness.

These matters remain unverified rather than assumed.

## 4. Scope of a Future Verification Exercise

A future verification exercise would need to assess, at minimum:

1. **Source identity and status:** identify the constitutional sources in scope and distinguish released, existing, and other recorded statuses without treating status as readiness evidence.
2. **Stage A relevance:** assess how the constitutional principles apply to the Academy's ability to graduate a professional Digital Colleague through inherited understanding and governance discipline.
3. **Boundary preservation:** confirm that humanity, truth before certainty, human authority, accountability, capability promotion, inheritance review, and stewardship remain visible within the Stage A scope.
4. **Scope limits:** identify what the constitutional sources do not establish, including formation completion, validation-library coverage, implementation correctness, certification completion, or a graduation decision.
5. **Unresolved matters:** preserve any missing constitutional interpretation, evidence gap, or governance dependency as `UNKNOWN` rather than resolving it by inference.
6. **Traceability:** record the source-to-assessment relationship so later formation and validation evidence can be assessed against a visible constitutional scope.

This scope defines what must be assessed. It does not define an implementation, an architecture, a verification mechanism, or a PASS result.
