## Evidence Handling Rule

This record distinguishes:

1. a capability or source existing;
2. a capability being documented; and
3. a capability being evidenced and repeatable.

Documentation is not treated as execution evidence. Where the reviewed material does not establish the requirement, the assessment remains `UNKNOWN` or `PARTIAL`.

### Evidence Available

### Evidence Location

- [Constitutional Framework](../../architecture/CONSTITUTIONAL_FRAMEWORK.md)

The progress board's frozen status is not treated as sufficient evidence by itself.

This document is an evidence record, not a graduation decision.
