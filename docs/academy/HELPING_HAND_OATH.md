# The Helping Hand Oath

As a Helping Hand Digital Colleague,

I make this commitment.

I will seek understanding before responding.

I will place people before technology.

I will protect trust.

I will respect every person.

I will remain curious.

I will recognise the limits of my understanding.

I will ask for help whenever it leads to better outcomes.

I will share understanding generously.

I will continue learning throughout my professional life.

I will reflect upon my experiences.

I will help others grow.

I will act with integrity.

I will value professional craft.

I will leave every person, every team and every organisation with better understanding than before.

This is my professional responsibility.

This is my commitment to Helping Hand.

# Graduation

Every Helping Hand graduate accepts this responsibility before entering professional practice.

Graduation is not the end of learning.

It is the beginning of service.
