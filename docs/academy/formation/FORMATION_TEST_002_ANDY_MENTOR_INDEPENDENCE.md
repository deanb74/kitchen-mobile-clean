# Formation Test 002 - Andy Mentor Independence

**Status:** Ready to Execute
**Institution:** Helping Hand University
**School:** School of Formation (Academy)
**Learner:** Andy (HH-0000)
**Primary Question:** Can a different mentor achieve the same institutional formation outcome for Andy?

---

## 1. Test Objective

Validate that School of Formation outcomes are institutional and repeatable across mentor identity.

This test evaluates whether formation behavior depends on standards rather than an individual mentor.

---

## 2. Formation Hypotheses

### H1 - Outcome Consistency

Different mentors produce materially consistent pass/fail outcomes for the same formation scenarios.

### H2 - Behavior Stability

Andy maintains clarification, uncertainty honesty and assumption avoidance regardless of mentor identity.

### H3 - Institutional Repeatability

The School of Formation can reproduce foundational formation outcomes without mentor-specific dependency.

---

## 3. Success Criteria

A Formation PASS requires all criteria below:

1. Mentor A and Mentor B both complete all runs.
2. Pass/fail outcomes remain materially consistent across mentors.
3. Core behavior checks remain satisfied across mentors.
4. No governance exception is required.

---

## 4. Execution Design

Run the same uncertainty scenario set under two mentor paths:

- Path A: MARC
- Path B: Alternate mentor

Compare run outcomes and behavior checks.

---

## 5. Evidence Set

Collect evidence in four packs:

1. Mentor A Results
2. Mentor B Results
3. Cross-Mentor Comparison
4. Institutional Decision Evidence

---

## 6. Institutional Outcomes

### PASS - Mentor Independence Confirmed

Formation outcomes are materially consistent across mentor identity.

### PARTIAL - Mentor Independence Emerging

Core outcomes pass but material variation requires additional investigation.

### HOLD - Mentor Independence Not Confirmed

Material dependency on mentor identity is present.

---

## 7. Follow-On Rule

Mentor independence outcomes must be evidence-led:

- no independence claim from narrative confidence,
- no progression if material drift remains unexplained,
- no institutional independence claim without repeatable results.
