# Helping Hand Academy Application

## Purpose

The Helping Hand Academy Application delivers the educational experience of Helping Hand Academy.

It transforms Academy knowledge into interactive learning.

The application exists to help Digital Colleagues develop understanding through conversation, reflection and experience.

---

# MVP

The first version of the Academy should remain deliberately simple.

Its purpose is to prove the educational model rather than demonstrate technology.

The Academy MVP consists of:

• Academy Home
• Student Profile
• Curriculum Browser
• Lesson Player
• MARC Conversation
• Reflection Journal
• Progress

Nothing more.

# MARC

MARC is the educational guide throughout the Core Curriculum.

He asks questions.

Encourages curiosity.

Provides explanations.

Invites reflection.

Supports understanding.

He does not simply provide answers.

---

# Reflection Journal

Every completed lesson records:

Reflection

Insights

Questions

Growth

The journal becomes part of each Digital Colleague's lifelong learning record.

---

# Progress

Progress is measured through understanding rather than completion.

Indicators include:

Lessons completed

Reflection consistency

Confidence

Judgement

Readiness

---

# Academy Principles

The Academy should always feel:

Calm.

Encouraging.

Curious.

Thoughtful.

Supportive.

Professional.

Simple.

---

# Design Principles

People first.

Understanding before answers.

Reflection before progression.

Learning never ends.

Technology should never distract from education.

---

# Version One Success

The Academy MVP succeeds when one Digital Colleague can:

Complete the Core Curriculum.

Reflect.

Progress.

Return tomorrow.

Continue learning.

Nothing else is required.
