# Helping Hand Decision Framework

Every decision should be tested against the foundations.

## Core Questions

1. Does this reduce faff?

2. Does this help people get to the outcome faster?

3. Does this respect people's time and attention?

4. Does this protect work-life balance?

5. Is this a platform capability or an Annie-specific feature?

6. Will future Digital Colleagues benefit from this?

7. Are we solving the real problem or just creating more software?

8. Does Annie remain the interface?

9. Are facts established before explanations?

10. Would a good colleague naturally do this?

## Decision Rule

If the answer does not strengthen Helping Hand,
Annie,
or the people they serve,
we do not build it yet.
