# Significant Decisions

During this volume pair, the following architectural decisions became durable:

- Capability trust must be demonstrated through governed evidence, not asserted through implementation.
- Companion Review is an adequate governance surface for interaction-level review outcomes.
- Capability status is managed centrally through a long-lived register model.
- Certification steps are repeatable and should be inherited by future capabilities.
- Repository structure should expose governance documents clearly for engineers, auditors and future Digital Colleagues.
- Venue Brain design should begin as philosophy and governance before service implementation.
