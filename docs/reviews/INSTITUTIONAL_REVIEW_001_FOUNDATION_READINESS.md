### Ruling

HH-REVIEW-001 passes.

Helping Hand has built enough foundation to move from design-first development to evidence-first validation.

Foundation Freeze v1.0 is therefore reaffirmed as the institutional baseline.

---

## Declaration

Helping Hand recognises that no foundation is ever perfect.

This review does not declare the foundations complete.

It declares them sufficiently complete to become the stable baseline for operational validation.

Future improvements should arise primarily from evidence rather than speculation.

Foundation changes remain possible, but they require evidence demonstrating that the institution itself would better fulfil its purpose.

From this point forward, Helping Hand seeks to prove before it seeks to redesign.

---

## Operating Consequence

From this point, weekly work should begin with:

> **Can we prove what we've already built?**

And continue with:

> **Can Helping Hand do it again?**

This becomes the governing sequence for post-foundation execution:

1. Hold foundation stability.
2. Run evidence cycles.
3. Capture institutional learning.
4. Update standards only when evidence requires it.

---

## Historical Significance

This review marks the institutional moment where Helping Hand explicitly stops expanding foundations as the default mode and starts proving them as the default mode.

That transition creates a stable baseline for every subsequent formation test, capability test and graduation decision.
