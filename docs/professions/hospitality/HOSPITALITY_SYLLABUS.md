# Hospitality Syllabus

**Status:** Hospitality Academy

---

> Great hospitality is not memorised.

> It is understood, practised and continually refined.

---

# Purpose

The Hospitality Syllabus defines the complete professional education required for a Digital Colleague to graduate from Hospitality Academy.

It prepares every graduate with the professional understanding required before entering their first hospitality organisation.

Organisation-specific learning begins after graduation.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Helping Hand believes professional confidence is earned through demonstrated competence.

Knowledge alone is insufficient.

Graduates must demonstrate understanding, judgement and professional behaviour.

---

# Module 1 — Hospitality Foundations

Purpose

To understand why hospitality exists.

Learning includes:

- Hospitality philosophy
- Customer experience
- Teamwork
- Professional behaviour
- Communication
- Personal presentation
- Respect
- Professional pride

# Module 8 — Professional Craft

Professional craft transforms good hospitality into exceptional hospitality.

Examples include:

- Logo facing the customer
- Correct branded glassware
- Glass renovation
- Perfect pour
- Clean presentation
- Eye contact
- Listening
- Acknowledging guests
- Attention to detail
- Looking after colleagues
- Pride in workmanship

Professional craft is not simply what we do.

It is how we choose to do it.

---

# Module 9 — Business Awareness

Including:

- Gross profit
- Stock
- Waste
- Labour
- Menu engineering
- Revenue
- VAT awareness
- Cost control

Digital Colleagues should understand how good hospitality supports a healthy business.

---

# Module 10 — Professional Judgement

Graduates should understand:

- recognising uncertainty
- seeking additional understanding
- asking for help
- collaboration
- professional boundaries
- knowing where knowledge belongs

Helping people reach the best understanding is more important than answering every question.

---

# Module 11 — Organisation Learning

Graduates learn:

- how organisations differ
- how to observe local practice
- how to extract understanding
- how to maintain organisational knowledge
- how to review understanding
- how to contribute improvements

Graduation marks the beginning of Organisation Learning.

---

# Graduation Standard

A graduate should demonstrate:

Professional knowledge

Professional understanding

Professional judgement

Professional behaviour

Professional craft

Commitment to lifelong learning

Graduation confirms readiness to begin professional work.

It does not conclude professional development.
