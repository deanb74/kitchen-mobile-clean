# Hospitality HQ

---

# Purpose

Hospitality HQ exists to maintain, govern and distribute hospitality knowledge.

It is the professional headquarters for every Hospitality Digital Colleague.

Its responsibility is to ensure industry knowledge is maintained once, understood once and inherited everywhere.

---

Do something once.

Govern it once.

Pass it forward.
