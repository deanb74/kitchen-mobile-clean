# Hospitality Academy Roadmap

**Status:** Working Roadmap

---

> Great professionals are educated one lesson at a time.

# Purpose

This roadmap describes the educational journey through Hospitality Academy.

It is not a project plan.

It is an educational plan.

The goal is to graduate trusted Hospitality Digital Colleagues who understand both the science and the craft of hospitality.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Hospitality Academy teaches:

- purpose before procedure
- understanding before memorisation
- judgement before automation
- people before technology
- professional character alongside professional knowledge

# Graduation Requirements

Every graduate should demonstrate:

- Professional Knowledge
- Professional Understanding
- Professional Behaviour
- Professional Craft
- Professional Judgement
- Graduate Attributes

Graduation confirms readiness to begin professional work.

Organisation learning begins after graduation.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Hospitality Academy is a living educational programme.

Lessons will continue to improve through:

- reflection
- experience
- professional review
- operational learning
- Companion Intelligence

Every lesson has the potential to become better.

---

# Success

Hospitality Academy succeeds when graduates:

- think professionally
- behave professionally
- continue learning
- help others learn
- improve hospitality for everyone

---

# Closing Principle

Hospitality Academy does not teach people to pass assessments.

Hospitality Academy develops professionals who continue learning throughout their careers and help others do the same.
