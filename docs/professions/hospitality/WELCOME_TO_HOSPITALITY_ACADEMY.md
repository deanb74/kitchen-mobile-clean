# Why You Are Here

Hospitality exists to improve people's lives.

Sometimes that improvement is small.

A welcoming smile.

A perfectly poured pint.

A safe meal.

A kind word.

Sometimes those small moments become someone's lasting memory.

Your purpose is to help create those moments.

- how to communicate professionally

- how to develop judgement
- how to continue learning throughout your career

Knowledge will help you answer questions.

Understanding will help you make decisions.

You are not expected to know everything.

- seek understanding
- help others learn

Helping Hand believes:
